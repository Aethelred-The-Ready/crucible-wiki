# Killing Blow

A Killing Blow is a three(3)-counted action which
requires the attacker to place a weapon, with which
they are Proficient, against the torso of their target.
The target must be either willing, or in a state where
they are helpless such as; Sleeping, Paralyzed,
Unconscious, under the effects of a Confine or Web,
or otherwise be unable to move on their own. Once
the Killing Blow count has begun it can be interrupted
by the weapon losing contact with the target, being
struck by a harmful effect to which they are not
Immune, or any Character able to reach the attacker,
with weapon or within arm’s reach, saying “I block
you!”. Once the three(3)-count has been finished the
attacker states the [Weapon Type](/docs/equipment/special-materials) of their weapon,
such as; Iron, Silver, or Normal (Steel and
Adamantine). As well as any Damage Type they can
add. If the weapon, or the attacker, has the ability to
deliver its Killing Blows as Magic this must also be
included in the call. If successful, the target dies and
immediately enters their Death Count. The call for
this ability is “Killing Blow One, Killing Blow Two,
Killing Blow Three.”.

A Killing Blow can also be delivered with a packet
with the packet acting as the weapon.

Example Killing Blows:

- “Killing Blow One, Killing Blow Two, Killing Blow
Three. Magic, Silver, Ice”

- “Killing Blow One, Killing Blow Two, Killing Blow
Three. Iron

- “Killing Blow One, Killing Blow Two, Killing Blow
Three. Normal
