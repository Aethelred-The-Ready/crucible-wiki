# Haste
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list), [Transmutation](../transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 3<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell instantly Refits any [Avoidance Armour](/docs/equipment/non-physical-armour) possessed by the target to its full value.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the power of Haste."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Refit Avoidance Armour](/docs/glossary/refit)"<br/>
