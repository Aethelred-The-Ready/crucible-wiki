# Frailty
<b>School:</b> [Affliction](../affliction_list), [Transmutation](../transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 5 Minutes<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This [Curse](/docs/glossary/curse) Spell causes the target to take double damage from all sources. Additionally, the Character cannot use any form of the Defensive Ability [Resist](/docs/glossary/resist) &lt;Effect&gt; or &lt;Effect&gt; [Resistance](/docs/glossary/resistance).<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Curse you with Frailty."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Frailty](/docs/glossary/curse#frailty)"<br/>
