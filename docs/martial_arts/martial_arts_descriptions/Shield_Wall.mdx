# Shield Wall
<b>School:</b> [Guardian](../guardian_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 4<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 10 Seconds<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> Upon casting this Artistry, the Caster must cross both arms across their chest and can move at no faster than a walking pace. So long as both these requirements are met, the Caster is immune to all Weapon Damage and [&lt;Effect&gt;- Strikes](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#strike). This effect automatically ends after ten(10) seconds.<br/>
