# Style Master
import Skill from '../skill.tsx'

<b>Requirements:</b> None\
The Character gains the ability to wield all
[Shields](../../equipment/shield) and the ability to use a single shield in
combat. Additionally, they gain the ability to wield any
[One-Handed Weapon](../../equipment/weapons), with which they are proficient,
in their off-hand at the same time they wield a
Weapon in their main hand. This Weapon's damage
is increased by the main hand Character's Weapon
Training by an amount equal to the Character's main
hand Weapon Training minus four(4). While this
means the minimum damage bonus is zero(0), the
Character will still cause the Base Damage of the
Weapon they are wielding in their off-hand. At the
time this Skill is bought, the Character may “sell back”
any Skills which are included in its benefit.
