# Force Storm
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 8<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self and Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Concentration<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> One this Spell is cast, the Caster may deliver a packet attack, causing ten(10) points of [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) Force damage, at will. This Spell ends if the Caster casts another Spell or suffers any effect which would Disrupt Spell casting including taking damage or entering a state which would prevent them from casting Spells. The Caster cannot run while Concentrating.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Conjure a Force Storm"<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "10 [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) Force … 10 Magic Force"<br/>
