# Flanking
<b>Requirements:</b> [Stealth Training](/docs/skills/expertise/Stealth_Training.mdx) 2 per Rank<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is bought, the Character may Invoke this ability against a single target. The Character gains a bonus to all Weapon Damage on their attacks against the target equal to their ranks in Stealth Training. This effect ends at the end of Combat. The call for this ability is "I Invoke Flanking against you."<br/>
