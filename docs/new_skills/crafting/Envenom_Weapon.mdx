# Envenom Weapon
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 3 Alchemy Slot, [Slimy Coating](/docs/new_skills/crafting/Slimy_Coating.mdx) 5<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Skill grants the Character the ability to deliver all 1 st , 2 nd , and 3 rd level Alchemy, the Character casts from memory, as a [&lt;Effect&gt;- Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#strike) with their Weapon instead of a Packet. The Delivery Type used to deliver the effect is determined by the Delivery Type of the Alchemy. For example, if the Alchemy allows the Character to deliver a Poison [Sleep](/docs/glossary/compulsion#sleep) effect, the call for this ability is "[Poison-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) Sleep". If the effect causes "10 Physical Acid" damage, the Channelled effect is "10 [Physical-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) Acid". They do not need a free hand to use Alchemy if they are wielding a Weapon with which they are Proficient. They may even Touch Cast Alchemy with their Weapon by placing the Weapon on the target during the Delivery Verbal.<br/>
