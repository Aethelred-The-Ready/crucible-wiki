# Diagnose
<b>Requirements:</b> [First Aid](/docs/new_skills/background/First_Aid.mdx)<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Skill allows the Character to determine the state of the target. In order to use this Skill, the Character must touch a willing, or helpless creature and state "Diagnose" followed by one of the following questions; "Are you Sleeping?", "Are you Unconscious?", "Are you Dying?", "Are you Dead?", "Are you Paralyzed?", "Are you Poisoned?", "Are you Regenerating?", "How long until you dissipate?", "How long until you wake-up?", "How long until you die?",  "How long until you become Unparalyzed?", "How long until you finish Regenerating?". "How many Hit Points are you missing?". It is not possible to use this Skill on a target without their knowledge and an unwilling target can simply say "I [Block](/docs/glossary/block) you." A Helpless target is unable to stop this Skill from being used on them. The Character cannot ask another question until the first is answered and this Skill takes as long as it takes for the Character to ask the question and the target to answer. It cannot, normally, be used against non-humanoid creatures, or those that have no, or alien metabolisms.<br/>
