# Empathic Healing
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 2<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self and Touch<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Sustained<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the Caster the ability to deliver a Touch effect causing one(1) point of [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing) to the target. Each time the Character uses this ability they lose one(1) Hit Point. This Spell is not Disrupted by the damage it causes to the Caster.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dawn to grant Empathic Healing."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "1 [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing)"<br/>
