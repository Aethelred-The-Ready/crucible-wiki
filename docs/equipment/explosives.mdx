# Explosives and Traps


### Grenade
These items represent a small explosive
device which harms only the first target with which it
is struck. Traditionally, they are represented by a
single packet, but they may also be represented by a
small thrown weapon measuring no more than
eight(8) centimetres in its longest dimension. Like all
weapons, these special grenades must pass
inspection at the start of each Event.

### Bombs
These items are represented by a handful
of packets. When used, the Character may throw any
number of packets, using one hand, and all at once.
Any target struck by one or more packets takes the
effect of the bomb. No device or method of delivery,
other than packets, is acceptable to deliver the
effects of a Bomb.

### Traps
The Physical Representations for Traps
follow the following requirements; the minimum
dimension of any Trap is thirty(30) centimetres. Any
recesses in the Trap’s representation, such as holes,
count against the total area. The maximum total area
of any standard Trap is one(1) square metre, or ten-
thousands(10,000) centimetres. The maximum total
area of any Trap modified by the Skill Giant Trap is
four(4) square metres, or ten-thousands(40,000)
centimetres. There are only five colours which can be
used for the Physical Representations of Trap; blue,
green, red, white, and yellow. No other colours can
be used. A marking in a contrasting colour to indicate
the type of Trap may also be added.
Once a Trap is set it may not be moved. But the
Character who set the Trap may adjust the Trap to
return it to its original position without triggering, or
needing to disarm, the Trap.
