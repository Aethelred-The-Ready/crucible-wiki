# Stasis

This effect renders the target completely
immobilised, they are unable to move, or be moved,
cannot use Skills, and all Counts and Counted
actions, such as; [Dying](/docs/combat/states-of-living#dying), [Regenerating](/docs/glossary/regenerate), [Refit](/docs/combat/armour-points#refit), and
even [Killing Blows](/docs/glossary/killing-blow) are frozen while this effect is in
place. A target under the effect of Stasis is [Immune](/docs/combat/defences#immmune) to
all effects until the effect ends. Even effects delivered
as Arcane cannot bypass this effect.
