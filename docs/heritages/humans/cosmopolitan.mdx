# Cosmpolitan

These humans live their lives within, or in support of a city. They may work on a farm that provides one with food, or they may work in the sewers beneath the roads of the capital. These humans know that the best people, best food and best life can be found in cities where all the people come together.\
Despite this positive outlook, they tend to assume that what they know is true. This leads to many of the other peoples looking upon them with contempt or as you would upon a child who is confidently incorrect. Some of them are able to move past this and see that the world is strange and full of wonders and the best way forward is to keep learning.

### Costuming Requirements

None

### Costuming Suggestions

Cosmopolitan humans have a tendency to wear tailored clothing but the styles vary greatly between regions. Their clothing is a mix of comfort and vanity.
