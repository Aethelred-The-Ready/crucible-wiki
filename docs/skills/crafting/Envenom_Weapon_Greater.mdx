# Envenom Weapon, Greater
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 6 Alchemy Slot, [Envenom Weapon](/docs/skills/crafting/Envenom_Weapon.mdx)<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Skill grants the Character the ability to deliver all 4th, 5th, and 6th level Alchemy, the Character casts from memory, as a [&lt;Effect&gt;- Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#strike) with their Weapon instead of a Packet. They do not need a free hand to use Alchemy if they are wielding a Weapon with which they are Proficient. They may even [Contingency](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#touch) Cast Alchemy with their Weapon by placing the Weapon on the target during the Delivery Verbal.<br/>
