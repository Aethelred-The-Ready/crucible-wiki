# Ork

Orks are a group of proud and highly skilled warriors valuing a complex code of honour. Though each Ork has their own code, they all share basics of not attacking an unarmed opponent, or intentionally harming the helpless. Sadly this is where many Orks draw the line leading to many brutal battles.\
Orks are neither stupid not savage. They are trained from an early age as hunters and warriors. Though magic is often seen as secondary, they still boast a fair number of capable mages.

### Costuming Requirements

Green skin.\
Blackened Eyes.

### Costuming Suggestions

Orks tend to favour function over form. As a result their clothing and armour tends to look tossed together or only half finished.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Blooded Weapon:
Once per [Reset](../../glossary/reset), upon delivering a successful [Killing Blow](../../glossary/killing-blow) the Character's Weapon Damage is increased by one(1) until the end of [Combat](../../glossary/combat). The Ork may [Rest](../../glossary/rest) in order to regain the use of this ability.
