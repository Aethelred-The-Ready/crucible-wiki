# Dispel Elemental
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell instantly removes all effects which were delivered as [Elemental(-Strike)](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental) from the target.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dusk to Dispel Elemental."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel) [Elemental](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental)"<br/>
