# Familiar Bond
<b>Type:</b> Magic<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Reset<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> At the time of this Invocation's casting, the Character must touch a single target. The Caster gains the ability to cast Spells with a range of "Self" on the target as [Contingency](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#touch) spells. A Caster cannot have more than one target of Familiar Bond in existence at a time. This effect ends if the Caster enters their [Death](/docs/glossary/death) Count or they Cast this Invocation on a new target.

 If the Caster casts a Spell with a duration of Sustained on the target, any Spells cast by the target, or having another Spell with a duration of Sustained cast on them removes the previous Spell's effects.<br/>
