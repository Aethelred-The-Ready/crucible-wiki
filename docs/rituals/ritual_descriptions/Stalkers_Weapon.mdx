# Stalker's Weapon
<b>Cost:</b> P3 C1 D2 E1<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Magic, Smithing<br/>
<b>School:</b> [Affliction](/docs/magic/affliction_list), [Conjuration](/docs/magic/conjuration_list), [Transmutation](/docs/magic/transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Any Weapon<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Event<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual increases the base damage of the target weapon by two(2) whenever the wielder is behind their target and able to see both of their shoulder blades. This bonus damage also applies any time the Character is under an effect which would allow them to use their damage bonus from Stealth Training. Additional castings of this Ritual Effect in the same Concert increase the damage according to the following table. Castings Damage Bonus Ritual Slots 1 +2 1 3 +4 2 6 +6 3 10 +8 4 15 +10 5<br/>
