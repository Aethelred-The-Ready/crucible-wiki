# Innate Spellcasting, Superior
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 9 [Spell Slot](/docs/new_skills/magic/Spell_Slot.mdx), Greater [Innate Spellcasting](/docs/new_skills/magic/Innate_Spellcasting.mdx)<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Skill grants the Character the ability to deliver all 7 th , 8 th , and 9 th level Spells, the Character casts from memory, without their Incantation. Additionally, they may cast these Spells while unable to speak, such as while under the effects of a [Silence](/docs/glossary/compulsion#silence), or without a free hand.

Additionally, the Character may expend a use of the Quicken Spell Skill in order to instantly regain the ability to cast a single Spell, delivered from memory, which they would have been able to recover by Meditation.<br/>
