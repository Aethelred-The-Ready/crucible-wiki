# Elemental Portal
<b>Type:</b> Magic<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Concentration<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> Upon activating this Invocation the Character chooses one of the following elements; Chaos, Destruction, Dream, Essence, Fire, Ice, Light, Lightning, Order, Reason, Shadow, or Stone. The Character may deliver, by packet, an attack causing ten(10) points of [Elemental](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental) Damage of the chosen type at will.

 The effects of this Invocation last until the Character uses any other Skill, takes Hit Point damage, is subjected to any effect which would Disrupt the casting of a Spell, or they move any faster than a normal walking pace.<br/>
