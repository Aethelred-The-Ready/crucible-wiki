---
title: "Strength <#>"
---

This ability increases all weapon
damage of the Character by an amount equal to &lt;#>.
This effect stacks with all similar effects which
increase weapon damage but multiple effects which
grant Strength do not stack unless explicitly stated
otherwise.\
When calculating a Character's Strength, first
determine the points of non-stacking Strength then
every point of stacking Strength. For example a Zoan
Character with the Strength in Unity Pact Tactic has
Strength 1 from the ability. But when under the effects
of the Verdant Strength Spell they gain Strength 1.
Their total from non-stacking effects is one(1). This
would stack with the Skill Natural Strength for a total
value of Strength 1.
