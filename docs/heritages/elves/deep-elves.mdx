# Deep Elf

The Deep Elves can be found both far below the  surface in ancient cities and in the deepest reaches of ancient magical forests where the dense canopy of trees blots out the sun. They thrive in darkness with many being greatly averse to sunlight. A rigid caste system and societal pressures to adhere to it ensure their people's continued survival in their often inhospitable lands.

### Costuming Requirements

All Deep elves have dark grey or purple skin and pointed ears.

### Costuming Suggestions

Many have white hair, but some, especially those who have interbred with other peoples, have more common hair colours.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Double Throw:
Whenever a Deep Elf delivers an attack using a [Thrown Weapon](../../equipment/thrown-weapon) they may throw one(1) additional Thrown Weapon. Any creature struck by either Weapon is affected once by the attack. This additional weapon, and any others the Character may throw, must be thrown with the same hand and must be thrown at the same time.
