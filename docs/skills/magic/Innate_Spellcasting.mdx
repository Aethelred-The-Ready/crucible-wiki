# Innate Spellcasting
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 3 [Spell Slot](/docs/skills/magic/Spell_Slot.mdx), [Quicken Spell](/docs/skills/magic/Quicken_Spell.mdx) 5<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Skill grants the Character the ability to deliver all 1st, 2nd, and 3rd level Spells, the Character casts from memory, without their Incantation. Additionally, they may cast these Spells while unable to speak, such as while under the effects of a [Silence](/docs/glossary/compulsion#silence), or without a free hand.<br/>
