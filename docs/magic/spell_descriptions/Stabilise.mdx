# Stabilise
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 1<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell instantly [Stabilise](/docs/glossary/stabilise)s a Dying creature putting them up to zero(0) Hit Points. This Spell has no effect on a creature that is not Dying.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dawn to Stabilise you."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Stabilise](/docs/glossary/stabilise)"<br/>
