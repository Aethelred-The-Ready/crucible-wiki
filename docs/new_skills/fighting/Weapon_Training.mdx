# Weapon Training
<b>Requirements:</b> See Text<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> Upon gaining this Skill choose a hand; left or right. The Character increases the Weapon Damage call of all Weapons they wield in the chosen hand by one(1) for every Rank they have in this Skill. There are two ways to purchase this skill. The first is to trade in a number of ranks of Weapon Prowess equal to their number of ranks in Weapon Training plus three(3). The Character does not receive any build back for the traded in Weapon Prowess ranks as this is built into the cost for the Skill. The second is to purchase the Skill outright at a cost which is increased each time the Character purchases another Rank. For example, the cost for 3 Ranks of Weapon Training for a Character with Priority A in Fighting is 42 (12 + 14 + 16).<br/>
