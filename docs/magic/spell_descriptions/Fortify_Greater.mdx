# Fortify, Greater
<b>School:</b> [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 3<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Indefinite<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the target ten(10) Fortification Points. These Fortification Points are always the first lost due to damage. Multiple effects which grant Fortification Points do not stack.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the protection of Greater Fortification."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Fortify](/docs/glossary/fortify) 10"<br/>
