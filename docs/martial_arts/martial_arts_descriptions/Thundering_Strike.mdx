# Thundering Strike
<b>School:</b> [Duelist](../duelist_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 2<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Strike<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> As part of casting this Artistry the Caster chooses one of the following types; Fire, Ice, Lightning, or Stone. This Artistry causes ten(10) [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical)-Strike damage of the chosen type.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "10 [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical)-Strike &lt;Type&gt;"<br/>
