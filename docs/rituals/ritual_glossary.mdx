# Ritual Glossary

## Outcomes

### Success
The Primary Caster declares "Ritual Magic succeeds" and the Ritual takes effect. All expended components and scrolls are turned over to the Ritual Marshall, who now makes any tags or modifications to character sheets.  

### Flaw
A Ritual can succeed but with an unusual
effect either positive or negative. Flaws only occur in
Rituals which are naturally unstable, include Special
Components which cause it to become unstable, or
the Concert is the target of the Instil Chaos Ritual
effect.

See the [Flaw Table](./ritual_flaws) for more details

### Fail
A Ritual can fail if one of several events occur,
all of which are described below. A Ritual which Fails
ends without producing its effect but all components
that would have been used for its casting are
consumed.

### Backlash
This occurs when the Ritual not only fails, but does
so violently. This can be caused by any of the Ritual's
Casters taking Hit Point damage, entering a state
where they are unable to use Skills, such as
becoming Drained or Unconscious, or chooses to
cause the Ritual to Backlash.

There are two standard effects which occur
whenever a Ritual Backlashes. The first is that
everyone in the Circle takes an amount of [Arcane](../glossary/delivery-types#arcane)
Body damage equal to the total number of
Components in the Ritual times ten(10). The second
is that all Casters are affected by an [Arcane](../glossary/delivery-types#arcane) [Drain](../glossary/curse#drain). 

### Catastrophe

In rare cases, the Backlash of a Ritual may result
in a Catastrophe. These are effects which are far
more harmful than the standard Backlash. In the
event of a Catastrophe a [Hold](../intro#hold) must be called and the
Primary Caster rolls on the [Catastrophe Table](./ritual_catastrophies).

 The effects of all Catastrophes are considered to
be delivered as [Arcane](../glossary/delivery-types#arcane) and as such they cannot be
avoided in anyway even by [Immunity](/docs/combat/defences#immune).

## Targets

### Body
This refers to a normal Character who is not
in an alternate state of existence such as out of
game, or on another plane. These effects end if the
Character dissipates.

### Item
This refers to the Mastercrafted, Legendary or
Mythical Item, which is the target of one or more
Ritual effects. It can be a weapon, trinket, or armour.
All items which contain one or more Ritual effects are
[Indestructible](../glossary/indestructible) and [Immune](/docs/combat/defences#immune) to the effects of [Break](../glossary/break) and [Destroy](../glossary/destroy) &lt;Item&gt;.

Each Character is able to use no more than five(5)
Ritual Items at once. An item is considered to be a
Ritual Item, and subject to this limit, if it has at least
one(1) Ritual Effect. Should a Character have more
than five(5) such items in their possession they are
unable to use any of the effects contained in the
additional items unless they reduce the number of
items they have in their possession to five(5) or
fewer.

#### Masterwork Item
These Items can hold up to
five(5) Ritual Effects. They are the most common
form of Item and can be found or created by Skills
such as [Smithing](../skills/crafting/smithing) and [Merchant Skills](../skills/background/merchant).

#### Legendary Item
These rare Items can hold up to
eight(8) Ritual Effects. These Items can be found in
limited quantities and some [Merchant Skills](../skills/background/merchant) can create these
Items once per year.

#### Mythical Item
These incredibly rare Items can hold
up to ten(10) Ritual Effects. These Items can only be
found in the rarest of circumstances and never
created by Skills.


### Spirit
This refers to the target creature with the
ability to resurrect; such as a Player Character, or an
NPC designated as such by Plot. Simple animals,
mindless creatures, and even some townspeople do
not have a strong enough Spirit to be targeted by
Ritual effects.

## General Terms

### Caster
All Characters participating in the Ritual as
either the Primary Caster or a Secondary Caster.

#### Primary Caster
The Character who is in control of
the Ritual Effect. 

#### Secondary Caster
Any Caster other than the Primary Caster.

### Chaos
The [Instil Chaos Ritual](./ritual_descriptions/instil_chaos) effect and certain Special Components can add "Chaos" to a ritual

 Once the initial value of Chaos has been
determined the Primary Caster of the Instil Chaos
Ritual has sole decision making authority as to how it
is spent. They are also the one who rolls on the Flaw
Table to determine the result.

 There is one rare exception which can increase the
amount of Chaos available in the Ritual. When a
Special Component which increases Chaos is used in
the Casting of a Ritual which is part of the Concert,
the additional Chaos is available for that roll, and only
that roll. Any remaining Chaos originating from the
Special Component is lost.

### Circle
A Circle is a closed loop with a total diameter
of no more than 5 metres and a total circumference of
no more than 16 metres. Circles must be lit, either
because they are in an area which is constantly
illuminated or because the Circle itself is lit.

#### Protective Circle
All two(2) Year Ritual Circles and
Circles created with the Protective Circle Ritual Effect
are magically protected from intrusion. No creature
which is not Invested in the Circle may enter, or exit,
the Circle unless they are first “Recognize” by a
Character is is Invested in the Circle. All those inside
of a Protective Circle are [Immune](/docs/combat/defences#immune) to all effects which
originate from outside the Circle.

### [Component](./ritual_component)
A [Ritual Component](./ritual_component) is an item which is
necessary for casting Rituals. Components have
seven types; Power, Creation, Destruction,
Elemental, Spirit, Time and Void. Components are
expended to pay the Component cost listed on each
Ritual.

Only a Character posessing one(1), or more, of the following Skills;
Alkahestry, Grand Master Smithing, Ritual Magic can Identify a Ritual Component.

A Character without at least one of these skills is only able to recognized that it is a Ritual Component, but not its strength or type.

Components are typically referenced as "E1" or "P2" where the letter indicates the type and the numeral either the strength of the individual compoennt or the total amount of that component needed

A single component cannot be used in multiple Rituals, even within a single Concert.

#### Special Component
This refers to a Special
Component which needed to perform certain Ritual
effects. Special Components can be Identified to
discover any Ritual to which they are applicable. 

#### Component Voucher

A Component Voucher is a
special item which can be used towards the
Component cost for a single Ritual, or Ritual Concert.
A Component Voucher is treated as if it were a
collection of Strength one(1) Components whose total
value equals that listed on the Component Voucher.
At the end of the Ritual, or Ritual Concert, the
Voucher is consumed regardless of any Component
value remaining on the Voucher. All Component
Vouchers have an expiration date determined by
Logistics.

### Concert
Also called a “Batch Casting”, this refers to
a series of Rituals which are cast back to back on a
single target. The Primary Caster can change for the
casting of each individual Ritual but all participants in
any one Casting must be present as a Caster for all
casting within the Concert. Should any Ritual in the
Concert Fail or Backlash the Concert immediately
ends. Alternatively the Concert automatically ends if
one of the following events occurs; the Primary
Caster chooses to end the Concert, more than one(1)
minute, per Caster, goes by between Castings, or the
Concert completes the casting of an [Extend Ritual](./ritual_descriptions/extend_ritual)
effect.

### Limited
Certain scrolls are marked as Limited. This
means that their use by players is limited. They
cannot be memorised by Player Characters and
cannot be Copied.

### Ritual Effect Slots
A Mastercrafted Item has five(5)
Slots for Ritual Effects. A Legendary Item has eight(8)
Slots for Ritual Effects. A Mythical Item has ten(10)
Slots for Ritual Effects. Characters have five(5) Slots
for Ritual Effects. A target may have a number of
Ritual Effects, of any type; Alchemy, Smithing, or
Magic, whose total does not exceed the target's
number of Ritual Effect Slots and attempts to add a
additional Ritual Effect(s) automatically Fail.

### Ritual Voucher
This is a special Plot issued
Component set. A Ritual Voucher lists the name of
the Ritual acting as both the Scroll and all
Components needed for a single casting.

### Scroll

This is the general term for any Scroll,
Formula, or Schematic which details and allows for
the casting of the described Ritual effect contained
therein.

### Unstable Ritual
 Some Rituals are inherently
unstable, or can become unstable with the addition of
Special Components or if the Concert is the target of
the [Instil Chaos Ritual](./ritual_descriptions/instil_chaos) effect. Depending on the
cause, unstable Rituals may have an increased
chance to Fail, Flaw or Backlash.
