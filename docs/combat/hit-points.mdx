# Hit Points

Hit Points represent how much damage a
Character can endure before falling [Unconscious](/docs/combat/states-of-living#Unconsious).
Damage is usually done to [Armour Points](/docs/combat/armour-points) before Hit
Points but some effects do damage to Hit Points
directly. Effects which cause [Body](/docs/glossary/body) damage such as
Inflict Wounds spells and Cause Damage Alchemy
damage a target’s Hit Points bypassing any armour, but not
[Fortification points](/docs/combat/armour-points#fortification-points).


All Player Characters start with ten(10) Hit Points.
This value can be increased by the [Toughness Skill](/docs/skills/fighting/toughness).
Hit Points are restored by Healing effects. Each
point of Healing restored one(1) lost Hit Point. Any
amount of Healing which would restore more Hit
Points than the Character is missing is lost unless the
Character has an ability which states otherwise, such
as the [Narahk's](/docs/heritages/shaped/narahk) Runic Flesh ability.
