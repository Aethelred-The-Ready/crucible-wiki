# Archery
import Skill from '../skill.tsx'

<b>Requirements:</b> None\
The Character gains the ability to wield [Bows](../../equipment/bow) and [Crossbows](../../equipment/crossbow) in combat. The Character also has the ability to use these Weapons to block Weapon Attacks.\
In order to fire a shot from their (Cross)Bow the Character must remove an arrow, represented by an Archery Packet, from their Quiver. Then touch the Packet to the hand holding the (Cross)Bow and then role-play the drawing of the arrow back before throwing the Packet\
Unlike most packet attacks, Archery Packets can be blocked by Weapons and Shields.
