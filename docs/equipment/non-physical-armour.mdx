# Non Physical Armour

In order to provide a subtle, but distinct visual clue
that a person is benefiting from invisible armour, and
to avoid accidental accusations of not taking damage,
the three types of non-physical armour a Player
Character can possess must be represented as
follows.

### Arcane Armour
The Character must wear a number of distinct
runes, which are visible from a distance of two(2)
metres, equal to the amount of Arcane Armour the
Character is wearing divided by ten(10) rounded up.
This means a Character with thirty-five(35) points of
Arcane Armour must wear four(4) such runes as part
of their costuming. The runes must be worn as part of
a single accessory. The most common form of
costuming is a necklace with a number of charms
depicting a particular rune.

### Avoidance Armour
The Character must wear a number of feathers,
which are visible from a distance of two(2) metres,
equal to the amount of Avoidance Armour the
Character is wearing divided by ten(10) rounded up.
This means a Character with fifteen(15) points of
Avoidance Armour must wear two(2) such feathers as
part of their costuming. The feathers must be worn as
part of a single accessory. The most common form of
costuming is a necklace, or armband, with a number
of feathers.

### Slime Armour
The Character must wear a number of vials, which
are visible from a distance of two(2) metres, equal to
the amount of Natural Armour the Character is
wearing divided by ten(10) rounded up. This means a
Character with fifty(50) points of Slime Armour must
wear five(5) such vials as part of their costuming. The
vials must be worn as part of a single accessory. The
most common form of costuming is a necklace with a
number of small flasks hanging from it.

### Natural Armour
Natural armours other than Slime armour do not have a single unified physical representation, and occasionally (like the Harden Flesh spells) have no physical representation
