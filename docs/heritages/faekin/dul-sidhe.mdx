# Dul'Sidhe

The Dul'Sidhe are a group of Faekin known for being tied to death. Supposedly descended from the Dullahan and other thematically morbid Fae. Whatever their true ancestry, the Dul'Sidhe are known to be experts in all areas of death from causing it, to preventing it and even speaking to those who have moved on.\
Dul'Sidhe adventurers tend to be on the lookout for what their people call “a beautiful death”. What this entails varies between individuals but it typically involves a death which is welcomed as a friend, or is the result of willing self sacrifice to save the innocent

### Costuming Requirements

Collar worn around throat.\
Black around eyes.

### Costuming Suggestions

Many Dul'Sidhe dress in funerary garb or monochromatic clothing.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Dream of Death:
The first time this Character enters their [Death Count](../../glossary/states-of-living) they immediately receive the benefits of a [Contingency](../../glossary/compulsion#contingency) [Arcane](../../glossary/arcane) [Life](../../glossary/life) effect. This ability is usable once per [Reset](../../glossary/reset). This effect cannot be suppressed or prevented from triggering
