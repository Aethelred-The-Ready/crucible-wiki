# Teleport
<b>School:</b> [Transmutation](../transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 9<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell allows the Caster to either return to "Town", a designated Circle of their choosing, or to the last location the Character cast this Spell. The Caster may take any item or items they can carry, but cannot take any other creature. This Spell does not allow the Caster to travel between Planes. Upon casting this Spell, the Caster goes Out-of-Game and walks to the target destination. Immediately, upon arrival, the Player repeats the Delivery Verbal and goes In-Game. This is not a counted action. The Event Staff may designate a specific boundary as the location of "Town".<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> : "I alter this form to be Teleported."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Teleport](/docs/glossary/teleport)"<br/>
