# Dwarf

Dwarves are bearded humanoids who are known for their exceptional crafting abilities. Known for producing both large scale engineering projects as well as finely detailed works of art.\
As a result, Dwarves are very cooperative with their peers as they work together to accomplish their shared goal. Unfortunately, Dwarves are also reluctant to trust anyone who hasn't proven that they are competent.\
Dwarves honour their word and contracts as without agreements on each party's commitments it can lead to disappointment and mistakes

### Costuming Requirements

Costume Beard. If the Player has a natural beard they may add extensions or incorporate a number of trinkets, minimum three(3), decorating or woven into the hair.

### Costuming Suggestions

Dwarves are very proud of their hair and often add decorations, or try bold styles. They also prefer well made and well fitting clothing including various items the Dwarf has made, inherited, or been paid with.
