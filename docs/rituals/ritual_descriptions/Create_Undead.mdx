# Create Undead
<b>Cost:</b> Varies<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Magic<br/>
<b>School:</b> [Affliction](/docs/magic/affliction_list), [Transmutation](/docs/magic/transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Any Spirit<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Event<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual creates an Undead of the type specified by the [Scroll](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#scroll).

 Mindless Undead are bound to perform a single Service and a number of additional Services based on the number of Casters participating in the casting. For every three(3) Casters participating, rounded down, the number of services may be increased by one(1) to a maximum of five(5) total Services.

 Mindless Undead are only able to perform simple Services such as "Defend me,", "Follow me and search every creature I kill." or "Guard this area.". Invalid Services are ones which require Sentience such as "Travel to the town of Glenn and count how many foreign soldiers are there.". The maximum time a Service can take is one(1) hour.

 Intelligent Undead are bound to perform a single Service and a number of additional Services based on the number of Casters participating in the casting. For every five(5) Casters participating, rounded down, the number of services may be increased by one(1) to a maximum of five(5) total Services.

 A Service is a single finite task. The maximum time a Service can take is one(1) hour. Examples of valid Services are; "Follow and protect me on this adventure.", "Go to the town of Exampleton and tell me how many soldiers are there.", "Aid me with the casting of this Ritual.". Examples of invalid Services are; "Guard this area forever.", "Stay in this [Circle](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Circle) and help us cast all our Rituals.", "Explore all the camps of the foreign military and bring me a list of all commanders.".<br/>
