# Elemental Reduction
<b>School:</b> [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 2<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Touch<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Indefinite<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell reduces the damage of the next effect causing [Elemental](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental) damage, by which they are struck, by half rounded down.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the protection of Elemental Reduction."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Imbue Skill](/docs/glossary/imbue-skill): Limited [Elemental](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental) [Resist](/docs/glossary/resist)ance"<br/>
