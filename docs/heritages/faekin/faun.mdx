# Faun

The Faun are supposedly the descendants of Satyrs and nature Fae. They value personal freedoms and dislike any form of oppressive rule as well as magical compulsions which rob a person of their senses. Their incredible life spans lead them to seek new experiences and the oldest often choose to forget the past so they aren't bored.\
As adventurers the Faun tend to be there for the experience and a desire to help others gain new experiences. They offer advice to anyone who'll listen, though this advice may not always make sense as some will deceive just to see what happens.

### Costuming Requirements

Antlers or small horns

### Costuming Suggestions

Some Faun have large ears, either pointed or deer-like, and some have hoofed feet but these are not requirements

### Heritage Abilities

#### Free Will:
A Faun may, at will, spend sixty(60) seconds role-playing with another creature. If they choose to do so, they may deliver, by [Touch](../../glossary/delivery-types#touch), an “[Arcane](../../glossary/arcane) [Dispel](../../glossary/dispel) [Compulsion](../../glossary/compulsion)” effect against the target of this ability. If used, this ability must be used against the target of their role-play.\
Once per [Reset](../../glossary/reset), the Character may, instead of the Dispel Compulsion effect, deliver an Arcane [Remove](../../glossary/remove) [Enthral](../../glossary/enthrall) effect. If the Character has the [Break Compulsion Skill](../../skills/break-compulsion) as well, the Character may use the Break Charm ability an unlimited number of times each Reset.
