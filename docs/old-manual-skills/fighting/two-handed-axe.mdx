# Two-Handed Axe
import Skill from '../skill.tsx'

<b>Requirements:</b> None\
The Character gains the ability to wield a
[Two-Handed Axe](../../equipment/two-handed-axe) in combat. The Character may
block attacks while holding the Weapon with one
hand but cannot attack or use the weapon to deliver
any Skill until they have both hands on the Weapon.
