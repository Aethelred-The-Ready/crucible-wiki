# Miscellaneous Rules

## The Bank

Crucible uses a banking system in order for
players to store non-expiring game treasure in the
form of coins, ingots and similar items. This is done in
order to help supplement the limited availability of
these items and to reduce treasure accidentally being
lost. As a result the game exists in both an in and out
of game capacity. It exists as an in-game institution
for the storage of in-game resources but is otherwise
completely out of game and cannot be affected by
any in-game actions. Resources stored in the bank
can be retrieved only by the Character who deposited
the items.

Below is a list of items which can be stored in the
bank.

|Item|Can be Stored?|
|---|---|
|Currency|Yes|
|[Special Material Ingot](/docs/equipment/special-materials.mdx)|Yes|
|Non-Expiring [Ritual Components](/docs/rituals/ritual_component)|Yes|
|[Potions](/docs/skills/crafting/Create_Potion)/[Scrolls](/docs/skills/crafting/Create_Scroll)|Never|
|Mastercraft Item|Never|
|Ritual Items|Never|
|Plot Items|Never|

## Mailbox
The Mail system which Characters to communicate
with others through letters. If this rule is in use, a
Mailbox will be available for use by all Characters
allowing those with the ability to read and write to
send letters. Letters may be sent to any character so
long as the sender knows the recipient’s name. Mail
is not instantaneous, nor does it even communicate
at a predictable rate. Only letters may be deposited
into the mailbox. Only Staff may open the mailbox to
retrieve letters.

## Merchant Draw
Characters with the appropriate Skills will, at the
time of Event Registration, be given a number of
tickets for the Merchant Draw. These tickets cannot
be transferred to another Character or stolen. They
represent abstract connections the Character has
made with a large network of merchants throughout
the region. At the end of the Event any unused
Merchant Tickets must be destroyed.

 At each Event there will be a number of Merchant
Lots which all Characters with Merchant Tickets may
enter. The lots will have a general description of the
contents but may be general. Characters may divide
their tickets in any number of these lots. At the end of
the Event a single ticket will be drawn from each
Merchant lot containing one(1) or more tickets. The
Character who holds the winning ticket stub will be
awarded the contents of the lot.

 Under no circumstances may a Non-Player
Character enter or win a Merchant Draw. In the event
this should occur the ticket is discarded and another
is drawn in its place.

## Pre-Event Logistics
In order to accommodate the large number of
logistical activities that are carried out immediately at
the start of an Event certain activities can be
performed in advance. Effects which are placed upon
your sleeping area and personal belongings, such as
Sanctuary and Safeguard may be cast before the
beginning of gameplay. Spells cast upon a shared
sleeping area must include all those assigned to, or
set up to, sleep in that area. Additionally, certain
Ritual effects may be cast in advance by informing
Logistics in advance of the Event.

## Costumes and Disguises
While Players are encouraged to be creative when
designing their Character's costume there are some
artificial limitations in what Characters can wear do to
the limits of of our own world. Firstly, Player
Characters cannot wear monster masks which may
cause others to mistake them for a monster. It would
be obvious to even a casual observer that a human
wearing cloth skeleton bandanna is not really a
skeleton but there is little difference between a PC
wearing a skull mask and an NPC wearing a similar
one. As a result, the only masks that Player
Characters are permitted to use are coloured
bandanna, domino masks and veils provided that
these do not depict any creature.

 All Player Characters are recognisable as
themselves at all times. This is unfortunately
necessary as some Players may split their time
playing multiple Characters during an Event or take
spend some time helping to NPC adventures.

## Additional Rooms
Event Staff may, at their discretion, designate
additional areas as valid targets for effects which
target a Room, such as the Sanctuary effect, when
they believe the type of available accommodations, or
other circumstances of the Event warrant them. 

## Location of Town
Event Staff may, at their discretion, designate a
specific location as being “Town” for the purpose of
effects, such as [Teleport](/docs/glossary/teleport), which return a Character to
Town. Their discretion even allows them to designate
a specific area as “Town” for the purposes of effects
requiring you to be in Town such as Rest.

## Character Ritual Item Slots
Each Character is able to use no more than five(5)
Ritual Items at once. An item is considered to be a
Ritual Item, and subject to this limit, if it has at least
one(1) Ritual Effect. Should a Character have more
than five(5) such items in their possession they are
unable to use any of the effects contained in the
additional items unless they reduce the number of
items they have in their possession to five(5) or
fewer.

## Town Forge, Potion and Scroll Laboratories
Each town includes a Town Forge, Scroll and
Potion Laboratory which is freely usable by all Player
Characters. These laboratory include a number of
props and paraphernalia related to the crafted items
such as hammers, anvils for Smiths. Inks, quills and
papers for Scrolls. Herbs, vials of liquid and mixing
devices for Potions. Players are not permitted to
remove items from these laboratories. The supplies
for these laboratories are unlimited as each Player
must provide their own props for any Scrolls or
Potions they create with the aid of the laboratory.

 Additionally, Players may, with the approval of
Staff, create their own private laboratories but these
areas face a number of restrictions. Firstly, they can
only be placed in private areas where they will not
interfere with gameplay. Placing them in high traffic or
public areas is not allowed. Secondly, they must be
inspected and confirmed to meet a minimum
standard of immersion as determined by Staff. Finally,
they must be included in the Marshal notes for any in
game area in which they are placed
