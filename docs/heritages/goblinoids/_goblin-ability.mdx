#### Peerless Survivors: 
The first time a Goblinoid enters their [Dying count](../../glossary/states-of-living#dying) they automatically gain the effects of a [Contingency](../../glossary/compulsion#contingency) [Physical](../../glossary/delivery-types) [Stabilise](../../glossary/stabilise). Once triggered, the Goblinoid must spend five(5) minutes Resting before they can use it again.
