---
title: "Vorpal <#>"
---

This ability grants a number of charges
of the Vorpal effect to the target equal to &lt;#>. This
ability increases the Character's Weapon Damage
call by five(5) for a single landed hit for each charge.\
A charge is consumed only when the Character lands
a valid hit. One which strikes a shield or other
weapon does not consume a charge, nor does an
accidental strike to an illegal target. All remaining
charges are lost at the end of Combat.
