# The Shaped

The Shaped are reputed to be a group of peoples who were artificially created by some mysterious and long gone progenitor civilization called the Shapers. Whether this is true or not the Heritages of the Shapes all excel in particular areas of society.\
The Shaped all have a strong sense of duty, community and purpose with those who choose to adventure being those who want to bring back knowledge and materials which will benefit their homes and families.
