# Collapsing Cosmos
<b>Type:</b> Magical, Martial<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Intervention grants the Character the ability to deliver one attack for each of the following elements; Fire, Ice, Lightning, Stone, and Force. Each of the five(5) attacks causes one-hundred(100) points of [Piercing](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#piercing) [Elemental](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental) damage of the appropriate type.

 When performed as a Magical Intervention, the Character may choose to have all of these attacks delivered as packet attacks. Alternatively, and when performed as a Martial Intervention, the effect is delivered as Weapon attacks. The Character may use these attacks in any order but at the end of combat, all remaining attacks are lost. When delivered as a packet attack the call is "100 Piercing Elemental &lt;Type&gt;". When delivered as a weapon attack the call is "100 Piercing [Elemental-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental) &lt;Type&gt;"<br/>
