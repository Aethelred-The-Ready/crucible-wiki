# Predation
<b>School:</b> [Transmutation](../transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 4<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Touch<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Day<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the target two(2) points of [Cunning](/docs/glossary/cunning), increasing their Weapon Damage, when behind their target by two(2). The Cunning from this effect does not stack with any other source.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the power of Predation."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Imbue Skill](/docs/glossary/imbue-skill): [Cunning](/docs/glossary/cunning) 2"<br/>
