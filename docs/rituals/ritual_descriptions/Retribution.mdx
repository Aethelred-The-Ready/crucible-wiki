# Retribution
<b>Cost:</b> P4 C1 D1 E1 S2<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Magic<br/>
<b>School:</b> [Affliction](/docs/magic/affliction_list), [Conjuration](/docs/magic/conjuration_list), [Restoration](/docs/magic/restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Spirit<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Two(2) Years<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual empowers the target with a powerful form of magical vengeance. When the target is the victim of a Killing Blow the Player may choose to trigger this effect. If triggered, the killer is affected by "50 [Arcane](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#arcane) [Body](/docs/glossary/body)" plus twenty(20) additional Arcane Body damage for each additional Caster. This ability can be triggered once per [Reset](/docs/glossary/reset) for every five(5) Casters, rounded up, who participated in its casting. A Character may have only one casting of Retribution on their Spirit and any new casting fails unless it is more powerful than the previous one.<br/>
