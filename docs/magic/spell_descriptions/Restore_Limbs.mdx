# Restore Limbs
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell removes all [Cripple](/docs/glossary/curse#cripple) effects from the target regardless of Delivery Type.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dawn to Restore your Limbs."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel) [Cripple](/docs/glossary/curse#cripple)"<br/>
