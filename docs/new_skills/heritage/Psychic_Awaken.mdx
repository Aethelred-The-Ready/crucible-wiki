# Psychic Awaken
<b>Requirements:</b> None<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is bought, the Character may deliver, by Touch or Packet, a "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel) [Compulsion](/docs/glossary/compulsion)". This ability may also be used on themselves on a ten(10)-count even if they were in a state where they normally wouldn't be able to cast Spells.<br/>
