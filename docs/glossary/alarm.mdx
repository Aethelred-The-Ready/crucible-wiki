# Alarm

This effect can only be placed on a Room.
Should any creature, not invested in the Alarm effect,
enter the room they, or the accompanying Marshal,
must knock loudly for ten(10) seconds. The purpose
of this effect is to alert the occupants of the room, not
to prevent the intruder from defending themselves in
any way. The intruder may still engage in other
activities while they are knocking. An Alarm must be
included in the Marshal Notes for the affected Room.
The points of entry of the room must be marked with
a Stylized “A” or “W”.
