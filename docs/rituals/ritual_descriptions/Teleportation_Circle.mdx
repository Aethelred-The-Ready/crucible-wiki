# Teleportation Circle
<b>Cost:</b> P4 C1 S2 P1 per additional target. See text<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Magic<br/>
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](/docs/magic/conjuration_list), [Transmutation](/docs/magic/transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Target:</b> The Caster and One additional target per additional Power component<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> The targets are transported to Town. All targets immediately go Out of Game and walk to the area designated as Town by Staff. Upon arrival, all targets appear at the edge of the area with the phrase "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Teleport](/docs/glossary/teleport)".

 If the [Caster](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Caster) has access to a Special [Component](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Component) provided by Plot, this Ritual effect can be used to travel to the location designated by the [Special Component](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Special-Component)'s effect.

 The maximum number of targets of this Ritual is equal to twice the number of Casters participating in the Ritual's casting.<br/>
