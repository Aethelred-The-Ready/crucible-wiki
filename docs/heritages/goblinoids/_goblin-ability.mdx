#### Peerless Survivors: 
The first time a Goblinoid enters their [Dying count](/docs/combat/states-of-living#dying) they automatically gain the effects of a [Contingency](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#contingency) [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types) [Stabilise](/docs/glossary/stabilise). Once triggered, the Goblinoid must spend five(5) minutes Resting before they can use it again.
