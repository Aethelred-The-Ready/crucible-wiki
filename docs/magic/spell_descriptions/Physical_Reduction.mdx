# Physical Reduction
<b>School:</b> [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 1<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Touch<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Indefinite<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the target [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Resistance](/docs/glossary/resistance), reducing the damage from the next effect delivered as [Physical(-Strike)](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical), which strikes the target, by half rounded down. If the effect is non- damaging its duration is reduced to ten(10) seconds. This effect does not protect against effects delivered as [Piercing](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#piercing).<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the protection of Physical Reduction."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Imbue Skill](/docs/glossary/imbue-skill): Limited [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Resistance](/docs/glossary/resistance)"<br/>
