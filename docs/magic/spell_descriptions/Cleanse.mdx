# Cleanse
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 9<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell instantly removes all harmful effects from the target which would be removed by the target's [Death](/docs/glossary/death) as well as [Enthral](/docs/glossary/enthral). The target's player makes the determination as to which effects are harmful.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dawn to Cleanse you."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Cleanse](/docs/glossary/cleanse)"<br/>
