# Envenom Weapon, Superior
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 9 Alchemy Slot, Greater [Envenom Weapon](/docs/new_skills/crafting/Envenom_Weapon.mdx)<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Skill grants the Character the ability to deliver all 7 th , 8 th , and 9 th level Alchemy, the Character casts from memory, as a [&lt;Effect&gt;- Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#strike) with their Weapon instead of a Packet. They do not need a free hand to use Alchemy if they are wielding a Weapon with which they are Proficient. They may even Touch Cast Alchemy with their Weapon by placing the Weapon on the target during the Delivery Verbal. Additionally, the Character may expend a use of the Slimy Coating Skill in order to allow themselves to deliver a single copy of any Alchemy they have delivered as &lt;Effect&gt;-Strike using this ability if the initial swing failed to strike any target. The call for this copy is "Echo: &lt;Delivery Verbal&gt;".<br/>
