#### Iron Will:
Once per [Reset](/docs/glossary/reset), Elves can negate a single [Compulsion](/docs/glossary/compulsion) effect, by which they would be affected, as per the Skill [Resist Compulsion](/docs/skills/heritage/resist_compulsion).\
Additionally, once per [Reset](/docs/glossary/reset), an Elf may [Rest](/docs/glossary/rest) in order to recharge this ability allowing them to benefit from its effects a maximum of twice per Reset. The call for this ability is “Resist”.
