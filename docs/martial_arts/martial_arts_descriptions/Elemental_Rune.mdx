# Elemental Rune
<b>School:</b> [Slayer](../slayer_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 4<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Charged<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> The Caster of this Artistry may, at a time of their choosing, trigger it allowing them to use one of the following damage types; Fire, Ice, Lightning, or Stone, for the duration of Combat. Combat is considered to be over when the Character goes one(1) minute without attacking, or being attacked by, an enemy.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Elemental](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental) [Aura](/docs/glossary/aura)"<br/>
