# Energy Darts
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 0<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self and Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Charged (5)<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Cantrip grants the Caster five(5) charges of this effect. The Caster may expend one charge to deliver a packet attack causing two(2) points of [Elemental](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental) Damage of any of the following types; Fire, Ice, Lightning, or Stone. As a Cantrip, this effect can be used once, afterwards the Character must Rest in order to regain its use.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> None<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "2 [Elemental](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental) &lt;Type&gt;"<br/>
