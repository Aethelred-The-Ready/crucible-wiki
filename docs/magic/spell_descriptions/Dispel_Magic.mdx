# Dispel Magic
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list), [Transmutation](../transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 8<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell instantly removes all effects which were delivered as [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic)(-Strike) from the target.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dusk to Dispel Magic."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel) Magic"<br/>
