# Dispel Binding
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 5<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell instantly removes all [Binding](/docs/glossary/binding) effects from the target. The following effects are Binding; Bind, [Confine](/docs/glossary/binding#confine), [Pin](/docs/glossary/binding#pin), and [Web](/docs/glossary/binding#web).<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dusk to Dispel Binding."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel) [Binding](/docs/glossary/binding)"<br/>
