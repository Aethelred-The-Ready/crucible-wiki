# Bone Armour
<b>School:</b> [Affliction](../affliction_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 3<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Day<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> In order to cast this Spell the Caster must have costuming depicting bones on their torso. This Spell grants the Caster five(5) points of [Natural Armour](/docs/equipment/non-physical-armour). This effect does not stack with any other source of Natural Armour.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the power of Bone Armour."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Imbue Skill](/docs/glossary/imbue-skill): [Natural Armour](/docs/equipment/non-physical-armour) 5"<br/>
