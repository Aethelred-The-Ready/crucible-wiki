# Remove Drain, Mass
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 8<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self and Touch<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Concentration<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the Caster the ability to deliver, by Touch, a "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Remove](/docs/glossary/remove) [Drain](/docs/glossary/curse#drain)" effect at will. This effect removes, from the target, a single Drain effect. The Caster may continue to deliver this effect until they go ten(10) seconds without affecting a new target or the Spell is Disrupted. No target can benefit from this effect more than once.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dusk to Remove the Drains of the Masses."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Remove](/docs/glossary/remove) [Drain](/docs/glossary/curse#drain) … Magic Remove Drain"<br/>
