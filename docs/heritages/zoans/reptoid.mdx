# Reptoid

Many Reptoids are offended by being in the same group as the other Zoans but then again, Reptoids tend to get mad at a lot of things. They are much more solitary than the other Zoan forming groups when they need to solve a large or complex problem that they can't fix on their own.\
Reptoid adventurers are often in heavy combat roles where they are able to more easily demonstrate their contributions to battle. This does not mean they are always on the front lines, some are capable casters, but they want their presence to be known and recognized.

### Costuming Requirements

Reptile or avian traits.

### Costuming Suggestions

Reptoids tend to favour scaled leather and metal armour. Even their regular clothes have a tendency to follow this scaled theme.\
Some Reptoids instead choose to favour a single colour wearing only clothing of their preferred hue.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Venomous:
The Character gains the ability to [Conjure](../../glossary/conjure) a single Claw, up to a [Long Sword](../../equipment/long-sword) in length, and gains Proficiency in Claws. Additionally, the Character may grant themselves a “[Physical](../../glossary/delivery-types) [Vorpal](../../glossary/vorpal) 1” effect. This effect can be recovered by [Meditating](../../glossary/meditate).\
If the Character has the [Two Weapon Fighting Skill](../../skills/fighting/two-weapon-fighting), they may Conjure up to two(2) Claws at once in order to wield one(1) in each hand.\
If the Character is under an effect which prevents the use of Skills, such as [Drain](../../glossary/curse#drain), the Character is unable to attack or defend with these Claws.
