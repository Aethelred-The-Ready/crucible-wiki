# Lifebind
<b>Requirements:</b> None<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> Once per Reset, the Character may deliver a "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Tarry](/docs/glossary/tarry)" effect by Touch. Alternatively, they may activate this ability on themselves at any time during their [Death](/docs/glossary/death) Count.

If the Character has Rank 4 of the Seer Skill, this Skill can be expended to increase the time they may be Manifested by five(5) minutes.<br/>
