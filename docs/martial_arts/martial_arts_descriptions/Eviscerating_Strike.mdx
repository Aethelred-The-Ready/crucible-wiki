# Eviscerating Strike
<b>School:</b> [Slayer](../slayer_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 8<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Strike, Backstab<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Artistry allows the Caster to deliver a Weapon Attack causing fifty(50) points of damage appropriate to the Weapon Type of their weapon. In order to use this Artistry the Caster must be standing behind the target and able to see both the target's shoulder blades or expend a Charge of [Ambush](/docs/glossary/ambush).<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "50 [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical)-Strike &lt;Weapon Type&gt;"<br/>
