---
title: "Cunning <#>"
---
This ability grants the Character &lt;#>
additional points of Weapon Damage when they are
standing behind their target and able to see both of
their shoulder blades. This effect stacks with all
similar effects which increase weapon damage but
multiple effects which grant Cunning do not stack
unless explicitly states otherwise.
When calculating a Character's Cunning, first
determine the points of non-stacking Cunning then
every point of stacking Cunning. For example a Zoan
Character with the Hidden Numbers Pact Tactic has
Cunning 2 from the ability. But when under the effects
of the Greater Predation Spell they gain Cunning 4.
Their total from non-stacking effects is four(4). This
would stack with the Skill Natural Cunning for a total
value of Cunning 6.
