# Jellyflesh
<b>School:</b> [Alchemy](../alchemy_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 5 Minutes or Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> As part of casting this Alchemy the Caster may choose a limb such as left arm, left leg, right arm, right leg. This Alchemy prevents the target from using the chosen limb. A [Cripple](/docs/glossary/curse#cripple)d arm cannot be used to use any item or cast Spells. A Crippled leg prevents the target from walking, forcing them to kneel. Alternatively, this Alchemy may be used to remove all Cripple effects from the target regardless of Delivery Type.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) [Cripple](/docs/glossary/curse#cripple) &lt;Limb&gt;" or "Poison [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel) Cripple"<br/>
