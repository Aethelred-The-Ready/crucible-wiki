# Voxian

The Voxians are skilled diplomats, teachers and communicators. They are often found as mediators for complex social issues as well as on the stage acting as thespians and musicians.\
Voxians tend to get along with everyone being both friendly and accommodating of others. This has led them to being viewed, by some, with suspicion. Each Voxian wears a mask which depicts characteristics of another Heritage. In ages past these masks were supposedly perfect imitations but in the modern world they are little more than theatrical masks worn only to give the artistic impression of the Voxian belonging to another Heritage.

### Costuming Requirements

Full face mask depicting a Heritage.

### Costuming Suggestions

Voxians tend to wear bulky clothing which hides their body shape. Their clothing is often imperfect, sleeves the wrong length, seams not quite straight or other errors.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Commanding Voice: 
When using any ability which allows them to throw a packet attack causing a [Compulsion](../../glossary/compulsion) effect, this Character may throw an additional packet.
