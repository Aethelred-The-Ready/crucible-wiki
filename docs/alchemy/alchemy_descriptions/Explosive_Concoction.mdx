# Explosive Concoction
<b>School:</b> [Alchemy](../alchemy_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 9<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> As part of casting this Spell the Caster chooses one of the following options; Acid or Fire. This Alchemy allows the Caster to deliver a packet attack causing fifty(50) points of [Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) damage of the chosen type.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "50 [Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) Acid" or "50 Poison Fire"<br/>
