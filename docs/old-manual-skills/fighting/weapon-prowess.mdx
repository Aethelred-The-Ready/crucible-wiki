# Weapon Prowess
import Skill from '../skill.tsx'

<b>Requirements:</b> None\
Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is
bought, the Character may grant themselves the
effects of a [Vorpal](../../glossary/vorpal) 5. This allows them to increase the
Weapon Damage call of their next five(5) landed hits
by five(5). The call for this ability is
“Physical Vorpal 5”
A Character may have a number of Weapon
Prowess that does not exceed five(5) plus one(1) for
each [Weapon Training](./weapon-training) they possess.
