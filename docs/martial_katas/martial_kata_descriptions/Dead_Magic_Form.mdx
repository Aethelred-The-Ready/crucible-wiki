# Dead Magic Form
<b>School:</b> [Guardian](/docs/martial_arts/guardian_list)<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Kata grants the Character five(5) Charge of this ability. The Character can expend one(1) Charge to reduce the damage of an effect delivered as [Magic(-Strike)](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) by half. Alternatively, the Character may expend one(1) Charge to reduce the duration of any effect delivered as Magic(-Strike) to ten(10) seconds. The call for this ability is "Reduced".<br/>
