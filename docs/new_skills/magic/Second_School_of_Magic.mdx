# Second School of Magic
<b>Requirements:</b> [First School of Magic](/docs/new_skills/magic/First_School_of_Magic.mdx)<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Skill grants the Character access to their second School of Magic. The Character is able to expend their Spell Slots to use the Spells which belong to any schools they know. See Chapter 7: Magic for more information on the Schools of Magic.<br/>
