# Miscellaneous Glossary

Some terms have specific meanings out of game. They are described here

### Blanket
This is an amount of Experience Points equal to a Character's current Skill Point Total. An Adventurer earns one for every "Adventuring Day" they participate in, and 0.5 for each Partial Day event attended.

### Character
This refers to a creature, Player Character or other in game character. 

### Character Level
A Character with 60-69 Skill Points inclusive is a Level 1 Charater. A Character's level is always their currently SP total divided by 10, rounded up, then minus 5.

### Copper
Out of game Volunteer currency, earned for NPCing, assisting with cleanup, pulling a Kitchen shift, or donations. They can be used to purchase Blankets, Character Rebuilds, Ritual Components, Items, or Scrolls, or buy back deaths

### \# Count (aka Counted Action)
 This is an action
which takes some amount of time for the Character to
perform. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, in the
ability’s description or the Character’s Skill Card, all
counts are interrupted if; the Character is struck by
any effect other than Touch Casting, the Character
uses any Skills, or enters a state where they are
unable to use Skills. A Character can perform only
one count at a time. This section applies only to
actions taken by the Character. It does not apply to a
Character’s Death Count and similar timed effects.

### Day
This refers to a Game Day. Events have a
number of game Days, usually two(2), that they run.
Unless the Event is only one(1) Day long, each Day
ends at 18:00 and the next one begins. Effects which
end at the end of the Day automatically expire at
18:00 or the end of the Event, whichever occurs first

### Encounter (aka Module or Mod or Adventure)
 This refers
to a planned encounter run, or instigated, by an
Encounter Marshal as part of the Event’s Plot.

### Event
This refers to the event itself. Effects which
last until the end of the Event, as well as all Scrolls
and Potions automatically expire at the conclusion of
the Event. 

### Experience Points (aka EXP or XP)
This refers to the
Character’s accumulated experience total. As the
Character gains Experience Points these points are
automatically converted into Skill Points at a ratio
determined by the Character’s Level.

### Humanoid
This refers to all Player Characters as
well as similar creatures.

### Invested
Certain effects, such as Sanctuary and
Protective Circles, have Characters Invested in them.
An Invested Character is able to enter or exit the
effect. Any other Character is prevented from passing
through the effect, entering or exiting, unless they are
“Recognized” through the effect by a Character who
is Invested. Characters can become Invested
according to the rules defined by the individual
effects.


### Marshal
This is a local member of staff who
oversees one or more aspects of an event. There are
many types of Marshals:

Weapons Marshals must check all Weapons prior to their use for safety and verification

Armour Marshals must check all Armour for validity and safety

Ritual Marshals oversee the successful execution of a ritual

Encounter Marshals adjudicate rules specific to their encounter, or answer general questions

Rules Marshals can answer or adjudicate rules questions

### Player
This refers to the Player themselves and not their Character

### Player Character (aka PC)
This refers specifically to the Character(s) created by and under control of their Player

### Non-Player Character (aka NPC)
This refers to
Characters who are under the control of Plot. They
may be indistinguishable from a PC or be something
more unusual. 

### Plot
This refers to the group of Marshals who run the Event

### Rest
This refers to a special five(5) minute action
where a Character recuperates from combat. It can
only be performed in Town or at a designated Rest
area. Certain Skills and abilities can be recovered if
the Character Rests. If the Character has multiple
abilities which require a Rest they may recover all of
them with a single Rest. A Rest is interrupted if the
Character is struck by any effect, other than Touch
Casting, the Character uses any Skills, or enters a
state where they are unable to use Skills.

### Room 
A Room is any immovable structure, or area
within such a structure which has clearly recognized
boundaries such as walls and a doorway through
which to enter the area, which includes a ceiling or
roof. It includes, but is not limited to constructed
buildings, caves, tents which are secured to the
ground, covered wagons which have been rendered
immobile and similar areas. An effect which targets a
Room can never be placed on an area which has
been designated as out of game such as bathrooms.

### Skill
This refers to all abilities which are purchased
using Skill Points unless the Skill explicitly states
otherwise.

### Skill Point (aka SP)
 This refers to the currency
used by Characters to purchase Skills for their
Character.

### Town
This is generally the area where the Players sleep
although the Event Staff may designate, at the start
of the Event, a more specific location. For example,
Staff may designate that Town is the main floor of the
Inn, or the seating area of the firepit. When such a
location is designated, effects which require the
Character to be in Town, such as Resting, require the
Character to enter these areas to benefit from these
effects. Finally, effects, such as Teleport, which return
the Character to Town require the Character to enter
the designated area before resolving them.
