# Safeguard
<b>School:</b> [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Container<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Event<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell magically seals the target Container preventing any creature from accessing the affected Container. Creatures which were touching the Container at the time of casting may add or remove items from inside the [Safeguard](/docs/glossary/safeguard). This Spell cannot be [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel)led. Characters can never be stored inside of a Safeguard. The Container targeted by this ability must be marked with an "S".

 If this Spell is cast upon a container within a [Sanctuary](/docs/glossary/sanctuary), the Caster may choose to cause the target container to become unmovable. This Spell must be included in the Marshal Notes for the Room.

 Examples of valid containers for this effect are; pouches, bags, chests, bottles, jars and even pockets which have covers. Invalid targets are bowls, cups or pockets which do not have a top.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the Protection of a Safeguard."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Safeguard](/docs/glossary/safeguard)"<br/>
