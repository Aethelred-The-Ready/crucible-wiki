# Annihilation
<b>Cost:</b> P4 D3 S3 + Special<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Magic<br/>
<b>School:</b> [Affliction](/docs/magic/affliction_list)<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Any Spirit<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual instantly slays the target and causes them to receive two(2) additional deaths. The effects of this Ritual are negated if the target receives a [Life](/docs/glossary/life) Spell before Dissipating. This Ritual is always [Unstable](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Unstable), takes one hour to cast and has a 10% Chance to Succeed, a 60% Chance to [Flaw](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Flaw), a 20% Chance to [Fail](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Fail), and a 10% chance to [Backlash](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Backlash). If this Ritual Backlashes, roll on the [Catastrophe](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Catastrophe) table.<br/>
