# Indomitable Fortress
<b>Type:</b> Martial<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Intervention grants the Character [Resistance](/docs/glossary/resistance) to all effects until the end of Combat. This causes the Character to take half-damage from all damaging effects and reduce the duration of all harmful effects with a duration other than Instant to ten(10) seconds. The Player determines which effects are harmful.<br/>
