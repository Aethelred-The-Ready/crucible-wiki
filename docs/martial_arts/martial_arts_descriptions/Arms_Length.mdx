# Arm's Length
<b>School:</b> [Duelist](../duelist_list), [Guardian](../guardian_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 2<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Strike<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 10 Seconds<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Artistry forces the target to take ten(10) steps away from the Caster and prevents them from approaching for its duration.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Physical-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Repel](/docs/glossary/repel)"<br/>
