# Rejuvenate
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell causes the target to begin Rejuvenating. Upon being affected by this Spell the target may immediately spend a sixty(60)-count to benefit from this effect. At the end of this count the target is healed of all harmful effects except those that persist through the target's [Death](/docs/glossary/death). It is up to the target's Player to determine which effects are harmful. This effect ends, without effect, if the target is affected by any damaging effect, uses any Skill, or moves faster than a normal walking pace.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dawn to Rejuvenate you."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Rejuvenate](/docs/glossary/rejuvenate)"<br/>
