# Cure Wounds, Mass
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 5<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Concentration<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell allows the Caster to deliver a Touch effect which causes ten(10) points of [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing) to the target. Any healing above the Character's maximum is lost. The Caster may continue to deliver this Touch effect to any number of creatures but this effect ends if the Caster goes ten(10) seconds without affecting a new target. No target may benefit from this effect more than once from a single casting. This Spell also ends if the Caster casts another Spell or suffers any effect which would Disrupt Spell casting including taking damage or entering a state which would prevent them from casting Spells. The Caster cannot run while Concentrating.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dawn to Cure the Wounds of the Masses."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "10 [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing)" … "10 Magic Healing"<br/>
