# Verdant Strength
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 2<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Touch<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Day<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the target one(1) point of [Strength](/docs/glossary/strength) increasing their Weapon Damage by one(1). This effect does not stack with Strength from any other source. This Spell requires the target to openly wear leaves, wooden armour or some other plant based component as part of their costuming.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dawn to grant you Strength."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Imbue Skill](/docs/glossary/imbue-skill): [Strength](/docs/glossary/strength) 1"<br/>
