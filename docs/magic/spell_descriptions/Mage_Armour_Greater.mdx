# Mage Armour, Greater
<b>School:</b> [Protection](../protection_list), [Transmutation](../transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 6<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Day<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the Caster ten(10) points of [Arcane Armour](/docs/equipment/non-physical-armour). This does not stack with Arcane Armour from any other source. A Character with Arcane Armour must wear one Runes for every ten(10) points of Arcane Armour, or portion thereof, a Character has.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the Protection of Greater Mage Armour."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Imbue Skill](/docs/glossary/imbue-skill): [Arcane Armour](/docs/equipment/non-physical-armour) 10"<br/>
