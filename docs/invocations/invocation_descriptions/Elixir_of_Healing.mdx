# Elixir of Healing
<b>Type:</b> Alchemy<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Charged (5)<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This [Invocation](/docs/invocations) grants the Character five(5) Charges. The Character may spend one(1) Charge to deliver a packet attack causing twenty(20) points of [Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing).<br/>
