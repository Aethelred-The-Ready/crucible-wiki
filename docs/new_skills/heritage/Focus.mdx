# Focus
<b>Requirements:</b> None<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is bought, the Character may ignore Disruption for the casting of a single Spell. Once the Spell is cast, the Character must state "Focus" if they were subject to an effect which normally would have Disrupted the casting. This allows a Character to even use Defensive Skills such as [Resist](/docs/glossary/resist) [Curse](/docs/glossary/curse) or Dodge during the Casting of, or maintain Concentration on, a Spell after taking Hit Point damage. This ability does not allow a Character to cast Spells while Concentrating or Sustaining another Spell. The Character's Spell Casting can still be Disrupted if they are subjected to an effect which causes them to enter a state where they are unable to cast Spells such as Unconsciousness or [Drain](/docs/glossary/curse#drain).<br/>
