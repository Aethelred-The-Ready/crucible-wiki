# Alchemist's Flask
<b>School:</b> [Alchemy](../alchemy_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 2<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> Upon using this Alchemy the Character chooses one of the following damage types; Acid, or Fire. The Character may deliver a packet attack causing ten(10) points of [Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) damage of the chosen type.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "10 [Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) Acid" or "10 Poison Fire"<br/>
