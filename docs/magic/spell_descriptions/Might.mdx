# Might
<b>School:</b> [Transmutation](../transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 2<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Touch<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Day<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the target one(1) point of [Strength](/docs/glossary/strength) increasing their Weapon Damage by one(1). This effect does not stack with Strength from any other source.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the power of Might."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Imbue Skill](/docs/glossary/imbue-skill): [Strength](/docs/glossary/strength) 1"<br/>
