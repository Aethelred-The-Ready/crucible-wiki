# Ogri

The Ogri are, unlike the other Goblinoids, patient and ponderous with great skill at magic. It was they who first negotiated to gain their people's recognition as intelligent civilised creatures rather than mindless brutes.\
Ogri are all capable of using some magic, though this is typically limited. Those who choose to focus on magic excel at it. They often favour defensive magic but some are still capable warriors.

### Costuming Requirements

Light green to blue skin.\
Lower Tusks or Prominent Brow

### Costuming Suggestions

The Ogri tend to favour fine ornamental clothing. Robes, hoods, sashes, and grand capes are not out of place.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Natural Magician:
At the start of each Day, the 6:00 PM [Reset](../../glossary/reset), choose a single [School of Magic](../../skills/magic/schools). Once per Reset, the Character is able to cast a single Spell, below 6th level, from the chosen School. If the Character has the [Natural Magic](../../skills/heritage/natural-magic) Skill, they may cast a second spell, below 6th Level, from the chosen School in place of this Skill's normal effect.
