# Protective Circle
<b>Cost:</b> P3 C2 S1 T2<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Magic<br/>
<b>School:</b> [Protection](/docs/magic/protection_list)<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Circle<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> One(1) Year<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual creates a Protective [Circle](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Circle). Anyone inside this Circle at the time of casting gains an investiture into it allowing them to enter or leave it at will as well allowing others to do so. Anyone inside the Circle is [Immune](/docs/combat/defences#immune) to all effects which originate from outside of it. Creatures who are not Invested are incapable of entering the Circle unless a Character who is has allowed them access.<br/>
