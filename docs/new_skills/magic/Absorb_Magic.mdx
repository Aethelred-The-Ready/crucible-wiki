# Absorb Magic
<b>Requirements:</b> [Arcane Armour](/docs/new_skills/magic/Arcane_Armour.mdx) 1<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> The Character may [Absorb](/docs/glossary/absorb) Spells which they Touch Cast, from memory, on themselves. These Spells, instead of their normal effect, restore an amount of [Arcane Armour](/docs/equipment/non-physical-armour) equal to five(5) times the level of the expended Spell Slot. The Spell's effect must be one which is Delivered as Magic. The call for this use is "&lt;Incantation&gt; &lt;Delivery Verbal&gt;, Absorb".

If the Character has the [Resist](/docs/glossary/resist) [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) Skill, they may also use it to restore their Arcane Armour to full value. The call for this specific use is "Resist, Magic [Refit](/docs/glossary/refit) Arcane Armour".<br/>
