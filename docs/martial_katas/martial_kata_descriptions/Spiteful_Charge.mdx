# Spiteful Charge
<b>School:</b> [Slayer](/docs/martial_arts/martial_arts#school)<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Kata grants the Character three(3) Charges of this ability. The Character may expend one(1) Charge from this ability in order to double the damage caused by any Martial Art delivered as an [&lt;Effect&gt;- Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#strike).

 Alternatively, the Character may expend one(1) Charge from this ability to double the damage caused by any of the following Skills; Critical Strike, Deadly Strike, Penetrating Strike, Precise Strike, Vital Strike.

 Regardless of which effect of this ability is used, once the effect is delivered the Character is affected by an [Arcane](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#arcane) [Fumble](/docs/glossary/curse#fumble) preventing them from using the weapon for ten(10) seconds after delivering the effect, even if they fail to strike any target.<br/>
