# Innate Spellcasting
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 3 [Spell Slot](/docs/new_skills/magic/Spell_Slot.mdx), [Quicken Spell](/docs/new_skills/magic/Quicken_Spell.mdx) 5<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Skill grants the Character the ability to deliver all 1 st , 2 nd , and 3 rd level Spells, the Character casts from memory, without their Incantation. Additionally, they may cast these Spells while unable to speak, such as while under the effects of a Silence, or without a free hand.<br/>
