# Annihilation
<b>Cost:</b> P4 D3 S3 + Special<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Magic<br/>
<b>School:</b> [Affliction](/docs/magic/affliction_list)<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Any Spirit<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual instantly slays the target and causes them to receive two(2) additional deaths. The effects of this Ritual are negated if the target receives a [Life](/docs/glossary/life) Spell before Dissipating. This Ritual is always Unstable, takes one hour to cast and has a 10% Chance to Succeed, a 60% Chance to Flaw, a 20% Chance to Fail, and a 10% chance to Backlash. If this Ritual Backlashes, roll on the Catastrophe table.<br/>
