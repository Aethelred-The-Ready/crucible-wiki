# Skyvorne

The Skyvorne are composed of intellectuals, artists and some of the finest thinkers the world has to offer. Unfortunately their predilections towards academic endeavours have left them somewhat lacking in hands on work. As a result, they tend to separate themselves from people who work for a living.\
Those who can overcome this aversion towards work become world travellers trying to unearth and explain the secrets of the past as well as understand the nature of the world. They are willing to work with others to achieve these goals but have a tendency to think their beliefs and theories are correct.

### Costuming Requirements

Feathered eyebrows and feathers in hair.

### Costuming Suggestions

Skyvorne have a tendency to wear fancy, layered clothing with intricate details in bold colours with metallic accents. Their accessories are equally ostentatious and conspicuous.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Telepathy:
The Character may enter the mind of a willing target allowing the Character to view the target's memories and heal any traumatic memories allowing them to remove most forms of role-playing insanity. The process of healing takes at least five(5) minutes. Alternatively, the Character may expend this ability allowing them to deliver a [Magic](../../glossary/delivery-types) [Inspiration](../../glossary/inspiration) to themselves or by Touch. If this ability is expended in this way it cannot be used, for either effect, until the next [Reset](../../glossary/reset) period.

#### Feather Touch:
Free Skill: [Thrown Weapon Proficiency](../../skills/fighting/thrown-weapon-proficiency)
