# Cure Damage
<b>School:</b> [Alchemy](../alchemy_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 2<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Alchemy causes ten(10) points of [Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing) to the target. Any healing above the Character's maximum is lost.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "10 [Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing)"<br/>
