# Augment Potion
<b>Requirements:</b> [Create Potion](/docs/new_skills/crafting/Create_Potion.mdx) 20<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> The Character may expend one use of a single [Spellshape](/docs/magic/magichapes) or Mutation Skill. The Character may imbue a single [Diagnose](/docs/skills/crafting-skills/potion) with the effects of the chosen Skill. Upon use, the Potion's effect, modified by the imbued Spellshape or Mutation, is triggered.

If the Character has access to a Potion Laboratory, they may target two(2) Potions with this ability.

The name of the imbued Spellshape or Mutation must be included on the Potion's label. No Potion may benefit from more than one Spellshape or Mutation effect.<br/>
