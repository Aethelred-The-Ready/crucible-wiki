# Combat

All persons participating in this game consent to being struck by weapons which have been approved for use in Crucible. Any person who is unable to be struck by weapons due to medical issues may still participate but are limited to a non-combatant role.

While accidental contact may occur, it is not acceptable to engage in non-consensual physical contact of any kind. Whenever possible contact should be done through weapons or packets.

### Non-Combatants
Some people are unable to engage in combat due to medical conditions or being too young. All such people must wear a yellow headband which is visible from all directions. No person shall strike any person with a yellow headband with a weapon for any reason. All that is required to incapacitate such a person is to say “I drop you.” while standing within weapon's reach. Any ability which negates a weapon attack such as a [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Shield](/docs/combat/defences#shield), [Parry](/docs/combat/defences#parry) or [Dodge](/docs/combat/defences#dodge) can be used by the non-combatant to negate a single call of “I drop you.”. Any Character, wielding a weapon or shield, may defend a non-combatant so long as they are within weapon's reach of the non-combatant. The call for defending a non-combatant is simply “I defend you.”.

Once the target has been dropped, they immediately begin [Dying](/docs/combat/states-of-living#dying).

### Delivery Verbals and Ability Calls
Almost all effects in Crucible have a [Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal) or “Call” associated with them. Everything from Spells, to Alchemy, to defensive skills like Resist Poison have a requirement for the Player to say their associated verbals. All Delivery Verbals must be spoken in a clear voice loud enough for their target to hear the effect. This does not mean screaming as loud as possible, merely speaking them so others can hear and recognize the effect the Character is using. All abilities require their full Delivery Verbal to be said prior to swinging any weapon, or throwing any packet. While we all occasionally get excited or make mistakes any Player who is consistently delivering the attack before completing the Delivery Verbal may be subject to disciplinary actions.

### Counted Actions in Combat
Many actions require a certain amount of time to perform. Actions are either silent, such as [Refitting breached Armour](/docs/combat/armour-points) or [Meditating](/docs/glossary/meditate), to actions which must be counted out loud. In general, all counts which are more than ten(10) seconds in length are silent meaning that the Player does not need to count out loud. All three(3)-counts, counts lasting three(3) seconds, must be said out loud in a clear speaking voice. The use of any Skill, or ability, during a Counted Action of any kind interrupts the Action. Abilities which are passive such as [Immunity](/docs/combat/defences#immune), or [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Resistance](/docs/combat/defences#resistance), never interrupt Counted Actions. Additionally, some Counted Actions have additional restrictions which may interrupt the Counted Action. For example, a Character cannot run while Refitting Armour and being struck by any harmful effect to which the Character is not Immune interrupts a [Killing Blow](#killing-blows).

### Attacking with weapons
Crucible uses a light touch combat system where
all an attacker has to do is strike the body of their
target with a weapon. In order to reduce the likelihood
of injury Crucible imposes the following restrictions on
weapon use.

- All weapon swings should fall between 45
and 90 degrees. Excessive force when
striking with a weapon is not allowed.

- Weapons which have been explicitly
approved for thrusting may be used for
thrusting attacks. No other weapon may be
used for thrusting.

- All attacks must be called prior to each
weapon attack. This means that damage
must be called out with every swing and the
Delivery Verbal for weapon delivered effects
must be finished before starting your swing.
Attacks must be announced in a clear voice
loud enough to be heard by the target.

- It is not acceptable to strike an opponent in
the head (from the neck up), the hands (from
the wrist out) or the groin. Any strikes to
these areas do not count.

- Any person intentionally, or repeatedly,
violating these rules will be subject to
disciplinary action ranging from suspension
of weapon skills to being ejected from play.

### Blocking Weapon Attacks
In Crucible a weapon is considered blocked if the
defending Character prevents the attacker's weapon
from coming into contact with their body, usually by
interposing their own weapon or shield between
themselves and the attacker's body. In order to block
an attack the Character must be Proficient with the
Weapon, or Shield, with which they are blocking. If
the Character is under an effect that prevents them
from using Weapons, such as Drain or Web, they are
unable to block Weapons.

- If any part of the attacker's weapon's striking
surface comes into contact with the
defending Character's body the attack is not
blocked.

- Packet attacks, except Archery Packets,
cannot be blocked.

- &lt;Effect&gt;-Strikes, such as Poison-Strikes and
Physical-Strikes, cannot be blocked.

- Weapon Damage which uses the Weapon
Type: Massive cannot be blocked, unless the
Character has a specific ability to do so

### Fighting with Two Weapons
Characters with the [Florentine](/docs/skills/fighting/florentine) or [Two Weapon Fighting](/docs/skills/fighting/two_weapon_fighting) Skills have the ability to fight with two [One-handed Weapons](/docs/equipment/weapons) at the same time. A Character with
Florentine is able to wield, in their off-hand, any Short
Weapon which includes, Short Axe, Short Mace and
Short Sword. A Character with Two Weapon Fighting
is able to wield any One-Handed Weapon in their off-
hand. This ability never allows a Character to wield
two Shields. A Character's Off-hand damage is
determined by their [Weapon Training](/docs/skills/fighting/weapon_training) and their Skills
according to the following table.
Unless explicitly stated otherwise by the individual
ability description, a Character can perform Martial
Arts and other Weapon Delivered attacks using either
their main hand or their off-hand.

|Character Skill|Off-Hand Damage|
|---|---|
|[Florentine](/docs/skills/fighting/florentine)|[Base Weapon Damage](/docs/equipment/weapons) (Cannot be increased)|
|[Two Weapon Fighting](/docs/skills/fighting/two_weapon_fighting)|(Ranks in [Weapon Training](/docs/skills/fighting/weapon_training) - 4 (min zero(0))) + [Base Weapon Damage](/docs/equipment/weapons)|
|[Greater Two Weapon Fighting](/docs/skills/fighting/two_weapon_fighting_greater)|(Ranks in [Weapon Training](/docs/skills/fighting/weapon_training) - 2 (min zero(0))) + [Base Weapon Damage](/docs/equipment/weapons)|
|[Superior Two Weapon Fighting](/docs/skills/fighting/two_weapon_fighting_superior)|(Ranks in [Weapon Training](/docs/skills/fighting/weapon_training)) + [Base Weapon Damage](/docs/equipment/weapons)|

### Fighting with Two-Handed Weapons
Characters fighting with a Two-Handed Weapon
are able to block attacks so long as they have at least
one hand on the weapon. A Character may wield, and
attack with a second weapon if, and only, if they have
the Skill Two-Weapon Fighting. They may block
attacks with the Two-Handed Weapon.

When attacking with a Two-Handed Weapon, the
Character must have both hands on the Weapon at
all times. Additionally, the Character must have both
hands on the weapon in order to use any Skills
requiring the use of the weapon except for Parry and
Counter.

### Killing Blows
A Killing Blow is a three(3)-counted action which
requires the attacker to place a weapon, with which
they are Proficient, against the torso of their target.
The target must be either willing, or in a state where
they are helpless such as; Sleeping, Paralyzed,
Unconscious, under the effects of a Confine or Web,
or otherwise be unable to move on their own. Once
the Killing Blow count has begun it can be interrupted
by the weapon losing contact with the target, being
struck by a harmful effect to which they are not
Immune, or any Character able to reach the attacker,
with weapon or within arm’s reach, saying “I block
you!”. Once the three(3)-count has been finished the
attacker states the [Weapon Type](/docs/equipment/special-materials) of their weapon,
such as; Iron, Silver, or Normal (Steel and
Adamantine). As well as any Damage Type they can
add. If the weapon, or the attacker, has the ability to
deliver its Killing Blows as Magic this must also be
included in the call. If successful, the target dies and
immediately enters their Death Count. The call for
this ability is “Killing Blow One, Killing Blow Two,
Killing Blow Three.”.

A Killing Blow can also be delivered with a packet
with the packet acting as the weapon.

Example Killing Blows:

- “Killing Blow One, Killing Blow Two, Killing Blow
Three. Magic, Silver, Ice”

- “Killing Blow One, Killing Blow Two, Killing Blow
Three. Iron

- “Killing Blow One, Killing Blow Two, Killing Blow
Three. Normal

### Attacking with Packets
Crucible uses packets made of cloth and birdseed
and closed with elastic to represent a variety of
effects ranging from spells to arrow to poisons. Most
ranged abilities make use of packets which are
subject to the following rules. Individual Sites may
have specific rules restricting colours which may be
used such as limiting colours to brown and green.

- Packet strikes on any part of the target from
their body to their immediate possessions,
such as cloaks, count.
- The Delivery Verbal for any packet attack
must be completed before the packet is
thrown.
- Do not use excessive force when throwing
packets.
- Effects which are delivered as Touch require
the user to touch the target with a packet.
- Effects which are delivered using multiple
Packets affect each target struck by one or
more packets. Should the effect be Reflected
by one or more targets, the Character who
delivered the effect may expend a single
applicable Defensive Skill to negate every
Reflected effect regardless of how many
targets Reflected the effect.

### Archery Packets
In order to represent arrows and bolts, Crucible
uses Archery Packets. Attacking with these packets
follow special rules. In order to fire a shot from their
Bow the Character must remove an arrow,
represented by an Archery Packet, from their Quiver.
Then touch the Packet to the hand holding the Bow
and then role-play the drawing of the arrow back
before throwing the Packet. For information on the
requirements for the Physical Representations for
Bows and Quivers, see Chapter 5: Equipment.
- Archery Packets can be blocked using
Weapons and Shields but otherwise strikes
on any part of the target from their body to
their immediate possessions, such as cloaks,
count.
- These packets are considered items and a
Character cannot hold more than one at a
time. This also means that a Character
cannot hold even a single arrow with the
same hand being used to hold their bow.
- The Delivery Verbal for any packet attack
must be completed before the packet is
thrown.
- Do not use excessive force when throwing
packets.

### Being Behind targets
In Crucible, certain abilities can only be used when
a Character is behind their opponent. A Character
cannot lean over or around their target and be
considered behind their target. To be considered
“Behind” a target the Player must meet all of the
following requirements:
- The Character must have both feet behind
their target’s back.
- The Character must, if all obstructions were
removed, be able to see both shoulder
blades. Being able to see only one because
of their target’s body being turned so that
only one shoulder is visible is not sufficient.

Alternatively, some abilities, such as Ambush, allow
a Character to ignore any positional requirements for
a limited amount of time, or for limited purposes as
explicitly stated by the Skill, or ability, description.

As long as the Character meets the above
requirements, they may activate abilities which
require them to be “Behind” their target. If their target
turns, or the Character moves in such a way as they
are no longer “behind” their target, the ability is not
interrupted so long as the Character has already
started the ability’s Delivery Verbal. For example, if a
Character starts their damage call, increased with
Stealth Training, and their opponent turns part way
through they may finish the damage call as well as
the attack, but would not be able to keep benefiting
from increased damage until they are, again, behind
their target.

### End of Combat
Certain effects, such as Aura Spells, or the Encounter Duration, end at the
end of Combat. Combat in Crucible is considered to
have ended when any of the following are true
- The Character goes five(5) minutes without
attacking, or being attacked.
- All enemies have left the site of the combat
and cannot be pursued, or the Character
chooses not to pursue.
- The Character leaves the area via an effect
such as Teleport, Rift, or Gate, unless
explicitly informed otherwise by the
encounter Marshal.
- The Character Dissipates and leaves to seek
Resurrection.
- The encounter Marshal explicitly states
combat has ended 

### Weapon Damage
The most common attack in play is weapon
damage. This attack is delivered by weapon and
packet and follows the following format:

10 (Damage) Normal (Weapon Type) Sleep (Carrier)

In the above case the attack causes ten(10) points
of damage. The Weapon Type is Normal and the
attack includes a Carrier Attack of Sleep.

<b>Damage:</b> Weapon damage is first subtracted from
any Fortifications Points the Character may have. Any
leftover damage is subtracted from any Armour
Points the Character may have and finally any
remaining damage is subtracted from the Character's
Hit Points.

<b>Weapon Type:</b> While “Normal” is the common
Weapon Type some creatures require special
materials to harm. The following are a list of the
Weapon Types:
- Normal: Affects most living creatures.
- Silver: Affects certain transformed creatures
that were once living such as undead and
werewolves.
- Iron: Affects certain magical creatures such
as Fae and Elementals.
- Massive: This special Weapon Type cannot
be blocked by weapons or shields. It will
affect any creature unless explicitly stated
otherwise.

<b>Carrier:</b> This is an effect which affects the target if
the target's Hit Points are reduced. Carrier attacks
are considered to be Physical in nature. If the
Character is under an effect which allows them to
ignore Weapon Damage, such as the Skill Mind Over
Body, they are not Immune to the effects of the
Carrier if they would otherwise be affected by the
Carrier.

All Weapon Damage is considered [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) in nature
and can be defended against by any effect which
negates an effect delivered as Physical.

## Defending Against Attacks

While personal skill helps to avoid many attacks,
there are a number of abilities which can supplement
a Character’s ability to avoid attacks.

### Negating Attacks
Any attack which is defended against by a
Defensive Ability which “Negates” the effect is
completely negated. For example a weapon attack
causing “10 Normal Sleep” which is defended by a
Resist Sleep ability is completely negated, not just
the Sleep effect.
