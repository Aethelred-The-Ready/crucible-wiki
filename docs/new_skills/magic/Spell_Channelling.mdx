# Spell Channelling
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 3 [Spell Slot](/docs/new_skills/magic/Spell_Slot.mdx), [Channel Spell](/docs/new_skills/magic/Channel_Spell.mdx) 5<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Skill grants the Character the ability to deliver all 1st, 2nd, and 3rd level Spells, the Character casts from memory, as an [&lt;Effect&gt;- Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#strike) with their Weapon instead of a Packet. The Delivery Type used to deliver the effect is determined by the Delivery Type of the Spell. For example, if the Spell allows the Character to deliver a [Lesser](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#lesser) [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Pin](/docs/glossary/binding#pin) effect, the call for this ability is "Lesser [Magic-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) Pin". If the effect causes "20 [Piercing](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#piercing) [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) Ice" damage, the Channelled effect is "20 Piercing [Physical-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) Ice". Additionally, the Character does not need a free hand to cast Spells if they are wielding a Weapon with which they are Proficient. They may even Touch Cast Spells with their Weapon by placing the Weapon on the target during the Incantation and Delivery Verbal.

For more information on Channelling Spells see Page 100.<br/>
