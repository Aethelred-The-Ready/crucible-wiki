# Rune Carving
<b>Requirements:</b> [Create Wand](/docs/new_skills/crafting/Create_Wand.mdx), [Create Scroll](/docs/new_skills/crafting/Create_Scroll.mdx) 20<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is bought, the Character may inscribe a Rune, attuned to a single Spell they can cast, upon a willing target or their immediate possessions. The target may charge the Rune by [Absorb](/docs/glossary/absorb)ing a Touch Cast of the attuned Spell. Once the Rune has been Charged in this way, the Character on whom the Rune was drawn may cast the Spell using the following verbal: "Activate: &lt;Delivery Verbal&gt;".

A Rune must be a conspicuous mark between 2 to 5cm in one dimension and 4 to 10cm in the second. Runes last until the end of the Event.<br/>
