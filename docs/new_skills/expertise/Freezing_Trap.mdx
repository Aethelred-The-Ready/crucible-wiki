# Freezing Trap
<b>Requirements:</b> [Freezing Grenade](/docs/new_skills/expertise/Freezing_Grenade.mdx) 2 per Rank<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is bought, the Character may lay down a Freezing Trap, represented by a small blue coloured cloth, or similar marker, with an area of no more than 1m 2 which can be of any shape but may not contain voids. Once the rep has been placed the Character must spend ten(10) seconds to arm the Trap during which time they must keep at least one hand on the Trap. Once activated anyone who steps or touches the Trap is affected by a "Physical Web" effect. A Trap can be disarmed by striking it with a [Break](/docs/glossary/break) or [Destroy](/docs/glossary/destroy) effect or by its creator spending ten(10) seconds touching it in order to disarm it. Disarming a Trap does not trigger its effects. A disarmed Trap is considered Destroyed. A Character may have no more than one(1) Trap, of any type, placed at a time.<br/>
