# Spell Eater
<b>Cost:</b> P4 C2 D2 T2<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Smithing<br/>
<b>School:</b> None<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Iron Mace<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Event<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual imbues the target weapon with the following ability.  Once per [Reset](/docs/glossary/reset), the wielder of this Weapon may use it to [Absorb](/docs/glossary/absorb) a single effect, delivered as [Magic(-Strike)](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic), by which they are struck. Upon Absorbing this effect the Weapon may, until the end of Combat, be used to deliver Killing Blows as Magic. Alternatively the wielder may discharge this effect, losing the ability to deliver Killing Blows as Magic, to grant themselves the effects of a "Magic [Vorpal](/docs/glossary/vorpal) 5". The call for this ability is "Absorbed".<br/>

This Ritual can be cast on a target more than once
