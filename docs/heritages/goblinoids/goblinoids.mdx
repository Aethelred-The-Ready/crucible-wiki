# The Goblinoids

Goblinoids are a group of humanoid who were previously mistaken as monsters but have now become a regular part of society. They are hardy and capable of surviving in the most inhospitable regions of the world. This led to many of them following nomadic lifestyles until eventually stumbling onto the “soft lands” populated by the non-goblinoid peoples.\
Many of these early encounters had the foreign goblinoids viewed as a brutal invading force which was reciprocated by both groups Eventually, small peaceful groups were encountered and the embarrassing War of Ignorance was brought to an end.
