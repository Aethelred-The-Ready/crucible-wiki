# Repel
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list), [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 2<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 10 Seconds<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell forces the target to take ten(10) steps away from the Caster and prevents them from approaching for its duration.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Conjure a Force to repel you."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Repel](/docs/glossary/repel)"<br/>
