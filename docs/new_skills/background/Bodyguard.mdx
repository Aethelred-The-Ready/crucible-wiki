# Bodyguard
<b>Requirements:</b> None<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Skill grants the Character the ability to guard another. The Character may, once per Reset, designate a Character as their charge. A Character may have only one(1) charge at a time but may change it as often as they Rest. Once per Reset, the Character may negate any Weapon Attack, or attack delivered, by Weapon or Packet, as [&lt;Effect&gt;- Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#strike) or [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) by which their charge is struck. The charge must be within Weapon's reach of the Character to be targeted by this effect. The call for this ability is "Parry". If the Character has any uses of the Parry Skill, the Character can use this ability to negate an applicable attack which strikes their Charge so long as they are within their Weapon's reach.<br/>
