# Elemental Bladeshroud
<b>School:</b> [Duelist](/docs/martial_arts/martial_arts#school), [Slayer](/docs/martial_arts/martial_arts#school)<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Kata grants the Character one(1) Charge of this ability. The Character can expend one(1) Charge to deliver an [Elemental-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental) causing one- hundred(100) points of damage of any of the following elements; Chaos, Destruction, Dream, Essence, Fire, Ice, Light, Lightning, Order, Reason, Shadow, or Stone. Once this attack has been delivered against their target, the Character may replace their Weapon Type with the chosen element against the same target. Once activated, this ability lasts until the end of Combat.<br/>
