# Shadow Shot
<b>Requirements:</b> Weapon Proficiency ([Archery](/docs/new_skills/fighting/Archery.mdx) or [Thrown](/docs/new_skills/fighting/Thrown.mdx))<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is bought, the Character may use a [Avoidance Armour](/docs/equipment/weapons) or Archery Packet to deliver any weapon delivered attack, such as a Martial Art, including those which normally require the Character to be behind their target, regardless of their position relative to their target. Additionally, when a Thrown Weapon is used, the Character may throw a second Weapon at the same time.<br/>
