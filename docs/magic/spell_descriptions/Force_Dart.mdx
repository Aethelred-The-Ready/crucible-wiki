# Force Dart
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 1<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell allows the Caster to deliver a packet attack causing five(5) points of [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) Force damage.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Conjure a Force Dart"<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "5 [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) Force"<br/>
