# The Humans

Humans are the most populous and widespread of the peoples. They also have a tendency of slapping labels on things even if those labels turn out to be wrong.\
There have been many people who have said that humans are this way due to their short lives and are stubborn because they don't have the lifespan to go back and do it right the second time. As a result, humans are always moving forward, even if they know what's in store for them is terrible they will keep going, move past it and continue on.
