# Ranger Bond, Survivor
<b>Cost:</b> P3 C2 S3<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Magic, Smithing<br/>
<b>School:</b> [Protection](/docs/magic/protection_list), [Transmutation](/docs/magic/transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Leather Armour<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Event<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual requires the target Suit of Leather Armour to include the front of the torso and at least four other areas. The first time each [Reset](/docs/glossary/reset) the Character Dies, they gain the effects of a "[Contingency](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#contingency) &lt;Death&gt;: [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Life](/docs/glossary/life)" effect.<br/>
