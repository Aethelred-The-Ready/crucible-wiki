# Goblin

The first Goblinoids encountered by small groups of humans, the goblins served as accidental ambassadors of first contact. Unfortunately their curiosity and enthusiasm was mistaken as attempts at thievery, sabotage and caused no end to problems.\
Goblins are a very curious, somewhat reckless group of people who are inventive and clever, but frequently make mistakes or fail to plan ahead.Though this is true in general, there are still many goblins who are careful planners, cunning warriors and even well trained mages.

### Costuming Requirements

Green skin.\
Pointed green ears.

### Costuming Suggestions

Goblins tend to favour black and dark colours. This is done in order to hide stains, powder burns and blood making them seem more careful than they really are.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Tamper:

A Goblin can spend a sixty(60) count tampering with an item in order to deliver a “Physical Break &lt;Item&gt;” against the item by Touch. Alternatively, this ability can be expended in order to deliver a “Physical-Strike Break &lt;Item&gt;” effect. Once expended for the latter effect, the Character must [Rest](../../glossary/rest) in order to regain the use of this ability.
