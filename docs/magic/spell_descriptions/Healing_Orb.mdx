# Healing Orb
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 3<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self, Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Charged (3)<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the Caster three(3) Charges. The Caster may expend one(1) charge to deliver a packet attack causing ten(10) points of [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing).<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dawn to create a Healing Orb."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "10 [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing)"<br/>
