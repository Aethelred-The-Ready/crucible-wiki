# Alchemy

## Casting a Spell
Casting a Spell requires a Character to have an
available [Spell Slot](../skills/magic-skills/spell-slot.mdx) of the level of the spell they wish
to cast, access to the School of Magic to which the
Spell belongs, a free hand, and a packet held by that
hand. Under normal circumstances, a Character must
complete an Incantation to cast a Spell. This
Incantation is a specific phrase which “forms” the
Spell into existence. Once the Incantation is
completed the Character has three(3) seconds to
state the Delivery Verbal of the Spell. Once the
Delivery Verbal has been stated the Character has
three(3) seconds to throw the packet, touch the
target, or otherwise deliver the effect. Both the
Incantation and Delivery Verbal must be made in a
normal speaking voice and loud enough to be heard
by the target and an imaginary observer standing
next to the target. Whispering Delivery Verbals is not
permitted.

 If a Character fumbles an Incantation the Spell fails
but is not expended. The Character can [Meditate](../glossary/meditate) in
order to recover it. Likewise, if the Character fails to
hit any target with the Packet or Weapon Strike, is
Disrupted, the Magic is Resisted, Warded, or the
target is Immune the Character may regain it by
Meditating. This does not mean that it is acceptable
to intentionally fail the Incantation or deliberately miss
a target. It is meant to allow a Player who has tried, in
earnest, to perform the Spell properly but failed, to
recover the wasted Skill.

If a Spell causes an effect with a deferred Delivery
Verbal, such as Healing Orb, is delivered without the
Incantation the Character must state “Magic &lt;Spell
Name>” in order to make it clear they cast the Spell.

## Touch Casting a Spell
Some Spells, and other similar effects, can be
delivered by Touch. This has benefits in that there is
no chance of missing your target and it will not trigger
any protective effects the target may have but it also
allows the target the option to refuse the effect. Any
effect, which is delivered by Touch can be “Refused”
by the target. A target who has Refused a Touch
Casting is never affected by the effect.

An Unconscious Character cannot Refuse a Touch Cast
Spell, though they may accept it allowing it to bypass
any protective effects they may have. When cast on
an unwilling Unconscious Character the target may
trigger protectives as though it were a normal packet
delivered attack. All Spell effects which allow a
Character to deliver an effect by Packet can also be
delivered by Touch.

## Channelling a Spell
Certain effects, such as the Skills Channel Spell
and Spell Channelling, allow a Character to deliver a
Spell as an &lt;Effect&gt;-Strike. The Spell's normal
Delivery Verbal determines the Delivery Verbal of the
&lt;Effect&gt;-Strike. Effects which are delivered as Magic
&lt;Effect&gt; are delivered as Magic-Strike &lt;Effect&gt;, while
effects delivered as Lesser Physical &lt;Effect&gt; are
delivered as Lesser Physical-Strike &lt;Effect&gt;, and so
on.

In order to cast a Spell in this way the Character
must; be wielding a weapon, with which they are
Proficient, complete the Spell's Incantation, unless
another Skill, such as Innate Spellcasting, removes
this requirement, state the Spell's Delivery Verbal,
modified as described in the above section, and strike
their target, or their immediate possessions with a
valid weapon strike with the wielded weapon. Shields
and Weapons cannot block an &lt;Effect&gt;-Strike unless
the target has the explicit ability to do so.

It is not possible to Channel a Spell when you are
in a state where you are unable to wield weapons,
even by Touch Casting. This means that you cannot
Channel Spells while under the effects of a Web,
Fumble, or Cripple &lt;Arm&gt; if you are using a TwoHanded Weapon.

If an Spell grants a Character the ability to deliver a
packet attack, or touch effect, such as the Spells
Element's Fury and Healing Touch, a Character
cannot deliver these effects by Channelling unless
the Spell's description explicitly states otherwise.
## Disrupting a Spell
 A Spell can be Disrupted by causing the Caster to
take Hit Point damage between when they start the
Incantation and when they deliver the effect.
Additionally, causing the Caster to enter a state
where they are unable to cast Spells, such as by
causing them to lose access to Skills, or depriving
them of a free hand such as by some Binding effects
or the Cripple effect.
## Stacking Effects
 Unless explicitly stated otherwise, no more than
one effect with the same name can be stacked, which
is to say, a Character can be affected by only one
instance of any effect at a time. Spells, and similar
effects, with the same name, including effects such
as Mirror Image, Greater Mirror Image and Superior
Mirror Image, cannot be stacked either.


## Spell Descriptions
 The following are the rules for understanding the
Spell Entries. Although the nature of these effects vary, all
entries follow the same template.

### Spell Name [Effect Keyword]
<b>School:</b> Spell's School Name<br/>
<b>Level:</b> Spell Level<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Spell Range<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Spell Duration<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> What the Spell does and any special
rules for using the Spell.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> The Spell's Incantation, if any.<br/>
<b>Delivery Verbal:</b> The Delivery Verbal which must be
spoken before delivering the Spell.<br/>

### Spell Keywords
 Certain Spells have associated keywords.
These are shorthand words that make it easy to
recognise an Spell as belonging to a specific
category or if special rules are associated with the
Spell. These keywords are next to the name of
the Spell being described.

### [Binding](../glossary/binding):
The effect is considered a Binding Effect.
Binding effects are those which restrict the target’s
movements and can be defended against, or
Removed, by abilities which are applicable against
Binding effects or the Delivery Type used to deliver
the Binding effect.

### [Compulsion](../glossary/compulsion):
The effect is considered a
Compulsion Effect. Compulsion effects are those
which affect the target’s mind and can be defended
against, or Removed, by abilities which are applicable
against Compulsion effects or the Delivery Type used
to deliver the Compulsion effect. The Effects
Dominate and Enthral can override weaker
Compulsion effects including; Calm, Shun, and Fear.
In the event of a Character being affected by Enthral
and Dominate the Enthral effect overrides the
Dominate.

### [Curse](../glossary/curse):
The effect is considered a Curse Effect.
Curse effects have a variety of different effects such
as preventing the target from using Skills, causing
them to take more damage, or immobilising them
completely. are those which affect the target’s mind
and can be defended against, or Removed, by
abilities which are applicable against Curse effects or
the Delivery Type used to deliver the Curse effect.


### School
All Spells belong to a particular School. The
Character must have access to the School to which
the Spell belongs in order to perform a
Spell.

Affliction – The Spell’s School of Magic<br/>
Conjuration – The Spell’s School of Magic<br/>
Protection – The Spell’s School of Magic<br/>
Restoration – The Spell’s School of Magic<br/>
Transmutation – The Spell’s School of Magic<br/>

### Level
 The Spell’s level, numbered one(1) through
nine(9). This indicates the Slot which must be
expended in order to cast the Spell.

### Range
Packet – The Spell can be delivered as a
packet attack, by Touch, or on the Caster.

Packet, Multiple – The Spell can be delivered
as a packet attack, by Touch, or on the Caster. When
delivered as a packet attack the Character may throw
any number of packets. Any creature struck by one,
or more, packets is affected by the effect only once.
Unless otherwise stated, the Caster may throw the
packets using both hands, and all packets must be
thrown all at once. If not delivered as Packet, the
effect only affects a single target.

Self – The Spell can only be used on the
Caster, or the Spell affects the Caster in some
way granting them special abilities. Spells with
this Range cannot benefit from the effects of the
Piercing Slime or Piercing Spell Skills. These
Spells follow the rules for Touch casting Spells
except the Caster may only use these Spells on
themselves.

Touch – The Spell can be delivered as a Touch
Effect, or on the Caster. Spells with this Range
cannot benefit from the effects of the [Piercing Spellshape Skills](../skills/magic/piercing-spell).

### Duration
5 Minutes – The Spell lasts until it is Removed,
Dispelled, the Character Dies, or five(5) minutes
pass.

10 Seconds – The Spell lasts until it is
Removed, Dispelled, the Character Dies, or ten(10)
seconds pass.

Charged (#) – This Spell grants the target
multiple Charges of an ability. The Character may
expend charges as described by the Spell. A
Character can expend charges even if they are under
an effect which prevents them from using Skills such
as Drain. The Character must be Conscious to
expend Charges. All Charges are lost if the Character
Dies, the Spell is Removed or Dispelled, or at
the end of the Reset.

Concentration – The Spell lasts until it is
Removed, Dispelled, the Character Dies, the end of
the Day, Reset occurs, or the Character is affected by
any effect which would Disrupt the Skill. If the
Character uses any other Skill while Concentrating,
the Spell ends. Additionally, the Character may
need to follow some additional condition(s) described
by the individual Spell. Finally, the Character
cannot run while Concentrating.

Day – The Spell lasts until it is Removed,
Dispelled, the Character Dies, or the end of the Day.

Indefinite – The Spell lasts until it is Removed,
Dispelled, the Character Dies, or the current Event
ends.

Instant – The Spell is immediate. It cannot be
Removed, or Dispelled, as there is nothing left to
remove. Spells with this Duration are the only
ones which can benefit from the effects of the
Catalyst or Empower Spell Skills.

Sustained – The Spell lasts until it is Removed,
Dispelled, the Character Dies, Reset occurs, or the
end of the Day. If this Spell is granted by a Spell,
the Spell also ends if the Character casts any
other Spell, or enters any State where they are
unable to cast Spells. If this Spell is granted by
a Martial Art, the Spell also ends if the Character
casts any other Martial Art, or enters any State where
they are unable to cast Martial Art.

Timed – These Spells have a fixed duration.<br/>
Either five(5) minutes or ten(10) seconds.

### Effect
 This section describes what the Spell does as
well as any special rules the Caster needs to follow.

### Incantation

 This phrase is the Spell’s
Incantation. Normally Spells must have their
Incantations spoken out loud prior to speaking the
Delivery Verbal. There are a small number of Spells
which do not have Incantations and there are Skills,
such as Quicken Spell and Innate Spell Casting,
which allow a Character to cast Spells without
Incantations.

### Delivery Verbal
 The Delivery Verbal is a phrase which must be
spoken, loud enough for the target to hear, prior to
delivering the Spell. This section is never
optional as it represents the effect the ability has on
the game on a mechanical level
