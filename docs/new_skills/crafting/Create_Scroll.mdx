# Create Scroll
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 1 [Spell Slot](/docs/new_skills/magic/Spell_Slot.mdx)<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is bought, the Character may expend a single Spell Slot to create a [Diagnose](/docs/skills/crafting-skills/scroll) containing a Spell, the Character can cast, with a Range of Touch or Packet, the same level as the expended Spell Slot. Once a Spell has been put into a Scroll, any Character with access to a School of Magic which includes the inscribed Spell may cast the stored Spell, consuming the Scroll. To do so, the user must touch a Packet to the Scroll and speak the Spell's full Incantation and Delivery Verbal. Spells contained within Scroll cannot be modified by Spellshapes unless they were included in the Scroll's creation. Each Scroll is usable only once. When a Spell has multiple effects, such as Blood Bolt, the Character must choose which of the effects are contained within the Scroll. A Scroll must be marked with the Spell it contains, as well as the Incantation and Delivery Verbal, and must be a piece of paper with an area of at least 40cm 2 . Though the physical representation of each Scroll may be reused, each Scroll is usable only once. If the Character has access to a Scroll Laboratory they may instead create two(2) Scrolls for each consumed Spell Slot. All Scrolls expire at the end of the Event.<br/>
