# Energy Sphere
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self and Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Charged (5)<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> As part of casting this Spell the Caster chooses one of the following types; Fire, Ice, Lightning, or Stone. The Caster gains five(5) charges of this ability. The Caster may expend a charge from this ability in order to deliver a packet attack causing ten(10) [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) damage of the chosen type.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Conjure an Energy Sphere"<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "10 [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) &lt;Type&gt;"<br/>
