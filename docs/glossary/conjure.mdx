---
title: "Conjure <Item>"
---

This effect creates an item which is
usable only by the character with this ability. The item
cannot be taken from its possessor, nor can it be
handed off to another. If the item is a [Thrown Weapon](/docs/equipment/weapons),
the item can still be thrown but cannot be used again
until the item is either retrieved, or the Character has
multiple, identical, Physical Representations for the
item. The item must be marked with a thick red band on
the handle and must be mostly, at least 50%, red in
colour.
