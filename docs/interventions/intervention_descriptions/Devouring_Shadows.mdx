# Devouring Shadows
<b>Type:</b> Shadow<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Intervention allows the Character to deliver a packet attack causing two-hundred(200) points of [Arcane](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#arcane) Shadow damage. The Call for this ability is: "200 Arcane Shadow". If the Character misses with this packet attack they may continue to throw packets until they successfully hit any target. Once the Character strikes a target successfully, the Character benefits from the effects of an Arcane [Revive](/docs/glossary/revive).<br/>
