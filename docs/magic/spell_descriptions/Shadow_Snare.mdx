# Shadow Snare
<b>School:</b> [Affliction](../affliction_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 1<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 10 Seconds<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This [Binding](/docs/glossary/binding) Spell prevents the target from moving their right foot from its position at the time they are affected by this effect. The target may pivot on this foot but can neither lift, nor move it.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Conjure a Shadow Snare."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Lesser](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#lesser) [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Pin](/docs/glossary/binding#pin)"<br/>
