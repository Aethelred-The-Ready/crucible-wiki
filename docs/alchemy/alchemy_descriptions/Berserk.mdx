# Berserk
<b>School:</b> [Alchemy](../alchemy_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 9<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 5 Minutes<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Alchemy forces the Character into an insane killing frenzy forcing them to attack everyone around them to the best of their Player's ability using the most effective means at their disposal. This means that the Character's morals or personality have no impact on the means they use to attack those around them.<br/>
