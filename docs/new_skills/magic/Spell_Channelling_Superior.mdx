# Spell Channelling, Superior
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 9 [Spell Slot](/docs/new_skills/magic/Spell_Slot.mdx), Greater [Spell Channelling](/docs/new_skills/magic/Spell_Channelling.mdx)<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Skill grants the Character the ability to deliver all 7 th , 8 th , and 9 th level Spells, the Character casts from memory, as an [&lt;Effect&gt;- Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#strike) with their Weapon instead of a Packet. They do not need a free hand to cast Spells if they are wielding a Weapon with which they are Proficient. The Character may Touch Cast Spells with their Weapon by placing the Weapon on the target during the Incantation and Delivery Verbal.

Additionally, the Character may expend a use of the Channel Spell Skill in order to allow themselves to deliver a single copy of any Spell they have delivered as &lt;Effect&gt;-Strike using this ability if the initial swing failed to strike any target. The call for this copy is "Echo: &lt;Delivery Verbal&gt;".<br/>
