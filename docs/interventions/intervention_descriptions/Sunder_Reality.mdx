# Sunder Reality
<b>Type:</b> Martial<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Intervention allows the Character to deliver an attack causing five-hundred(500) points of [Arcane-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#arcane) [Body](/docs/glossary/body) damage. Upon doing so, the Character takes one-hundred(100) points of Arcane Body damage. The Call for this ability is "500 Arcane- Strike Body". If the Character misses with this attack they suffer one-hundred(100) points of Arcane Body damage but may attempt to use this Intervention again, after Meditating, until they successfully struck a target.<br/>
