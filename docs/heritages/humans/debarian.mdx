# Debarian

The Debarians are a group of humans who revere the dead and are able to speak to them. They were initially thought of as charlatans and frauds but, over time, proved that they were right. Despite this, many still view them with suspicion since most can't see the invisible beings to which they speak.\
Debarians are extremely varied due to their upbringing but the two things they agree on is fair exchange and that blood is more valuable than gold. Though there is a life beyond, it is only in this one where we can discover the happiness and memories that will keep us warm for eternity.

### Costuming Requirements

Speaker Doll (Small doll between ten(10) and thirty(30)cm in length) or Speaker Mask.

### Costuming Suggestions

Debarians wear clothing meant to gather the attention of the Spirits with whom they want to commune. As a result their style of dress often includes bright primary colours although some prefer pure white and others pure black.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Spirit Medium:
All Debarians are able to see and converse with Spirits. If the Character has the ][Seer skill](../../skills/background-skills/seer), their Rank is increased by one(1). Additionally, they do not need to provide an offering for the Beckon Haunting ability of the Seer Skill. The Seer may choose whether their Beckon Haunting summons a benevolent or malevolent Spirit.
