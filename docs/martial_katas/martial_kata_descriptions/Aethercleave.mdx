# Aethercleave
<b>School:</b> [Slayer](/docs/martial_arts/martial_arts#school)<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Kata allows the Caster to perform a "[Physical-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) Sunder [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic)" upon its completion. The Physical-Strike must be performed within ten(10) seconds of the end of this Kata. This Kata does not count against the five(5) Kata limit.

 This effect allows the Character to [Destroy](/docs/glossary/destroy) a magical effect, but it cannot be used against an Item or a Creature. When this Kata is completed, or Interrupted, the Caster is affected by an [Arcane](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#arcane) [Weakness](/docs/glossary/curse#weakness) effect.

 In the event that this ability is used against a Player Character created protective effect, such as a [Sanctuary](/docs/glossary/sanctuary), the Marshal may increase the time needed to perform this Kata to up to one(1) hour.

 The caster must be able to touch the effect during the casting. In the case of targeting a Sanctuary, the caster must be able to touch one of the doorways, or portal, into the targeted Sanctuary.<br/>
