# Elemental Aura
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list), [Transmutation](../transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 4<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Charged<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> The target of this Spell may, at a time of their choosing, trigger it allowing them to use one of the following damage types; Fire, Ice, Lightning, or Stone, for the duration of Combat. Combat is considered to be over when the Character goes one(1) minute without attacking, or being attacked by, an enemy. The chosen damage type is appended to the end of the Character's Weapon Damage call, after the Weapon Type.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the power of an Elemental Aura."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Elemental](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental) [Aura](/docs/glossary/aura)"<br/>
