# Delivery Types

A core component of a [Delivery Verbal](../../glossary/delivery-verbal)

A standard call comes in two forms:\
10 Magic Sleep or\
Magic [Remove](/docs/glossary/remove) Sleep

In both of these cases, the "Delivery type" is <i>Magic</i>. Any defences that work against <i>Magic</i> would be / can be triggered as appropriate, and removing the effect if there is one would require something that can remove Magic, remove anything, remove that condition's group ([Compulsion](./compulsion), [Curse](./curse), [Binding](./binding)) or remove that specific condition.

The standard delivery types are: 
- [Arcane](#arcane) (powerful effects & monsters, bypasses all defences)
- [Elemental](#elemental) (elemental magic & elementals)
- [Magic](#magic) (raw magic)
- [Physical](#physical) (weapons) 
- [Poison](#poison) (alchemy & nature)

[Lesser](#lesser), [Piercing](#piercing), [Sound of Voice](#sound-of-voice), [Area of Effect](#area-of-effect) and [Contingency](#contingency) modify other delivery types, and are always used in conjunction with another one.

[Touch](#touch) is a special non-delivery type that bypasses defences by default, but can be refused

<h3 id="strike">&lt;Delivery Type>-Strike</h3>
An effect which is
delivered as a &lt;Delivery Type>-Strike is one which is
delivered by a weapon. This is a weapon delivered
attack which must strike the target, or their direct
possessions. The attack may not strike areas which
are not legal targets such as the head, hands or
groin. Additionally, any effect delivered as &lt;Delivery
Type>-Strike follows the rules for the specific Delivery
Type. For example, a Magic-Strike follows the rules
under this section as well as those for Magic(-Strike)
&lt;Effect>

<h3 id="arcane">Arcane(-Strike) &lt;Effect></h3>
This effect bypasses any
and all defensive Skills and abilities, including
Immunity.

<h3 id="elemental">Elemental(-Strike) &lt;Effect></h3>
This effect is magical
in nature but composed of elemental energy. Effects,
with a duration other than instant, are removable by a
[Remove](/docs/glossary/remove) Elemental, or [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel) Elemental, or effect
which targets the delivered effect.

<h3 id="magic">Magic(-Strike) &lt;Effect></h3>
This effect is magical in
nature and composed of magical energy. Effects, with
a duration other than instant, are removable by a
[Remove](/docs/glossary/remove) Magic, or [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel) Magic, or effect which
targets the delivered effect.

<h3 id="physical">Physical(-Strike) &lt;Effect></h3>
This effect is mundane
in nature, though its origin may involve magic in its
creation. Effects, with a duration other than instant,
are removable by a [Remove](/docs/glossary/remove) Physical, or [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel)
Physical, or effect which targets the delivered effect.

<h3 id="poison">Poison(-Strike) &lt;Effect></h3>
This effect is mundane in
nature, being created through Alchemy or the
biological processes of certain creatures. Effects, with
a duration other than instant, are removable by a
[Remove](/docs/glossary/remove) Poison, or [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel) Poison, or effect which
targets the delivered effect.

<h3 id="lesser">Lesser &lt;Delivery Verbal></h3>
Effects which are
delivered as Lesser have a duration of ten(10)
seconds. They can be removed by any effect which
would remove the base effect. In the Deliver Verbal,
Lesser is used immediately before the Delivery Type
or, if present, Piercing. For example, “Lesser Piercing
Magic Sleep” or “Lesser Arcane Stasis”.

<h3 id="piercing">Piercing &lt;Delivery Verbal></h3>
Effects which are
delivered as Piercing bypass any &lt;Effect> Shield,
Resist &lt;Effect> or &lt;Effect> Resistance the target
may have. In the Deliver Verbal, Piercing is used
immediately before the Delivery Type. For example,
“10 Piercing Magic Fire” or “Piercing Elemental
Death”.

<h3 id="sound-of-voice">Sound of Voice: &lt;Qualifier> &lt;Delivery Verbal></h3>
This powerful ability affects all targets which meet the
designated Qualifier, if any, with the effect defined in
the Deliver Verbal. All a Character has to do to be
affected by such an effect is be able to hear the
Deliver Verbal. It is absolutely not acceptable to
simply cover your ears or yell loudly to prevent
yourself or others from hearing the effect and
intentionally doing so constitutes a breach of Fair
Play. Since these effects affect an area, they cannot
be avoided by Defensive Abilities which are limited to
a Weapon or Packet delivered effect such as Dodge
but can be defended against by other effects which
are applicable to the Delivery Type or the Effect itself.

<h3 id="area of effect">Area of Effect: &lt;Area> &lt;Delivery Verbal></h3>
This refers to effects which a defined area with visible
boundaries such as a Circle, Room or the Physical
Representation of a Trap. The affected area is
defined in the “Area” portion of the verbal. Since
these effects affect an area, they cannot be avoided
by Defensive Abilities which are limited to a Weapon
or Packet delivered effect such as Parry but can be
defended against by other effects which are
applicable to the Delivery Type or the Effect itself.

<h3 id="contingency">Contingency &lt;Delivery Verbal></h3>
These effects are ones which are automatically triggered when a
particular trigger has been met. Most commonly
these triggers are entering a particular state of living
such as Dead, Dying, or Unconscious but can be any
valid trigger states defined in the source of this ability.\
All effects delivered as Contingency follow all rules
for Touch Casting an effect.

<h3 id="touch">Touch</h3>
Though not a delivery type any effect which
can be delivered by one or more packets can also be
delivered as Touch. This has benefits in that there is
no chance of missing your target and it will not trigger
any protective effects the target may have but it also
allows the target the option to refuse the effect. Any
effect, which is delivered by Touch can be “Refused”
by the target. A target who has Refused a Touch
Casting is never affected by the effect. An
Unconscious Character cannot Refuse a Touch Cast
Effect, though they may accept it allowing it to bypass
any protective effects they may have. When cast on
an unwilling Unconscious Character the target may
trigger protectives as though it were a normal packet
delivered attack.
