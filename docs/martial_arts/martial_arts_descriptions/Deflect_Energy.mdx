# Deflect Energy
<b>School:</b> [Guardian](../guardian_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Indefinite<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> As part of casting this Artistry the Caster chooses one of the following types; Acid, Fire, Ice, Lightning, or Stone. The Caster gains a shield against the next effect by which they are struck which causes the chosen type of damage.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) &lt;Type&gt; [Shield](/docs/glossary/shield)"<br/>
