# Teleport Beacon
<b>Cost:</b> P3 S2 T2<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Magic<br/>
<b>School:</b> [Transmutation](/docs/magic/transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Any Item or Spirit<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Event [Cannot be Extended]<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual creates in the target a [Teleport](/docs/glossary/teleport) Beacon allowing the Primary [Caster](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Caster) to use the Spell Teleport to travel to the location of the target. It does not grant the Caster any special knowledge of the beacon's surroundings. If the holder or target of the beacon casts Teleport, the Caster may immediately cast Teleport to "follow" them to their destination. Except as explicitly stated above, this Ritual does not otherwise modify the Spell Teleport. This Ritual cannot be Extended.<br/>
