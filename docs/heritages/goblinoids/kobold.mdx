# Kobolds

Kobolds are a group of subterranean dwelling creatures who make their homes in the most dangerous areas, near the most dangerous creatures that can be found. As a result, they have no time for many of the cultural hang ups of other peoples being completely egalitarian in society treating anyone as an equal so long as they are willing to help stave off death for another day.

### Costuming Requirements

Reptilian or canine snout.\
Steampunk goggles.

### Costuming Suggestions

Kobolds tend to avoid anything that is not practical in their outfits. Tools, aprons, knives are all good but capes and frills are dangerous.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Light Step:
Kobolds have Trap [Resistance](../../glossary/resistance), reducing the damage from Traps by half, rounded down, and reducing the duration of non-damaging Traps to ten(10) seconds. The call for this ability is “Reduced”.
