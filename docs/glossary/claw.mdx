# Claws/Natural Weapons

This refers to a special type
of Conjured Weapon. These are special Weapons
which, unless explicitly stated otherwise, have a Base
Damage of one(1) and use Weapon Type: Normal. All
such items must use Physical Representations which
are marked with a thick red band on the handle, or are,
at least, 50% red in colour. Claws are always Immune to
[Break](/docs/glossary/break) and [Destroy](/docs/glossary/destroy) &lt;Item&gt;. These weapons are valid
targets for [Ritual Effects](/docs/rituals) which target Weapons, as well
as Ritual Effects which target Claws. If the Claws are
subjected to the Ritual Effect [Transmuting Weapon](/docs/rituals/ritual_descriptions/transmuting_weapon), they
may also be the target of Ritual Effects which target
Weapons made of Special Materials. Claws follow
construction rules for swords.

### FAQ
If your Character has two Claws they will require two Castings of Transmuting Weapon to make both Transmuting, with one casting only one of the claws will have this benifit.
