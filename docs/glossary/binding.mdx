# Bindings

Binding effects are those which restrict the target’s
movements and can be defended against, or
Removed, by abilities which are applicable against
Binding effects or the [Delivery Type](./delivery-types) used to deliver
the Binding effect.

### Bind
This Binding effect forces the target's arms to
their sides and keeps them there throughout its duration.
The creature cannot use any Skill which requires the
use of their arms such as using weapons or casting
spells.

### Confine
This Binding effect immobilises the target
preventing them from moving or using any Skills
while in effect. Additionally, the target cannot be
moved. The target may still speak.

### Pin
This Binding effect prevents the target from
moving their right foot from where it was at the moment
they were affected by the Pin. The target may still pivot
upon the foot but cannot be lifted or moved in any other
way.

### Web
This Binding effect prevents the target from
moving or being moved. The target may still speak
and use Skills which do not require the use of their
arms and hands.
