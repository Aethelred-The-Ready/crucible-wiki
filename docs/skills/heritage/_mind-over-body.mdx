<b>Requirements:</b> None\
Once per [Reset](../../glossary/reset), the Character remains Conscious while Dying. This Skill does not grant the Character the ability to use any Skills while in this state, but allows the Character to move at a normal walking rate ignoring all further damage. This effect ends when the Character is healed to one(1) or more Hit Points or when they enter their Death Count.\
This effect does not grant Immunity to any effects other than Weapon Damage.
