# Special Materials & Item Qualities

## Item Materials
### Steel Item
Weapons made of this material use the
Weapon Type: Normal. Normal weapons are effective
against most enemies. Items made from this material
can be made Mastercraft Items and enhanced in
special ways using Ritual Smithing effects.

### Silver Item
Weapons made of this material use the
Weapon Type: Silver in place of Normal. Silver
weapons are most effective against enemies which
are abominations of nature such as Undead, Oozes
and Shapeshifters. Additionally, items made from this
material are also Mastercraft Items and can be
enhanced in special ways using Ritual Smithing
effects.

### Iron Item
Weapons made of this material use the
Weapon Type: Iron in place of Normal. Iron weapons
are most effective against enemies composed of
magical energy such as Fae and Elementals.
Additionally, items made from this material are also
Mastercraft Items and can be enhanced in special
ways using Ritual Smithing effects.

### Adamantine Item
Weapons made of this material
use the Weapon Type: Normal. Adamantine items are
[Indestructible](../glossary/indestructible) and cannot be [Broken](../glossary/break) or [Destroyed](../glossary/destroy).
Additionally, items made from this material are also
[Mastercraft](#mastercrafted) Items and can be enhanced in special
ways using Ritual Smithing effects.\
<b>Reminder:</b> Adamantine items can be destroyed & broken by [Arcane](../glossary/delivery-types#arcane) Destroy & Break respectively

## Item Qualities
### Mundane Item
This refers to a common item made
of common materials. They cannot be made into
Ritual items. Such items do not require an item tag
and a Character begins each event with all items for
which they have a valid Physical Representation.

### Mastercrafted Item
These items are exceptionally
well made. They have five(5) Slots available for Ritual
Effects. All items made of Special Materials, such as
Adamantine, Iron, and Silver, are always
Mastercrafted Items.\
Many [Profession Skills](../skills/background/profession) allow for the creation of Mastercrafted Items.

### Legendary Item
These Mastercrafted items have
eight(8) slots for Ritual Effects. They can be created
with some Skills but require an investment of one-
hundred(100) gold to produce. They are always
Indestructible.

### Mythical Item
These Mastercrafted items have
ten(10) slots for Ritual Effects. They are always
Indestructible.
