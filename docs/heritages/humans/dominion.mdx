# Dominion

Dominion humans value personal freedoms to an extreme. Centuries ago they were enslaved by a group of powerful mages. Though the people eventually destroyed their old masters they were fearful of the same thing happening again.\
Dominion humans are cooperative with others and believe in community and that the purpose of leaders is to represent and speak for their people. They tend to kill their leaders who speak and act for selfishly.

### Costuming Requirements

Chain worn around the neck or upon the shoulders.

### Costuming Suggestions

Dominion humans, like
Cosmopolitan humans, tend to wear fitted clothing. The Dominion however, tends to have a very military-like style to their clothing with coats and high boots being the norm.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Break Magic:
Upon being affected by an effect delivered as [Magic(-Strike)](../../glossary/deliver-types) the Character may expend this Skill to reduce the duration to ten(10) seconds. This Skill is usable at will, but the Character must spend five(5) minutes [Resting](../../glossary/rest) to recharge this Skill. The call for this ability is "Reduced".
