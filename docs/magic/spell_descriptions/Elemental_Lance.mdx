# Elemental Lance
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 4<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> As part of casting this Spell the Caster chooses one of the following types; Fire, Ice, Lightning, or Stone. This Spell causes twenty(20) points of [Elemental](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental) damage of the chosen type.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Conjure an Elemental Lance."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "20 [Elemental](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental) &lt;Type&gt;"<br/>
