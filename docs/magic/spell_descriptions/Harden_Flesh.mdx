# Harden Flesh
<b>School:</b> [Transmutation](../transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 4<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Touch<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Day<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the target ten(10) points of [Natural Armour](/docs/equipment/non-physical-armour). This effect does not stack with Natural Armour from any other source. A Character under the effects of this Spell cannot benefit from any other type of Armour preventing them from also benefiting from [Arcane Armour](/docs/equipment/non-physical-armour), [Avoidance Armour](/docs/equipment/non-physical-armour), [Physical Armour](/docs/equipment/armour), or any other source of Natural Armour.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I alter this form to grant it Hardened Flesh."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Imbue Skill](/docs/glossary/imbue-skill): [Natural Armour](/docs/equipment/non-physical-armour) 10"<br/>
