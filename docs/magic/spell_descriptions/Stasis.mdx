# Stasis
<b>School:</b> [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 9<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 5 Minutes<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell places the target in a magical stasis. While this Spell is in effect, all effects with a duration, as well any counted actions including, but not limited to, Regeneration, [Dying](/docs/glossary/states-of-living) Count, and Meditation are stopped. This Spell cannot be Removed or Dispelled. Only the Caster can end this effect by touching the target and stating "[Stasis](/docs/glossary/stasis) Down". While under this effect the target is unaware of their surroundings, unable to speak, immune to all effects and they cannot use any Skills or move being utterly immobilised.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the protection of Stasis."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Stasis](/docs/glossary/stasis)"<br/>
