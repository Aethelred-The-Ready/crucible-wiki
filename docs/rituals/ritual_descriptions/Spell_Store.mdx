# Spell Store
<b>Cost:</b> P2 C1 E1<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Magic<br/>
<b>School:</b> All<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Any Item<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Event<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual grants the target item three(3) levels of Spell Store. A Spell Store item allows the holder to [Absorb](/docs/glossary/absorb) Spells, other than those with a range of Self,  which are [Contingency](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#touch) Cast on the Character holding the item. The combined Level of all Spells stored within the item must be equal to, or less, than the number of levels of Spell Store the item possesses. The Character with the [Item](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Item) may use the Item to cast the Stored Spells. Spells which are the target of one or more [Spellshapes](/docs/magic/spellshapes) or Invocations cannot be Stored. The call for Absorbing Spells is "Absorb". The call for casting the stored Spells is "Activate: &lt;Incantation&gt; &lt;Delivery Verbal&gt;".

 Additional castings of this Ritual Effect in the same [Concert](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#concert) increase the number of levels of Spell Store available to the item according to the following table. Castings Spell Store Levels Ritual Slots 1 3 1 3 6 2 6 9 3 10 12 4 15 15 5<br/>

This Ritual can be cast on a target more than once
