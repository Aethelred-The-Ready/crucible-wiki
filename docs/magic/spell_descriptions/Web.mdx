# Web
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 5<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 5 Minutes<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This [Binding](/docs/glossary/binding) Spell forces the victim's arms to their sides and renders them unable to move or be moved. This also prevents the target from using any Skills, requiring the use of their hands or arms, while under its effects. The target may still speak.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Conjure a Force to Web you."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Web](/docs/glossary/binding#web)"<br/>
