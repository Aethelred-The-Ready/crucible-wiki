# Elemental Annihilation
<b>Type:</b> Crafting, Magical<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> When performed as a Magical Intervention this Intervention can only be performed by a Character able to cast Spells from the [Conjuration School of Magic](/docs/magic/spellcasting#school). The Character delivers, by voice, one-hundred(100) [Piercing](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#piercing) [Elemental](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental) damage of any of the following types; Fire, Ice, Lightning, or Stone, to all enemies on the field. Upon delivering this effect, the Character gains a single Charge of this ability. This Charge may be expended in order to deliver, by voice, a second one-hundred(100) Piercing Elemental damage of the same type. The call for this ability is "[Sound of Voice](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#sound-of-voice): All Enemies, 100 Piercing Elemental &lt;Type&gt;!"<br/>
