# Beacon of Hope
<b>Type:</b> Crafting, Magical<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> When performed as a Magical Intervention this Intervention can only be performed by a Character able to cast Spells from the [Restoration School of Magic](/docs/magic/spellcasting#school). The Character delivers, by voice, a "[Arcane](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#arcane) [Revive](/docs/glossary/revive)" to all Adventurer's on the field. The call for this ability is "[Sound of Voice](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#sound-of-voice): All Adventurers, Arcane Revive!".<br/>
