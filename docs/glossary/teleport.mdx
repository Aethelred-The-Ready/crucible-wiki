# Teleport

This effect allows the target to either
return to [Town](/docs/glossary/misc_rules#town), a designated Circle of their choosing,
or to the last location the target cast this Spell. The
target may take any item or items they can carry, but
cannot take any other creature. This spell does not
allow the target to travel between Planes. Upon being
affected by this effect, the target goes Out-of-Game
and walks to the target destination. Immediately,
upon arrival, the Player repeats the Delivery Verbal
and goes In-Game.
