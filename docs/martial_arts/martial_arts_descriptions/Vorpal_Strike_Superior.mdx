# Vorpal Strike, Superior
<b>School:</b> [Slayer](../slayer_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 5<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Indefinite<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Artistry grants the target the ability to cause five(5) additional points of damage with their next five(5) weapon attacks. The target can continue calling this bonus damage until they successfully land a valid strike on their target.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Vorpal](/docs/glossary/vorpal) 5"<br/>
