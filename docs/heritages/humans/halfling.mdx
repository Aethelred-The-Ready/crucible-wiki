# Halfling

There have been many rumours about how Halflings got their name. Some believed they used to live with giants, others believed it was because they were the product of inter-breeding. Whatever the reason, Halflings are a human heritage that value entertainment, good food, good friends and living life to the fullest.\
Many Halflings venture into the world to seek stories, exotic food and occasionally become an accidental legend. As a result they tend to lean more on being well rounded than extremely focused but this is not always the case.

### Costuming Requirements

Choose two: 
- Hairy brows
- Hairy hands
- Hairy feet
- Sideburns

### Costuming Suggestions

Some Halflings prefer to go without shoes, but this style is more seen when they are home or on vacation. Halflings tend to maintain organised collections leading many to have a large number of pouches and containers on their person.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Charming Personality: 
Once per [Reset](../../glossary/reset), A Halfling may spend sixty(60) seconds role-playing with another creature. If they choose to do so, they may deliver, by packet, an [Arcane](../../glossary/delivery-types#arcane) [Charm](../../glossary/compulsion#charm) against the target of this ability. If used, this ability must be used against the target of their role-play.\
Alternatively, the Halfling may use this ability to deliver a packet attack causing a “[Physical](../../glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Shun](../../glossary/compulsion#shun)” effect.\
This ability can be used even if the Halfling is under an effect which prevents the use of Skills. The Character may, once per Reset, [Rest](../../glossary/rest) in order to regain the use of this ability.
