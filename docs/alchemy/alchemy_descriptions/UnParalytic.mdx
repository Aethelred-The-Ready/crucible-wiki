# UnParalytic
<b>School:</b> [Alchemy](../alchemy_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 8<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 5 Minutes or Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Alchemy allows the Caster to deliver either a "[Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) [Paralysis](/docs/glossary/curse#paralysis)" effect or a "Poison [Remove](/docs/glossary/remove) Paralysis" effect.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) [Paralysis](/docs/glossary/curse#paralysis)" or "Poison [Remove](/docs/glossary/remove) Paralysis"<br/>
