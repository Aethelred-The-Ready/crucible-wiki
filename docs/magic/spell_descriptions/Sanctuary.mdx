# Sanctuary
<b>School:</b> [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 9<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Room<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Event<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell requires the Caster to touch a portal into the targeted room and perform a special five(5) minute ceremony in order for the Spell to have any effect. Once this Spell takes effect it magically seals the target Room preventing any creature from entering the affected area. Creatures which were inside at the time of casting may enter or exit the [Sanctuary](/docs/glossary/sanctuary) room at will, and can even "Recognize" others into the area. The Caster may spend five(5) minutes to grant one or more new Creatures the ability to pass in and out of the effect as if they were present at the time of casting. Neither weapon, nor packet attacks, can pass through the Sanctuary and those inside are not affected by any effect originating from outside the Sanctuary. This Spell cannot be Dispelled. The Room targeted by this ability must be marked with an "S" at all doorways into the room. This Spell must be included in the Marshal Notes for the Room.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the Protection of a Sanctuary."<br/>
