# Sanctuary

This effect can only affect a room. Once
in effect, this effect magically seals the target Room
preventing any creature from entering, or exiting, the
affected area. Creatures which were inside at the
time of casting may enter or exit the Sanctuary room
at will, and can even “Recognize” others into the
area. Neither weapon, nor packet attacks, can pass
through the Sanctuary and those inside are not
affected by any effect originating from outside the
Sanctuary. This effect cannot be dispelled. The Room
targeted by this ability must be marked with a stylized
“S” or “W” at all doorways into the room. This effect
must be included in the Marshal Notes for the Room.
