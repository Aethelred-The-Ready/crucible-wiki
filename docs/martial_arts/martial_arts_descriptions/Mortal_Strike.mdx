# Mortal Strike
<b>School:</b> [Duelist](../duelist_list), [Slayer](../slayer_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Strike<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 5 Minutes<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This [Curse](/docs/glossary/curse) Artistry causes the target to take double damage from all sources. Additionally, the Character cannot use any form of the Defensive Ability [Resist](/docs/glossary/resist) &lt;Effect&gt; or &lt;Effect&gt; [Resistance](/docs/glossary/resistance).<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Physical-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Frailty](/docs/glossary/curse#frailty)"<br/>
