# Cantrips
Cantrips are special magical effects which can be
cast by any Character who has access to the
appropriate School of Magic. While there is no limit to
how many times per Reset each Cantrip can be used,
they still follow all rules for Casting a Spell including
Disruption. As a Character gains access to additional
Schools of Magic, they gain access to additional
Cantrips each of which can be cast once and are
recovered when the Character Rests. Cantrips
cannot be recovered by Meditating nor are they a
valid target for effects such as Inspiration or Renew.
