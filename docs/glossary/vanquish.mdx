---
title: "Vanquish <Creature Type>"
---

This effect can
instantly reduce the target to zero(0) Hit Points if it is
the specified creature type. If the target is not the
creature type specified by the effect they are not
harmed by the effect. Though most creatures, of the
specified type, are instantly slain, some are instead
damaged by this effect. The amount of damage
varies from creature to creature. Player Characters
are affected by a “Vanquish Humanoid” effect which
causes them to immediately enter their Dying Count.
