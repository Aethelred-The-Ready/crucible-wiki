# Create Forge of Kings
<b>Cost:</b> P5 C2 E2 S2 T2<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Smithing<br/>
<b>School:</b> None<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Any Circle<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Two(2) Years<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual transforms the target [Circle](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Circle) into a [Protective Circle](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#protective-circle) of one of the following types; Creation: The Power [Component](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Component) cost of any Ritual Smithing cast in this Circle is reduced by one(1) to a minimum of one(1). Honing: Any Character who is invested in the Circle may spend one(1) minute to sharpen a single weapon granting them the effects of the [Invocation](/docs/invocations) Hone Weapon. Knowledge: Anyone invested may [Identify](/docs/invocations/identify) any Smithing effect within the Circle.

 Anyone in the Circle for the casting gains an Investiture into the Circle allowing them to enter or leave at will as well as allowing others to do so. Any creature inside of the Circle is [Immune](/docs/glossary/defences#immune) to all effects which originate from outside it.

 Multiple castings of this Ritual on the same Circle can be done. Each additional casting grants the target Circle one additional functions outlined above and grants the Circle a [Ward](/docs/glossary/Ward) against the next [Break](/docs/glossary/break) or [Destroy](/docs/glossary/destroy) [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) effect by which it is targeted. These Wards are replenished at the end of the Day.

 This Ritual cannot target a Circle under the effects of the Ritual Create Alchemical Fountain, or Create Magic Circle.<br/>

This Ritual can be cast on a target more than once
