#### Pack Tactics: 
Any number of Zoan may form a group using a special ceremony lasting a day and requiring five(5) minutes of role-play. This role-play may be in the form of discussion, a meal, singing competition or any other group activity the members are comfortable with. Once formed, the pack cannot be changed until the next day.\
All members of the pack gain one of the following benefits which must be agreed on by all members;

| Name | Effect |
|-|-|
| <b>Strength in Unity</b> | All members of the pack gain one(1) point of [Strength](../../glossary/strength), increasing the damage done by all weapon attacks by one(1). <br/>This effect does not stack with any other effect which grants Strength |
| <b>Unified Defence</b> | All members of the pack gain five(5) additional [Hit Points](../../glossary/hit-points). <br/>This effect does not stack with any other effect which grants the Hit Points other than the Skills [Toughness](../../skills/fighting/toughness) and [Natural Toughness](../../old-manual-skills/heritage/natural-toughness). |
| <b>Hidden Numbers</b> | All members of the pack gain two(2) points of [Cunning](../../glossary/cunning), increasing the damage done by all weapon attacks from behind their target by two(2). <br/>This effect does not stack with any other effect which grants Cunning. |
| <b>Cooperative Hunt</b> | All members of the pack gain the ability to grant themselves a “[Physical](../../glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Ambush](../../glossary/ambush) 1” effect, or a “Physical [Vorpal](../../glossary/vorpal) 1” effect. <br/>The Character must [Meditate](../../glossary/meditate) in between uses of this ability. |
