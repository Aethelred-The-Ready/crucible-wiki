# Arcane Shroud
<b>School:</b> [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 1<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Concentration and 10 Seconds<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> Upon casting this Spell, the Caster must cross both arms across their chest and can move at no faster than a walking pace. So long as both these requirements are met, the Caster is immune to all Weapon Damage and [&lt;Effect&gt;- Strikes](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#strike). This effect lasts a maximum of ten(10) seconds.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the protection of an Arcane Shroud."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Lesser](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#lesser) [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Aegis](/docs/glossary/aegis)"<br/>
