# Style Master
<b>Requirements:</b> None<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> The Character gains the ability to wield all Shields and the ability to use a single shield in combat. Additionally, they gain the ability to wield any One-Handed Weapon, with which they are proficient, in their off-hand at the same time they wield a Weapon in their main hand. This Weapon's damage is increased by the main hand Character's Weapon Training by an amount equal to the Character's main hand Weapon Training minus four(4). While this means the minimum damage bonus is zero(0), the Character will still cause the Base Damage of the Weapon they are wielding in their off-hand. At the time this Skill is bought, the Character may "sell back" any Skills which are included in its benefit. For information of the requirements for the [Physical Representations](/docs/equipment) for this, and other Weapons, see Chapter 5: Equipment.<br/>
