# Destroy Elemental
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 9<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell instantly Vanquishes most Elementals. A Vanquished creature instantly begins Bleeding Out. Greater Elementals may not be Vanquished but are instead significantly damaged by this Spell.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Conjure a Force to Destroy Elemental."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Vanquish](/docs/glossary/vanquish) [Elemental](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental)"<br/>
