# Dominate
<b>School:</b> [Alchemy](../alchemy_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 8<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 5 minutes<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This [Compulsion](/docs/glossary/compulsion) Alchemy places the target under the complete control of the Caster. They will follow all orders, even self destructive ones. Please note that this Alchemy does not force a Player to engage in any activities they are not comfortable with performing.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) [Dominate](/docs/glossary/compulsion#dominate)"<br/>
