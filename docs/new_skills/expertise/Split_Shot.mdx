# Split Shot
<b>Requirements:</b> One Rank of [Stealth Training](/docs/new_skills/expertise/Stealth_Training.mdx) or [Weapon Training](/docs/new_skills/fighting/Weapon_Training.mdx) per Rank.<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is bought, the Character may throw up to five(5) Archery Packets when using a Bow, or Crossbow, to deliver an attack. Alternatively, they may throw up to three(3) Thrown Weapons to deliver the attack. This ability is usable on both Weapon Damage and can even be used with Martial Arts and other Skills.<br/>
