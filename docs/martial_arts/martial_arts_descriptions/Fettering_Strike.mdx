# Fettering Strike
<b>School:</b> [Duelist](../duelist_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 3<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Strike<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 5 minutes<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This [Curse](/docs/glossary/curse) Artistry prevents the target from running or jumping. The target must have at least one foot on the ground at all times and cannot move faster than a normal walking pace.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Physical-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Fetter](/docs/glossary/curse#fetter)"<br/>
