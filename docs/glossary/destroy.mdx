---
title: "Destroy <Item>"
---
This effect instantly Destroys a
single item specified by the effect rendering it
unusable. Certain creatures, such as Constructs, may
be damaged by this Effect, An item, or effect, which
has been Destroyed cannot be Repaired. A tagged
item which has been Destroyed is lost and the tag
must be turned in at the end of the module, or to
logistics at the player’s earliest convenience. At the
discretion of the Event Logistics Marshals, destroyed
tags may have other policies regarding their disposal.
