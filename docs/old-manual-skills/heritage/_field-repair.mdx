<b>Requirements:</b> None\
The Character may, on a sixty(60)-count, Repair any number of mundane items without the use of a Portable Forge or expending any levels of Smithing. Once this ability is used, the Character cannot use it again until they return to Town to acquire replacement materials and maintain their tools, a process requiring access to a Forge and five(5) minutes.\
If the Character has the [Tinkerer](../../skills/background/merchant#tinkerer) Sub-Skill, they may use this ability an additional time before needing to Rest.
