# Trophy Hunter
<b>Requirements:</b> None<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> The Character gains an additional Rank in the Trophy Hunter Skill.<br/>

<table><tr><th>Cost (SP)</th><th>A</th><th>B</th><th>C</th><th>D</th></tr><tr><td>Trophy Hunter\*</td><td>5</td><td>-</td><td>-</td><td>-</td></tr></table>
*This Skill can be bought more than once

Category: [Background](./background-skills)

A Trophy Hunter is a Character who hunts
monsters, and occasionally people, for a price. The
Character has devoted much of their life to the study
of dangerous creatures whose components are
valuable to the right buyer, as well as rare resources
in areas rife with such creatures. Their training in
these areas mean that they are occasionally sought
out to capture particularly dangerous individuals who
have escaped the guards.

 Trophy Hunters have a Badge which indicates their
Rank allowing for quick proof of their skill. 

## Trophy Hunter Ranks
### Bounty Hunter 
**Prerequisites:** One(1) Rank in Trophy Hunter
**Benefit:** The Character gains access to Bounty
Missions, the details of this mission are provided to
all Trophy Hunters and vary from monster killing to
rescue missions.

 Additionally, the Character is able to harvest
Trophies from creatures and certain areas. These
items can be traded but have no value to anyone
other than the Trophy Hunter's Guild.

 Finally, once per [Reset](/docs/glossary/reset), for each Rank the
Character has in this Skill, the Character may deliver
a [Physical-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Fetter](/docs/glossary/curse#fetter) effect using any Weapon with
which they are Proficient.

### Lead Hunter
**Prerequisites:** Two(2) Ranks in Trophy Hunter
**Benefit:** The Character begins each Event with a
number of Trophies equal to the number of Days of
the Event.

 Additionally, once per [Reset](/docs/glossary/reset), for every two(2)
Ranks the Character has in this Skill, the Character
may deliver a [Physical-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Pin](/docs/glossary/binding#pin) effect using any
Weapon with which they are Proficient.

### Expert Hunter
**Prerequisites:** Three(3) Ranks in Trophy Hunter
**Benefit:** The Character may deputise one(1)
Character as a Trophy Hunters granting them the
ability to harvest Trophies as if they had one(1) Rank
in this Skill.

 Additionally, they will receive a letter containing a
mission which they may complete for additional
Trophies.

 Finally, once per [Reset](/docs/glossary/reset), the Character may deliver
a [Physical-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Confine](/docs/glossary/binding#confine) effect using any Weapon
with which they are Proficient.

### Master Hunter
**Prerequisites:** Five(5) Ranks in Trophy Hunter
**Benefit:** The Character may deputise up to two(2)
other Characters as Trophy Hunters granting them
the ability to harvest Trophies as if they had one(1)
Rank in this Skill.

 Additionally, the Character begins each Event with
a number of additional Trophies equal to the number
of Days of the Event. This stacks with the Trophies
received from Rank two(2) of this Skill for a total of
two(2) Trophies per Day of the Event.

 Finally, the Character may use the abilities granted
by having one(1), two(2), and three(3) Ranks in this
Skill an additional time per [Reset](/docs/glossary/reset), but the Character
must Rest in between uses of these.

Trophies. These are a form of currency represented
by a variety of items described in the Table below. Trophies
may be traded or given to another Character once
they are received by the Trophy Hunter.
