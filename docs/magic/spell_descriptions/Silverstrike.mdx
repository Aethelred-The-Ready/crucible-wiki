# Silverstrike
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell causes thirty(30) points of [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Silver](/docs/equipment/special-materials) damage to the target. Additionally, the Caster may use the Weapon Type: Silver against a single target until the end of combat.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Conjure a Silverstrike."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "30 [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Silver](/docs/equipment/special-materials)"<br/>
