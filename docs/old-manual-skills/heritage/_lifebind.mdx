<b>Requirements:</b> None\
Once per Reset, the Character may deliver a “Magic Tarry” effect by Touch. Alternatively, they may activate this ability on themselves at any time during their Death Count.\
If the Character has Rank 4 of the Seer Skill, this Skill can be expended to increase the time they may be Manifested by five(5) minutes.\
If the Character has Rank 10 of the Seer Skill, they may once per Reset, touch a target and deliver a “Lesser Arcane Imbue Skill: Last Words” allowing the touched target to use this Skill for ten(10) seconds.
