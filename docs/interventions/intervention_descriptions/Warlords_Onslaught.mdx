# Warlord's Onslaught
<b>Type:</b> Martial<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Intervention requires the Character to have access to the following Martial Arts Schools; Duelist, Guardian, Slayer. Upon performing this Intervention the character gains five(5) Charges of this ability. The Character may expend one(1) Charge of this ability to gain one of the following effects;

 Triplet Strike: When performing a Martial Art, the Character may deliver two(2) copies of the Martial Art. These copies include all the same options made when casting the Martial Art as per the [Echo](/docs/glossary/echo) Ability except the Character may Echo the Martial Art Twice instead of only once.

 Worldbreaker's Strike: This ability can only be used on Martial Arts with a Range of Strike. When performing a Martial Art, the Character may quadruple the damage caused by the Martial Art.

 Strike at the Soul: This ability allows the Character to Invoke, against a single target, the ability to ignore all positional requirements for their abilities. This last until the end of Combat.

 Unstoppable Will: This ability instantly restores the Character to full Hit Points, as per the [Revive](/docs/glossary/revive) effect, and removes all harmful effects, as per the [Cleanse](/docs/glossary/cleanse) effect. This ability can be used regardless of any effects on the Character. The call for this ability is "[Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) Revive, Physical Cleanse".

 Cycle of Violence: The Character Reflects a single effect by which they are struck.

 No single Martial Art may be affected by more than once(1) ability at a time. Any Charges remaining at the end of Combat are converted into [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing). At the end of Combat the Character will receive an amount of healing equal to ten(10) per Charge remaining. If the Character is [Dead](/docs/combat/states-of-living), one(1) Charge is expended to instead return them life at zero(0) Hit Points. Any remaining Charges heal as normal. <br/>
