# Phoenix Flame
<b>Type:</b> Crafting, Magical, Martial, Shadow<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Intervention grants the Character the effects of a [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Cleanse](/docs/glossary/cleanse) and a Magic [Revive](/docs/glossary/revive). Additionally, the Character may deliver, by [Contingency](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#touch), a Magic [Life](/docs/glossary/life) at will. No target may be affected by this Touch ability more than once. Finally, this ability grants the Character one(1) Charge. The Character may expend this Charge at any time, even while [Dead](/docs/glossary/states-of-living), to grant themselves another Magic Cleanse and Magic Revive. This effect, and any remaining Charges ends at the end of Combat.<br/>
