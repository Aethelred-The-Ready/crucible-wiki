# Two-Handed Sword
<b>Requirements:</b> None<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> The Character gains the ability to wield a Two-Handed Sword in combat. The Character may block attacks while holding the Weapon with one hand but cannot attack or use the weapon to deliver any Skill until they have both hands on the Weapon. For information of the requirements for the [Physical Representations](/docs/equipment) for this, and other Weapons, see Chapter 5: Equipment.<br/>
