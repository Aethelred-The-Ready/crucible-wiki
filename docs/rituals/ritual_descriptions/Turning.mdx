# Turning
<b>Cost:</b> P3 C2 D2 E1 T1<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Magic, Smithing<br/>
<b>School:</b> [Protection](/docs/magic/protection_list)<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Any Item or Spirit<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Event<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual imbues the target with the following ability. At the time of casting this Ritual the [Caster](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Caster) chooses one of the following effects; Acid, [Binding](/docs/glossary/binding), Charms, [Curse](/docs/glossary/curse), Fire, Ice, Lightning, Break/[Destroy](/docs/glossary/destroy), or Stone. Once per [Reset](/docs/glossary/reset), at the time of the player's choosing, the effect is Reflected back against the attacker. Should the attack be reflected again it is negated by the [Ward](/docs/glossary/Ward) effect. The Call for this ability is "Reflect".<br/>
