# Seal Portal
<b>School:</b> [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 3<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Portal<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Indefinite<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell prevents the target door or window from being opened by anyone but the Caster. Once the Caster opens the target portal this effect ends. The portal targeted by this ability must be marked with an "S".<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the Protection of a Sealed Portal."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Seal](/docs/glossary/seal) Portal"<br/>
