# Two Weapon Fighting
import Skill from '../skill.tsx'

<b>Requirements:</b> [Florentine](../../florentine)\
The Character gains the ability to wield any
One-Handed Weapon, with which they are proficient,
in their off-hand at the same time they wield a
Weapon in their main hand. This Weapon's damage
is increased by the main hand Character's Weapon
Training by an amount equal to the Character's main
hand Weapon Training minus four(4). While this
means the minimum damage bonus is zero(0), the
Character will still cause the Base Damage of the
Weapon they are wielding in their off-hand. A
Character with this Skill would need to have at least
five(5) Ranks in Weapon Training before they will
receive any bonus damage to their off-hand Weapon.
