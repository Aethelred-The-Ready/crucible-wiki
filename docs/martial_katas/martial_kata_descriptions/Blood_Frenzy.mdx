# Blood Frenzy
<b>School:</b> [Slayer](/docs/martial_arts/martial_arts#school)<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Kata grants the Character one(1) Charge of this ability. The Character can expend one(1) Charge to instantly heal to full Hit Points. This can be triggered if the Character is [Dead](/docs/glossary/states-of-living) or below zero(0) Hit Points but the decision to do so must be made immediately. Once this ability has been triggered the Character gains [Resistance](/docs/glossary/resistance) to all Damage until they receive any other healing or Combat ends.<br/>
