# Tranquil Elf

Another heritage of elves, nearly as ancient as the High Elves, the Tranquil are a heritage who believe that enlightenment can be obtained only by mastery of the self. To this end they have detached themselves emotionally from the world.\
Most show little to no emotional reaction and some even feel pain when doing so. They seldom use force preferring gentle discourse but when no other option is available, they can carry out acts of violence with a cold detachment that is both unnerving and devastatingly efficient

### Costuming Requirements

Like all elves, the Tranquil have long Pointed ears.\
They have skin which is completely white, though some decorate their lips or eyes with colour to indicate association, or position.

### Costuming Suggestions

Some Tranquil have metallic coloured skin appearing to have a slightly silverish, brazen or even coppery appearance to their flesh.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Telepathy:
The Character may enter the mind of a willing target allowing the Character to view the target's memories and heal any traumatic memories allowing them to remove most forms of role-playing insanity. The process of healing takes at least five(5) minutes.\
Alternatively, the Character may expend this ability allowing them to deliver a Magic [Inspiration](../../glossary/inspiration) to themselves or another, by [Touch](../../glossary/delivery-types#touch). If this ability is expended in this way it cannot be used, for either effect, until the next [Reset](../../glossary/reset) period.

#### Tranquility:
The Character is [Resistant](../../glossary/resistant) to the effects; [Berserk](../../glossary/berserk) and [Fear](../../glossary/compulsion#fear). This means that they reduce the duration of any Berserk or Fear effect to ten(10) seconds. Effects which are delivered as [Piercing](../../glossasry/piercing) or [Arcane](../../glossary/arcane) bypass this ability.
