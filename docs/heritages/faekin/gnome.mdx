# Gnome

Gnomes are optimistic inventors with big ideas and just need the right tools to bring them to life. They often move from one project to another and always chase after perfection. They are easily distracted by their next big idea and are curious about how things work.\
Gnome adventurers are often out to find the new and unusual. They love puzzles and many things which challenge their minds including figuring out the answer to the one question gnomes ask themselves the most; “How am I gonna get out of this one?”

### Costuming Requirements

Large rounded ears or bulbous nose.

### Costuming Suggestions

Most Gnomes carry a variety of tools, puzzles and other random knick-knacks they collect or find interesting. A common fashion choice is a tall conical hat.
