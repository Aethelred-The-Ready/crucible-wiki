# Poison Reserve
<b>Cost:</b> P2 C1 E1<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Alchemy<br/>
<b>School:</b> None<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Any Item<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Event<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual grants the target item three(3) levels of Alchemy Store of the chosen school. An Alchemy Store item allows the holder to [Absorb](/docs/glossary/absorb) Alchemy which are [Contingency](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#touch) Cast on the Character holding the item. The Character with the [Item](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Item) may use the Item to cast the Stored Alchemy . Alchemy which is the target of one or more Mutations or Invocations cannot be Stored. The call for Absorbing Alchemy is "Absorb". The call for casting the stored Alchemy is "Activate: &lt;Delivery Verbal&gt;".

 Additional castings of this Ritual Effect in the same [Concert](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Concert) increase the number of levels of Alchemy Store available to the item according to the following table. Castings Alchemy Store Levels Ritual Slots 1 3 1 3 6 2 6 9 3 10 12 4 15 15 5<br/>

This Ritual can be cast on a target more than once
