# Healing Touch, Superior
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 9<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self and Touch<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Sustained<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the Caster the ability to deliver, by [Contingency](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#touch), four(4) points of [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing). This ability can be used ten(10) times before the Character must Meditate to regain the ten(10) expended Charges. This Spell ends if the Caster casts any other Spell or enters a state where they are unable to cast Spells.

 This Spell cannot benefit from the effects of any [Spellshape](/docs/magic/spellshapes) or ability which modifies the casting of a single Spell.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dawn to grant me Superior Healing Touch."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "4 [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing)"<br/>
