# Identify
<b>Type:</b> All<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Invocation reveals to the Caster any and all special properties of the target item. Special properties are all Ritual Effects present on the target, as well as all other information on the item's tag. At the Marshal's discretion, this effect may also reveal additional information on the nature of the item.

 This takes an amount of time equal to the amount of time needed for the Marshal to convey the information.<br/>
