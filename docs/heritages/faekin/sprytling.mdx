# Sprytling

Among the Faekin, none are more magical than the Sprytling. These beings are imbued with a love for magic and life that borders on obsession. They love food, music, drinks, people, art and are just happy to be around. As a result, they are often viewed as exhausting by other peoples who don't understand the Sprytling's genuine enthusiasm for the world around them.\
When a Sprytling decides to be an adventurer it is almost always done to meet new people. Every new person is a potential friend and every enemy is also a potential friend. This does not mean they are naive, Sprytlings are fully aware of the danger posed by some creatures. They just have the optimism and positivity to hope for the best

### Costuming Requirements

Sparkles and wings.

### Costuming Suggestions

Being creatures of a capricious nature, Sprytlings tend to dress in a very colourful manner. Many carry objects related to their most recent fixation.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Faerie Dust:
When a Sprytling first enters their [Dying Count](../../glossary/states-of-living#dying), or if they enter their [Death Count](../../glossary/states-of-living#dead) as the result of a Death effect, they may deliver a special packet attack causing one(1) point of Magic Force damage to any target struck by one or more packets. Though the Character may throw any number of packets, all packets must be thrown at the same time and must be thrown within ten(10) seconds of this ability being triggered. Once this ability has been used, the Character must spend [Rest](../../glossary/rest) before they can use it again. The call for this ability is “[Death Throes](../../glossary/death-throes): 1 Magic Force”
