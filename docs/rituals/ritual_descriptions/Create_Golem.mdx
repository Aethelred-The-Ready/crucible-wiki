# Create Golem
<b>Cost:</b> Varies<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Magic, Smithing<br/>
<b>School:</b> [Protection](/docs/magic/protection_list), [Transmutation](/docs/magic/transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Any Spirit<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Event<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual creates a Golem of the type specified by the [Scroll](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#scroll).

 The Golem is bound to perform a single Service and a number of additional Services based on the number of Casters participating in the casting. For every five(5) Casters participating, rounded down, the number of services may be increased by one(1) to a maximum of five(5) total Services.

 As Golems are mindless, they are only able to perform simple Services such as "Defend me,", "Follow me and search every creature I kill." or "Guard this area.". Invalid Services are ones which require Sentience such as "Travel to the town of Glenn and count how many foreign soldiers are there.". The maximum time a Service can take is one(1) hour.<br/>
