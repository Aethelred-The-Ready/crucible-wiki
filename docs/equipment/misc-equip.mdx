# Miscellaneous Items

Any equipment that is not a weapon, armour, or explosive, is categorized here.\
If an item is described in detail elsewhere, the entry here is just a link to that description\
If an item is described here, but also has a link, the link will provide more details.

### Coin
This refers to all forms of coin currency. The
valid coins which exist are silver, gold and
platinum. One(1) platinum is worth ten(10) gold.
One(1) gold is worth ten(10) silver. One(1) silver is
worth ten(10) copper, which does not have a phyysical representation.
Coins cannot be destroyed by any effect.

### Component 
This refers to a special item used in
the creation of [Ritual Effects](../rituals). Components can be
Broken and Destroyed. Components cannot be
Repaired.\
Components are represented by a small bag with a stamp, with a paper tag and an object inside the bag. It is only valid with all three elements present, so keep the bag tied shut. More powerful components are represented by a glass phial with an object inside, with the cork sealed.\ 
There are seven types of Components: Power, Creation, Destruction, Elemental, Spirit, Time, and Void.\
The number of Components needed to
create a Ritual Effect is given in the Ritual Cost
portion of its description, though some may have
additional costs. The Component type Void can be
used in place of any Component type. There are four
rarities of Components; Common, Uncommon, Rare,
and Special. These components have a strength of
one(1), two(2), four(4) for the former three while
Special Components are used for specific Ritual
Effects such as the creation of Legendary Items or
Travel to an exact location on another plane.\
Ritual Components cannot be identified without a Ritual Skill.

### In Game Item
This refers to all Weapons, Armour, Tagged Items, Trinkets, Potions and Scrolls.

### Portable Forge
This refers to a prop which
appears to be a small portable blacksmith's forge. It is
up to the Marshal to determine if a representation
meets the requirements and once approved, is
usable for the Event. The forge must include the
following; an anvil, a forge or crucible, and a hammer.
These are the minimum requirements though a
Player is encouraged to expand on these
requirements and be creative in their design.

### [Potion](../skills/magic/create-potion)
This refers to a specially prepared vial or
bottle which contains an imbibable spell effect with a
Range of Touch or Packet. A potion is represented by
a container with a volume between twenty(20) and
one-hundred(100) millilitres. All Potions must contain
a label with the contained Spell’s name. In order to
use a Potion the Character must, on a three(3)-count
role-play drinking the Potion. A potion can be given to
another Character who is either willing or helpless. In
this case the Character administering the potion must
spend a three(3)-count role-playing feeding the target
the potion. Potions follow all rules for Touch casting.
They can be Broken or Destroyed. A Potion cannot
be Repaired. All Potions expire at the end of the
Event. 

### [Scroll](../skills/magic/create-scroll)
This refers to a specially prepared piece of
paper, parchment or vellum which contains a single
Spell with a Range of Touch or Packet. In order to
cast the spell contained in the Scroll a Character
must be able to read the Scroll, touch a packet to it,
and cast the spell with its full Incantation. A Character
cannot use their own Spellshape Skills on a spell cast
from a Scroll. All scrolls must include the name of the
spell it contains but it is recommended that the
Character who created it also include the School and
Level if they are going to share them with other
Characters. All scrolls must have an area of at least
40cm2. They can be Broken or Destroyed. A Scroll
cannot be Repaired. All Scrolls expire at the end of
the Event. 

### Ritual Item
This refers to any Item which is imbued
with one(1) or more [Ritual Effects](../rituals). All such Items are
automatically [Indestructible](../glossary/indestructible) and [Immune](../glossary/defences) to [Break](../glossary/break)
and [Destroy](../glossary/destroy) Effects. A Character may benefit from
the abilities of no more than five(5) Ritual Items at a
time. In order to use a Ritual Item a Character must
Attune to it. In order to Attune to a Ritual Item the
Character must know all effects of the Item to which
they wish to attune, spend a sixty(60) second Count
to Attune to the item. If a Character is already Attuned
to five(5) Ritual Items they must first spend a
sixty(60) second Count to Unattune to one(1) Ritual
Item first. During a [Rest](../glossary/rest), the Character may
Unattune, or Attune to any number of Ritual items.

### Tagged Item
This refers to all items which have an
Item Tag associated with it. This includes all
Mastercraft items as well as all items with one or
more Ritual Effects.

### Trinket
This refers to Tagged Items which have a Coind value allowing them to be sold to the bank

### &lt;Special Material> Ingot
This item represents a purified material fit to be used in the creation of
special items. The three common Special Materials
are; [Adamantine, Iron and Silver](./special-materials) though rarer types
exist.

### [Cauldron](../skills/crafting/create-cauldron)
This refers to a cauldron of potion created by the skill [Create Cauldron](../skills/crafting/create-cauldron). Anyone can on a three(3)-count drink out of a Cauldron once per reset to gain the benifit written on the Cauldron.

### [Flask](../skills/crafting/create-flask)
Flasks cannot be used by anyone but their creator

### [Wand](../skills/crafting/create-wand)
Wands cannot be used by anyone but their creator

### [Rune](../skills/crafting/rune-carving)
A rune can be carved onto a willing target, allowing them to absorb and cast a specific spell.
