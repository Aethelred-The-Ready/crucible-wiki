---
title: "Ambush <#>"
---
This ability grants a number of charges
of the Ambush effect to the target equal to &lt;#>.\
In order
to use some Skills, a Character must be standing behind
their target and able to see both shoulder blades. This
ability allows a Character to ignore this requirement a
number of times equal to the Character's Charges of
Ambush. This ability also allows a Character to use the
damage bonus from their Stealth Training regardless of
their position around their target. Each time the
Character successfully lands any valid hit on a target, or
uses any other weapon delivered effect, one(1) charge
is consumed. At the end of Combat, all remaining
Charges are lost.
