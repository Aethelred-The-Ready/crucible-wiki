# Pinning Shot
<b>School:</b> [Duelist](../duelist_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 2<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Strike<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 5 Minutes<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This [Binding](/docs/glossary/binding) Artistry can be used only with a Thrown Weapon or the Archery Skill. This Binding Artistry prevents the target from moving their right foot from its position at the time they are affected by this effect. The target may pivot on this foot but can neither lift, nor move it.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Physical-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Pin](/docs/glossary/binding#pin)"<br/>
