### Costuming
The game world is not modern, it is a medieval
fantasy setting. Thus modern features such as
zippers, velcro should be avoided. Modern items
such as printed t-shirts, wheelies, digital watches and
cell phones should also be avoided whenever
possible.

Any devices which are needed for medical reasons
such as eye glasses, walking casts, insulin pumps
and wheelchairs are considered to be justified
anachronisms and not subject to these requirements.

### Prohibited Items
The use of modern technologies for the purposes
of gaining an advantage in the game are not
permitted. Such devices include, but are not limited
to; night vision goggles, calculators, smartphones,
and similar tools. Additionally, real weapons of any
kind are not permitted to be worn or carried by
Players in attendance at the Event. This includes
pyrotechnics and explosives of all kinds..
Modern armour body armour can not be used as a
Physical Representation for Physical Armour in the
game

### Item Tags 
Items generally fall into two categories; tagged or
untagged. Mundane items, such as a common steel
sword or suit of armour, which lack any sort of special
ability beyond being that item do not have tags.
These items may be broken or destroyed while on an
adventure but otherwise do not need to be tracked.\
Potions, Scrolls, and similar items store game effects
and have tags authored by the Character who
created them and expire at the end of the Event.\
Items which have special powers such as magical
weapons or are just valuable items have tags which
describe their special properties as well as their
expiration date. These last items may exist for
several months, or even years. As these items are
part of the game, they can be interacted with, stolen
or sold.\
Coin can be considered an additional type of
game item but it has no tag and serves only as
currency.
