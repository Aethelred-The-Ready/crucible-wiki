#### Gift of Purpose: 
All Shaped have the ability [Drain](/docs/glossary/curse#drain) [Resistance](/docs/combat/defences#resistance). They treat all Drain effects as [Lesser](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#lesser) reducing their duration to ten(10) seconds. The call for this ability is “Reduced”. This ability does not apply to Drain effects delivered as [Piercing](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#piercing) or [Arcane](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#arcane).
