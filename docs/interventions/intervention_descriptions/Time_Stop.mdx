# Time Stop
<b>Type:</b> Crafting, Magical<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> When performed as a Magical Intervention this Intervention can only be performed by a Character able to cast Spells from the [Transmutation School of Magic](/docs/magic/spellcasting#school). The Character may perform an Intervention causing one of the following four(4) effects; "[Sound of Voice](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#sound-of-voice): All Adventurers, [Lesser](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#lesser) [Arcane](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#arcane) [Stasis](/docs/glossary/stasis)", "Sound of Voice: All Enemies, Lesser Arcane Stasis", "Sound of Voice: Lesser Arcane Stasis", or they may deliver a packet attack causing an "Arcane Stasis" effect. The Character performing this Intervention is never affected by this effect unless they target themselves with the "Arcane Stasis" effect.<br/>
