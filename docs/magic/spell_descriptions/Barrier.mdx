# Barrier
<b>School:</b> [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 2<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Indefinite<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants its Caster ten(10) [Fortification Points](/docs/glossary/fortify). These points are always the first lost due to damage. Unless explicitly stated otherwise, Fortification Points from multiple sources do not stack.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the protection of a Barrier."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Fortify](/docs/glossary/fortify) 10"<br/>
