# Energy Shield
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list), [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Indefinite<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> As part of casting this Spell the Caster chooses one of the following types; Fire, Ice, Lightning, or Stone. The target gains a [Shield](/docs/glossary/shield) against the next effect by which they are struck which causes the chosen type.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the protection of an Energy Shield."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) &lt;Type&gt; [Shield](/docs/glossary/shield)"<br/>
