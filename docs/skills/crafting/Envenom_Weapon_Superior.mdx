# Envenom Weapon, Superior
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 9 Alchemy Slot, Greater [Envenom Weapon](/docs/skills/crafting/Envenom_Weapon.mdx)<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Skill grants the Character the ability to deliver all 7th, 8th, and 9th level Alchemy, the Character casts from memory, as a [&lt;Effect&gt;- Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#strike) with their Weapon instead of a Packet. They do not need a free hand to use Alchemy if they are wielding a Weapon with which they are Proficient. They may even [Contingency](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#touch) Cast Alchemy with their Weapon by placing the Weapon on the target during the Delivery Verbal.

Additionally, the Character may expend a use of the Slimy Coating Skill in order to allow themselves to deliver a single copy of any Alchemy they have delivered as &lt;Effect&gt;-Strike using this ability if the initial swing failed to strike any target. The call for this copy is "[Echo](/docs/glossary/echo): &lt;Delivery Verbal&gt;".<br/>
