# Weapon Specialisation
<b>School:</b> All<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Kata increases the Character's Weapon Damage with a single chosen weapon, such as Long Sword, Short Axe, Two-Handed Mace, by two(2). Once the Character has performed this Kata they are unable to use Martial Arts unless they are wielding their chosen weapon. This effect does not stack with any other Kata which increases Weapon Damage. <br/>
