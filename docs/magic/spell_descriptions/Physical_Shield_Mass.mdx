# Physical Shield, Mass
<b>School:</b> [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 5<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self and Touch<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Concentration<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> Once this Spell is cast the Caster may deliver, by Touch, a "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Shield](/docs/glossary/shield)" effect. This effect grants the target a Shield against the next effect delivered as Physical(-Strike) by which they are struck. The Caster may continue to deliver this effect until they go ten(10) seconds without affecting a new target or the Spell is Disrupted. No target can benefit from this effect more than once.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant the Masses the protection of a Physical Shield."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Shield](/docs/glossary/shield) … Magic Physical Shield"<br/>
