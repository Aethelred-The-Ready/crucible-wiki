# Comforts of the Hearth
<b>Type:</b> Crafting<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Intervention targets a Circle. The Circle becomes an Area of Rest allowing Characters to Rest within its borders. Additionally, any Character who enters the Circle may Meditate and [Refit](/docs/glossary/refit) Armour on a ten(10)-Count instead of the normal sixty(60)- Count. This effect continues until the Character activating this Intervention leaves the Circle, or enters a state where they are unable to use Skills such as [Unconscious](/docs/glossary/states-of-living) or [Dead](/docs/glossary/states-of-living).<br/>
