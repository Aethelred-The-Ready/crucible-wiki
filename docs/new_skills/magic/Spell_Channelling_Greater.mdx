# Spell Channelling, Greater
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 6 [Spell Slot](/docs/new_skills/magic/Spell_Slot.mdx), [Spell Channelling](/docs/new_skills/magic/Spell_Channelling.mdx)<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Skill grants the Character the ability to deliver all 4 th , 5 th , and 6 th level Spells, the Character casts from memory, as an [&lt;Effect&gt;- Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#strike) with their Weapon instead of a Packet. They do not need a free hand to cast Spells if they are wielding a Weapon with which they are Proficient. The Character may Touch Cast Spells with their Weapon by placing the Weapon on the target during the Incantation and Delivery Verbal.<br/>
