# Instil Chaos
<b>Cost:</b> P1 C1 E1 S1 T1<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Alchemy, Magic, Smithing<br/>
<b>School:</b> Any<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Any Ritual Concert<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant (See Text)<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual can only target a Ritual [Concert](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Concert) of the appropriate type and must be the first Ritual performed in the Concert. This Ritual allows the Primary [Caster](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Caster) to roll on the Random [Flaw](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Flaw) Table. For each additional Caster after the first, the [Primary Caster](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Primary-Caster) gains one(1) charge of Chaos.

 After each Ritual in the Concert is cast, the Primary Caster rolls on the Flaw Table. They then have the option to expend one(1) or more Chaos to modify the result, increasing it, or decreasing it, by an equal amount. The Primary Caster is able to view the Flaw Table and their expected result.

 This Ritual cannot be cast more than once on a single Concert. Additionally, the Chaos available does not reset after each Ritual Casting. For example, The Ritual Concert begins with ten(10) Chaos. The Primary Caster rolls on the Flaw Table and chooses to change their result by three(3). Their amount of Chaos available for the rest of the Ritual Concert is now seven(7).

 Each Void [Component](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Component) used in the casting of a Ritual Concert targeted by this Ritual increases the amount of Chaos in the Ritual by one(1).<br/>
