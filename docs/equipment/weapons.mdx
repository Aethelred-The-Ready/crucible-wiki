# Weapons

## General Weapon Guidelines
In order to ensure the safety of all Players, no real
weapons are permitted to be used at any time. All
Weapon Physical Representations a Player brings to
the game must be inspected and approved for use by
a Weapon Marshal at each event. Any unapproved
weapon representations must be removed from site
or placed in a designated out of game area such as
under the bed or in a vehicle.

#### Weapon Size
The size of the weapon. One-
Handed weapons can be wielded with one hand. In
order for a Character to wield a weapon they must
have the appropriate Weapon Proficiency. The size of
the weapon also determines the diameter of the core
needed for the weapon.
#### Base Damage
This represents the base damage
caused by the weapon. There are a variety of ways to
increase this damage such as Skills, Rituals and
special abilities.
#### Overall Length
The weapon may not be shorter
than the lower value and cannot exceed the higher
value. The tolerance for this value is one(1)
centimetre.
#### Blade/Head Length
This is the striking surface of
the weapon. All striking surfaces of the weapon must
be covered with closed cell foam measuring a
minimum of fifteen(15) millimetres in thickness from
the core to the surface of the weapon.
#### Non-Striking Length
This is the part of the weapon
which may not be used for striking but may
unintentionally come into contact with another person
while in play. This applies to every part of the weapon
other than the grip and the striking surface. Staves,
Simple Weapons, Bows, and Crossbows are
composed entirely of non-striking length and as such,
can never be used to strike a person. All non-striking
surfaces must be covered with closed cell foam
measuring a minimum of nine(9) millimetres in
thickness.
#### Grip Length
This is the part of the weapon where
the wielder of the weapon grips the weapon. It does
not need to be covered by any foam but should be
covered with some form of grip.

## Weapon Descriptions

### Axe
An Axe is a bladed weapon which is composed
of a grip, shaft, blade, and, occasionally, a
crossguard. The striking surface of an axe is the head
of the axe. An axe may have more than one head but
all heads must meet the following requirements; the
head of the axe must be covered by an additional
twenty-five(25) millimetres of closed cell foam, the
length of the axe head must follow the size
restrictions on the Table above, and the axe must be
rigid enough that it does not fold over when striking,
or if it does, it must return to its original shape
immediately once pressure is removed. All areas of
the weapon other than the head and grip must be
covered by at least ten(10) millimetres of closed cell
foam.

### Mace
A Mace is a blunt weapon which is composed
of a grip, shaft, head, and, occasionally, a
crossguard. The striking surface of a mace is the
head of the mace. A mace may have more than one
head but all heads must meet the following
requirements; the head of the mace must be covered
by an additional twenty-five(25) millimetres of closed
cell foam, the length of the mace head must follow
the size restrictions on the Table 5.3, and the mace
must be rigid enough that it does not fold over when
striking, or if it does, it must return to its original
shape immediately once pressure is removed. All
areas of the weapon other than the head and grip
must be covered by at least ten(10) millimetres of
closed cell foam.

### Sword
A Sword is a weapon which is composed of
a grip, a blade, and, usually, a crossguard. The
striking surface of a sword is the length of the

### Bow
A bow is a weapon which allows the Character
to deliver a packet based weapon attack. Bows can
never be used to strike another Player but can be
used to block attacks normally. Bows must be curved
into the shape of a bent bow. Bows may include a
string for carrying but bows of any draw weight may
not be used. Even bows designed for LARPs may not
be used. All bows also include a quiver which holds
its ammunition and is necessary to use archery. The
Bow and Quiver are considered the same weapon for
the purposes of Ritual Effects and Special Materials
such as Iron.

### Crossbow
A crossbow is a weapon which allows
the Character to deliver a packet based weapon
attack. Crossbows can never be used to strike
another Player but can be used to block attacks
normally. Crossbows must be made into the shape of
a crossbow with a stock with two or more arms
extending perpendicularly from the stock. Crossbows
may include a string for carrying but crossbows of
any draw weight may not be used. Even crossbows
designed for LARPs may not be used. All crossbows
also include a quiver which holds its ammunition. The
Crossbow and Quiver are considered the same
weapon for the purposes of Ritual Effects and Special
Materials such as Iron.

### Quiver
A quiver is a special container which holds
arrows or bolts for use with a bow or crossbow
respectively. A quiver is considered to have unlimited
ammunition but a Character must have one in order
to use archery attacks. A quiver and bow are
considered to be the same weapon for the purposes
of Ritual Effects and items made from special
mat

### Thrown Weapon
A thrown weapon is one which is
composed entirely out of closed cell foam. Thrown
weapons may not contain a rigid core of any kind.
Finally, a thrown weapon may not be used in combat
except by throwing. This means that they cannot be
used to block attacks, attack in melee or even be
used to deliver a Killing Blow.

### Staff
A Staff is a special weapon which does not
require any Proficiency to wield. A Staff can be used
only for blocking and can never be used to deliver
any attack or effect. The entire length of the weapon
is considered a non-striking surface

### Simple Weapon
A Simple Weapon is any weapon that fits in the size limits.\
A Simple Weapon can only be used to block and can never be used to
attack but can be used to deliver a Killing Blow.
