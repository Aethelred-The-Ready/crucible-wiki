# Two Weapon Fighting, Greater
<b>Requirements:</b> [Two Weapon Fighting](/docs/new_skills/fighting/Two_Weapon_Fighting.mdx)<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> The Character's damage with their off-hand Weapon is increased to an amount equal to the Character's main hand Weapon Training minus two(2). While this means the minimum damage bonus is zero(0), the Character may still cause the Base Damage of the Weapon. A Character with this Skill would need to have at least three(3) Ranks in Weapon Training before they will receive any bonus damage to their off-hand Weapon.<br/>
