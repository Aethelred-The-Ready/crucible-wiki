# Narakh

The Narahk are a group of exceptionally skilled warriors and peacekeepers. They value discipline, law and order but also understand that mistakes can happen and as long as you learn from them, you can earn forgiveness and redemption.\
Narahk often become adventurers as a way of satisfying a primal urge to fight and protect others with them often finding employment as mercenaries so long as the battle is honourable.

### Costuming Requirements

Large Horns and rune(s)on face.\
Runes may also be found on limbs.

### Costuming Suggestions

Narahk usually wear their armour at all times. Even their spell casters still wear armour sections. They tend to decorate their armour with various runes which match those on their flesh.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Runic Flesh:
Whenever a Narahk is healed by an effect which would put them above their maximum [Hit Point](../../glossary/hit-point) total they may choose to have any remaining healing restore a number of missing [Armour Points](../../glossary/armour-point) equal to the excess Healing, up to the Character's full Armour Point Total. The Character may use this ability to restore a number of Armour Points equal to their maximum [Wear Points](../../glossary/wear-point) before they must [Rest](../../glossary/rest)in order to use this ability again.\
For example, If a Narahk, with a Wear Points total of thirty(30), was missing five(5) Hit Points and received twenty(20) points of Healing they would recover their five(5) missing Hit Points, then the remaining fifteen(15) would be applied towards their Armour Point total. If the Character was wearing a Physical Armour suit which was missing eight(8) Armour Points it would recover the missing Armour Points and the remaining seven(7) points of Healing would be lost. The Character could still restore twenty-two(22) Armour Point from this ability before needing to Rest.
