# Spell Conversion
<b>School:</b> [Transmutation](../transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 0<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Cantrip allows the Caster to expend a single Spell Slot in order to regain a single Spell Slot of any level lower than the one expended for this Cantrip. As a Cantrip, this effect can be used once, afterwards the Character must Rest in order to regain its use.

 If the Character has the Skill [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic)al Tradition &lt;Sorcery&gt;, they instead regain the ability to cast one(1) of their expended Cantrips as though they had Rested, in place of this Cantrip's normal effects.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> None<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) Spell [Conversion](/docs/glossary/conversion)"<br/>
