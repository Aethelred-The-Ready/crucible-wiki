# Tarry
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 5<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell adds five(5) minutes to the target's [Death](/docs/glossary/death) Count up to a maximum of ten(10) minutes.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dawn to grant you Tarrying."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Tarry](/docs/glossary/tarry)"<br/>
