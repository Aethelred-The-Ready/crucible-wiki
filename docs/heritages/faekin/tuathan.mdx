# Tuathan

Standing in contrast to the other Faekin, the Tuathan are very serious. They are great sages and warriors with each working to be the best in their particular field. Almost every Tuathan has something they have mastered to nearly, and sometimes literally, a supernatural extent.\
Tuathan adventurers always have something to prove. it's usually that they are number one at whatever it is they do. Those who aren't the best will at least try their best and do whatever it takes to improve and leave their peers behind. That being said, they recognize that while they might be good at one thing, they aren't the best at everything and cooperation is sometimes the only way to succeed.

### Costuming Requirements

Third eye on forehead.\
Glittery or ornately detailed makeup.

### Costuming Suggestions

The Tuathan tend to extensively favour the colours blue, green, and yellow in their clothing. Most often in the form of silks and shiny clothing.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Fragment of Light: 
Once per [Reset](../../glossary/reset), a Tuathan may deliver a single effect, which is normally delivered as Physical(-Strike), as Magic(-Strike) instead of its normal Delivery Type. If this effect causes Weapon Type damage, the Damage Type changes to “Force”. For example, a Tuathan can deliver an attack causing “50 Physical-Strike Iron” damage as “50 Magic Force” or “50 Magic-Strike Force”. This ability can be recharged by the Tuathan spending five(5) minutes Resting.
