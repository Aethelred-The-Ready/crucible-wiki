# States of Living

### Conscious
The normal state of a Character, with one(1) or
more Hit Points is Conscious. The Character is aware
of their surroundings.

### Unconscious
A Character is Unconscious if they are at zero(0)
Hit Points. An Unconscious Character is helpless and
unaware of their surroundings. After one(1) minute,
the Character regains one(1) Hit Point regaining
Consciousness. An Unconscious Character cannot
use any Skills, or [Defensive Abilities](/docs/combat/defences) which are
considered Choice Defences such as [Ward](/docs/combat/defences#ward), [Dodge](/docs/combat/defences#dodge)
or [Resist](/docs/combat/defences#resist).

### Dying (Bleeding Out)
A Character is Dying if they
are reduced to below zero(0) Hit Points. A Dying
Character is at -1 Hit Points. Excess damage is not
tracked. A Dying Character is helpless and unaware
of their surroundings. As soon as a Character begins
Dying they begin a sixty(60) second Bleed Out Count.
At the end of this count the Character becomes
Dead. A Dying Character cannot use any Skills, or
Defensive Abilities which are considered Choice
Defences such as Ward, Dodge or Resist.

A Dying Character can be Stabilised by the Skill
[First Aid](/docs/skills/background/first_aid), or by a [Stabilise](/docs/glossary/stabilise) effect. Once Stabilised the
character is at zero(0) Hit Points and becomes
Unconscious. If a Dying Character receives any
amount of Healing they are no longer Dying and
Conscious.

For example, Ills has 4 Hit Points when they are
struck by a spell causing 10 points of damage. Ills is
now Dying and after one minute will be Dead. But Ills
receives 10 points of Healing after a few seconds. Ills
is now at 9 Hit Points and able to act normally.

### Dead
A Character which is Dead is helpless and
unaware of their surroundings. As soon as a
Character becomes Dead they begin a five(5) minute
Death Count. If the Character’s Death Count ends the
Character immediately dissipates becoming a Spirit.
Once a Character has become a Spirit they must
leave any Game Items at the location of their body,
put on a red headband and walk to a Circle of
Resurrection. The Player chooses which Circle the
Character travels to. A Dead Character cannot use
any Skills, or Defensive Abilities which are considered
Choice Defences such as Ward, Dodge or Resist.

### Dissipated
A Player whose Character has dissipated and
become a spirit must report to Plot so their death may
be recorded. Additionally, if the Character's death
adds a Death to their Character, the Player must
draw from the Bag of Death. This bag contains
ten(10) white stones. For each time the Character
has Resurrected, after the first, one of the white
stones will be replaced with a black stone. If the
Player draws a white stone the Character resurrects
as normal. If however the Player draws a black stone
the Character suffers one of the fates outlined in the below table

|Fate Name|Effect|
|---|---|
|Soulflay|The Character returns to life and their number of Character Deaths is reduced to zero(0). However, the Character is reduced to 70% of their previous experience point total.|
|Reaper's Grasp|The Character returns to life but a looming fate awaits them. The Character may be destined to die in an imminent battle, or they may have some unfinished business. Either way, at the end of the next Event the Character attends the character is considered to be Permanently Dead. After this time, the Player may create a new Character with 75% of the Permanently Dead Character's Experience|
|Insurmountable Trauma|The Character returns to life but retires from adventuring. At the end of the event the Character is considered to be Permanently Dead. After this time, the Player may create a new Character with 80% of the Permanently Dead Character’s Experience.|
|Permanent Death|The Character returns to life but retires from adventuring. At the end of the event the Character is considered to be Permanently Dead. After this time, the Player may create a new Character with 80% of the Permanently Dead Character’s Experience.|

### Resurrections
A Character who has Dissipated, had their Death
recorded by Plot, and approved for Resurrection may,
as a Spirit, travel to a Circle of Resurrection. Any
Character, who is Invested in the Circle of
Resurrection, may perform a Resurrection.
Resurrections are a special five(5)-minute counted
action during which the Character may perform a
Guided Resurrection where they role-play guiding a
Spirit back to life. Alternatively, a Character may
perform an Unguided Resurrection, relying instead on
the Circle to bear the load of the resurrection. Once
an unguided resurrection has begun, it continues on
its own and the Character who started the
Resurrection may begin another one. This process is
physically, and mentally, draining on the spirit,
bordering on traumatic. Though there are no in-game
effects for either method, the resurrected character is
free to role-play as they deem appropriate.

If the Spirit is Invested in the Circle of Resurrection
they may force the Circle to resurrect them without
another character’s assistance. This process is every
bit as draining as an Unguided Resurrection.

Once the five(5) minute Resurrection count has
finished the Character returns to life, their body
reforming over a special three(3)-count. During this
three(3)-count the Character is Invulnerable to all
effects. A newly Resurrected Character regains all
expended uses of all per Reset and per Day Skills. A
Resurrected Character always has their full Hit
Points.

### Carrying a Character
Most Characters can be carried by another but
doing so is difficult. In order to role-play this a
Character must have both hands free, and cannot be
holding anything with either arm. They state to their
target, who is usually helpless, “I am carrying you.”.
The target must then stand in front of the Character
“Carrying them”. The Character doing the carrying
places both hands above the target’s shoulders and
both Characters walk together. It is never possible to
run while carrying a Character.

Once one Character is carrying another, should
either Character be struck by any effect delivered by
weapon or packet, even if it is negated, the carried
Character is “dropped” and the carrying Character
must wait ten(10) seconds before attempting to carry
them again. Effects which strike a Character carrying
or being carried only applies to whoever was struck.

It is possible for two Characters to carry the same
target, in this case each Character needs to have
only one free hand each, with each carrying
Character holding one hand above the target.

Some creatures cannot be carried by Player
Characters.
