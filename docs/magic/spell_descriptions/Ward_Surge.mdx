# Ward Surge
<b>School:</b> [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 3<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 10 Seconds<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the Caster a [Ward](/docs/glossary/Ward) against the next effect by which they are struck. This Ward is lost after ten(10) seconds.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> None<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Lesser](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#lesser) [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) Perfect [Ward](/docs/glossary/Ward)"<br/>
