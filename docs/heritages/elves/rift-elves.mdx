# Rift Elf

Claiming to come from worlds beyond our own, the Rift Elves are a mysterious group that just showed up without warning. They possess powerful control over elemental energy and magical forces

### Costuming Requirements

Like all elves, the Rift Elves have long Pointed ears.\
They have a single rune or gem set somewhere upon their forehead.

### Costuming Suggestions

Rift Elves tend to favour stars, moons and other celestial objects in their clothing and accessories.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Elemental Power:
Once per [Reset](../../glossary/reset), a Rift Elf may attune themselves to any of the following Damage Types; Chaos, Destruction, Dream, Essence, Fire, Ice, Light, Lightning, Order, Reason, Shadow, or Stone. The Character may Invoke this ability to use the chosen Damage Type, for all of their Weapon Damage Calls, against a single opponent at will until the end of Combat.\
Additionally, while using the above ability, the Character may replace the Damage Type of any spells, which normally allows the Character to choose from the following list; Fire, Ice, Lightning, Stone, with the chosen Damage Type against the target of this ability.\
Alternatively, they may expend this ability to negate a single effect causing any of the above Damage Types as per the [Ward](../../glossary/ward) ability. When used in this form the Call is “Ward”.
A Character may, once per Reset, recover the use of this ability by [Resting](../../glossary/rest) allowing it to be used, at most, twice per Reset.
