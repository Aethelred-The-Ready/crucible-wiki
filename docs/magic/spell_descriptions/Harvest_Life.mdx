# Harvest Life
<b>School:</b> [Affliction](../affliction_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 1<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell must be cast immediately before a Killing Blow. The Killing Blow must be started within ten(10) seconds of the casting of this Spell. Once the Caster has delivered a successful Killing Blow they recover ten(10) Hit Points. Any excess healing from this Spell is converted to Fortification Points. Multiple effects which grant Fortification Points do not stack.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the power to Harvest Life"<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "10 [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing)"<br/>
