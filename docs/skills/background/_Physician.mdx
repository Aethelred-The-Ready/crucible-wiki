After having mastered the art of Diagnose and 
First Aid, a character is ready to uncover the secrets 
of the living body. This skill gives a character a much 
broader depth understanding of the workings of First 
Aid and emergency surgery. The Physician skill 
grants its users a number of abilities based on the 
level of Physician they possess. In order to perform 
surgery,the Physician must carry with them a doctor's
bag, minimum volume of four(4) litres, and medical 
tools of the trade. These tools do not require a tag.	

	Characters can normally only use First Aid, and the
effects of this Skill on Humanoids. But if they have as 
Skill which allows them to use First Aid on non-
Humanoid creatures they are able to use the abilities 
of this Skill on those creatures as well. For example, 
if they have Academic (Necrology), they can use their
skills on Undead.

## Physician Sub-Skill Descriptions
	In the following descriptions of the Physician Sub-
Skills, the following terms are in use; "Patient" refers 
to the Character which is the target or beneficiary of 
the Physician's skills. "Physician" refers to the 
Character using the Skill.

### Field Medic [Physician]
**Prerequisites:** 1 Rank in Physician\
**Benefit:** A Character with this Skill may perform First 
Aid with greater versatility and efficiency than those 
without it. Firstly, the Character is able to perform 
First Aid on a target that is being carried. Secondly, 
their First Aid count is not interrupted by Hit Point 
Damage. Thirdly, they may perform First Aid on 
two(2) targets simultaneously. Finally, when the 
Character completes their First Aid count they may 
also deliver two(2) points of Physical Healing to all 
Characters they are performing First Aid upon. The 
call for this effect is "First Aid Complete. 2 Physical 
Healing".

### Plague Doctor [Physician]
**Prerequisites:** 2 Ranks in Physician\
**Benefit:** A Character with this Skill is able to identify 
most non-magical diseases and cure the target of 
them. Using this ability requires a one(1) minute 
ceremony and requires the Patient to lie down and 
remain still for its duration. During this ceremony the 
Physician is required to role-play an examination and 
the administration of medicine to the target. This 
process is interrupted if either the Physician is 
interrupted or either Character takes Hit Point 
damage. Additionally, this ability allows the Character 
to cure the target of many common afflictions. In 
addition to the previously mentioned abilities, once 
per Day the Character may deliver a "Physical Dispel
Curse" effect. 

### Physicker [Physician]
**Prerequisites:** 3 Ranks in Physician\
**Benefit:** A Character with this Skill may identify any 
Poisons or mundane toxins present in the target and 
flush them from the target's body. Using this ability 
requires a one(1) minute examination and requires 
the Patient to lie down and remain still for its duration.
During this ceremony the Physician is required to 
role-play an examination and the administration of 
medicine to the target. This process is interrupted if 
either the Physician is interrupted or either Character 
takes Hit Point damage. This Skill can be used on a 
Patient no more than once per Day. The call for this 
ability is "Physical Dispel Poison".

	Additionally, the Character is able to perform a 
special form of First Aid, called Field Dressing, on 
targets that are not Dying. The Physician may spend 
one(1) minute to heal the target a number of missing 
Hit Points equal to their Ranks in Physician. Once a 
target has benefited from this effect they must wear 
an armband, supplied by the Physician, made of 
gauze. The Patient cannot benefit from this ability 
until they spend five(5) minutes Resting at which time
they may remove the armband and return it to the 
Physician.

	Once a Character has three(3) Ranks in this Skill, 
they gain the ability to create a special Curative 
Tincture. Once per Reset, the Character may deliver 
a Touch effect causing five(5) points of Physical 
Healing to the target. This Character is able to use 
this ability once for every three(3) Ranks the 
Character has in the Physician Skill. The Character 
may Rest in order to regain all expended uses of this 
ability. The maximum number of times per Reset the 
Character can use this ability is equal to the number 
of Ranks they have in the Physician Skill. For 
example, a Character with six(6) Ranks in Physician 
may deliver the effects of the Curative Tincture twice 
before needing to Rest to recover the expended 
uses, allowing them to use this ability a maximum of 
six(6) times per Reset.

### Surgeon, Novice [Physician]
**Prerequisites:** 4 Ranks in Physician\
**Benefit:** A Character with this Skill may perform 
Surgery on Patients. This Surgery has two primary 
uses, the first is to repair injury. This allows the 
Character to heal a number of Hit Points equal to 
twice the Character's Ranks in the Skill Physician. 
The call for this ability is "# Physical Healing". The 
second use of this ability is to heal mundane injuries 
such as the removal of foreign objects and the setting
of broken bones. Using either of these abilities 
requires a five(5) minute ceremony and requires the 
Patient to lie down and either be Unconscious, 
Sleeping, or Intoxicated for its duration. During this 
ceremony the Physician is required to role-play an 
examination and the administration of medicine to the
target. This process is interrupted if either the 
Physician is interrupted or either Character takes Hit 
Point damage. Once the Surgery is complete, the 
target is affected by a "Piercing Physical Drain" effect.
A Character may benefit from Surgery no more than 
once per Day with any attempts after the first ending 
instead as a "Physical Death" in place of its normal 
effect(s).

	Additionally, the Character may, as a sixty(60)-
count, grant a Dead Character the effects of a 
"Physical Tarry". Neither Character can be moved 
during this count.

	Finally, the number of targets the Physician may 
affect with First Aid, or Field Dressing, simultaneously
is increased to three(3).

### Doctor [Physician]
**Prerequisites:** 5 Ranks in Physician\
**Benefit:** A Character with this Skill is able to perform 
Surgery which is able to remove virtually all afflictions
on the target. Using this ability requires a five(5) 
minute ceremony and requires the Patient to lie down
and either be Unconscious, Sleeping, or Intoxicated 
for its duration. During this ceremony the Physician is
required to role-play an examination and the 
administration of medicine to the target. This process 
is interrupted if either the Physician is interrupted or 
either Character takes Hit Point damage. Once the 
Surgery is complete, the target is affected by the 
effects "Physical Cleanse" and "Piercing Physical 
Drain" in that order. A Character may benefit from 
Surgery no more than once per Day with any 
attempts after the first acting instead as a "Physical 
Death".

	Upon reaching five(5) Ranks in this Skill the 
Character gains the ability to create yet another 
Curative Tincture. Once per Reset, for every five(5) 
Ranks the Character has in this Skill the Character 
may deliver a "Physical Remove &lt;Delivery Type&gt;" of 
any of the following Delivery Types; Elemental, 
Magic, Physical, or Poison. This use of Curative 
Tincture counts against the Character's maximum 
uses Curative Tinctures before Resting and per 
Reset.

### Resuscitator [Physician]
**Prerequisites:** 6 Ranks in Physician\
**Benefit:** A Character with this Skill is able to, as a 
sixty(60)-second count, deliver a "Physical Life" effect
on a Dead target. Upon doing so, the Physician is 
affected by a "Physical Drain". Alternatively, the 
Character may "hand-off" the count to another, 
willing, Physician, who has at least four(4) Ranks in 
this Skill. If the Character chooses to have another 
Physician take over for them it is the latter who is 
affected by the "Physical Drain".
Additionally, the Character may, as a ten(10)-count, 
grant a Dead Character the effects of a "Physical 
Tarry". Neither Character can be moved during this 
count.

### Surgeon, Expert [Physician]
**Prerequisites:** 7 Ranks in Physician\
**Benefit:** A Character with this Skill is able to perform 
Surgery which is able to restore all the target's lost 
Hit Points. Using this ability requires a five(5) minute 
ceremony and requires the Patient to lie down and 
either be Unconscious, Sleeping, or Intoxicated for its
duration. During this ceremony the Physician is 
required to role-play an examination and the 
administration of medicine to the target. This process 
is interrupted if either the Physician is interrupted or 
either Character takes Hit Point damage. Once the 
Surgery is complete, or interrupted, the target is 
affected by the effects "Physical Revive" and 
"Piercing Physical Drain" in that order.
Additionally, the number of targets the 
Physician may affect with First Aid, or Field Dressing, 
simultaneously is increased to four(4).

### Expert Medic [Physician]
**Prerequisites:** 8 Ranks in Physician\
**Benefit:** A Character with this Skill is able to perform 
Field Dressing on conscious Characters with greatly 
increased speed and effectiveness. The Physician 
may spend ten(10) seconds to heal the target a 
number of missing Hit Points equal to their Ranks in 
Physician. Once a target has benefited from this 
effect they must wear an armband, supplied by the 
Physician, made of gauze. The Patient cannot benefit
from this ability until they spend five(5) minutes 
Resting at which time they may remove the armband 
and return it to the Physician.

### Master Medic [Physician]
**Prerequisites:** 9 Ranks in Physician\
**Benefit:** A Character with this Skill is able to perform 
First Aid even while under an effect which would 
normally keep them from using Skills such as Drain, 
though they must still be Conscious. Additionally, they
may perform First Aid while using another Skill such 
as Diagnose. Finally, when the Character completes 
their First Aid count they may also deliver five(5) 
points of Physical Healing to all Characters they are 
performing First Aid upon. The call for this effect is 
"First Aid Complete. 5 Physical Healing".

### Master Physician [Physician]
**Prerequisites:** 10 Ranks in Physician\
**Benefit:** A Character with this Skill is able to perform 
Surgery on one(1) additional target for each 
Character with 5 Ranks in Physician that is assisting 
them. Additionally, they may perform Surgery on a 
Patient twice per Day and the Patient no longer 
needs to be Unconscious, Sleeping or Intoxicated. 
Finally, the number of targets the Physician may 
affect with First Aid, or Field Dressing, simultaneously
is increased to five(5).

	Upon reaching ten(10) Ranks in this Skill the 
Character gains the ability to create yet another 
Curative Tincture. Once per Reset, for every ten(10) 
Ranks the Character has in this Skill the Character 
may deliver a "Physical Cleanse" or "Physical Life" 
effect by Touch. This use of Curative Tincture counts 
against the Character's maximum uses Curative 
Tinctures before Resting and per Reset.
 
### Practised Medic [Physician]
**Prerequisites:** 15 Ranks in Physician plus 5 Ranks 
for each additional time this Skill is bought.\
**Benefit:** A Character with this skill increases the 
number of targets the Physician may affect with First 
Aid, or Field Dressing, simultaneously is increased by
one(1).

### Practised Surgeon [Physician]
**Prerequisites:** 20 Ranks in Physician plus 10 Ranks 
for each additional time this Skill is bought.\
**Benefit:** A Character with this skill increases the 
number of targets the Physician may affect with 
Surgery simultaneously is increased by one(1).
