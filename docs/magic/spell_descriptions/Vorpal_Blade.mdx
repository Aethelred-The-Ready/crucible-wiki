# Vorpal Blade
<b>School:</b> [Transmutation](../transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 1<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Indefinite<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the target the ability to cause five(5) additional points of damage with their next weapon attack. The target can continue calling this bonus damage until they successfully land a valid strike on their target.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the power of a Vorpal Blade."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Vorpal](/docs/glossary/vorpal) 1"<br/>
