# Augment Scroll
<b>Requirements:</b> [Create Scroll](/docs/skills/crafting/Create_Scroll.mdx) 20<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> The Character may expend one use of a single [Spellshape](/docs/magic/spellshapes) Skill. The Character may imbue a single [Diagnose](/docs/skills/crafting-skills/scroll) with the effects of the chosen Spellshape. Upon use, the Scroll's effect, modified by the imbued Spellshape, is triggered.

If the Character has access to a Scroll Laboratory, they may target two(2) Scrolls with this ability.

The name of the imbued Spellshape must be included on the Scroll. No Scroll may benefit from more than one Spellshape effect.<br/>
