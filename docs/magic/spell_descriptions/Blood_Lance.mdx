# Blood Lance
<b>School:</b> [Affliction](../affliction_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell can be used for one of two(2) effects. The first causes thirty(30) points of [Piercing](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#piercing) [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Body](/docs/glossary/body) damage to the target. The second causes thirty(30) points of Piercing Magic [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing) to the target. In either case, the Caster must expend seven(7) Hit Points to cast this Spell.

 If this Spell is affected by the [Echo](/docs/glossary/echo) [Spellshape](/docs/magic/spellshapes) effect the caster must expend the Hit Points again for each time the effect is duplicated.

 If this Spell is affected by the [Empower Spellshape](/docs/skills/magic-skills/empower-spellshape) effect the caster must instead expend twice the number of Hit Points the Spell normally requires.

 If this Spell is placed in a [Diagnose](/docs/skills/crafting-skills/potion) or [Diagnose](/docs/skills/crafting-skills/scroll), the Caster must choose which of the two(2) effects are contained within the crafted item.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Conjure a Blood Lance."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "30 [Piercing](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#piercing) [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Body](/docs/glossary/body)" or "30 Piercing Magic [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing)"<br/>
