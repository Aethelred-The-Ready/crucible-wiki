# The Zoan

The Zoan Peoples are anthropomorphic creatures
of the natural world which are endowed with human-like intelligence. While they vary widely in
appearance there are a small number of heritage
groups that can be recognized. The Zoan have a
number of origins, some are descendants of; animals
who were magically transformed into humanoids,
individuals who were punished by eldritch monsters,
lycanthropes and similar creatures or artificial beings
created in this form. Regardless of their origins, Zoan
are now counted among the Peoples of the world.\
All Zoan have an intense sense of community
where all members of the group treat each other as
family. This does not mean that they always get
along, it just means they form deep bonds for the
sake of mutual survival.
