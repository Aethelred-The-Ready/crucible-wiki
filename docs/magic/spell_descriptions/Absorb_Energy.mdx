# Absorb Energy
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list), [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 8<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Touch<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Charged<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> Upon casting this Spell, the Caster chooses one of the following energy types; Acid, Fire, Ice, Lightning, or Stone. The target may [Absorb](/docs/glossary/absorb) a single effect, which causes the specified damage type, negating it and healing a number of Hit Points equal to the damage absorbed. Any healing above the Character's maximum Hit Point total is lost. The call for the Absorb ability is "Absorb".<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the Protection of Absorb Energy"<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Imbue Skill](/docs/glossary/imbue-skill): [Absorb](/docs/glossary/absorb) &lt;Type&gt;"<br/>
