# Confine
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 8<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 5 Minutes<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This [Binding](/docs/glossary/binding) Spell forces the victim's arms to their sides and completely immobilises them, preventing them from moving or using any Skills while under its effects. The target may still speak. This Spell is a Binding effect.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Conjure a Force to Confine you."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Confine](/docs/glossary/binding#confine)"<br/>
