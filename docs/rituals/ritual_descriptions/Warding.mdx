# Warding
<b>Cost:</b> P3 C1 D1 E1 T1<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Magic, Smithing<br/>
<b>School:</b> [Protection](/docs/magic/protection_list)<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Any Item or Spirit<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Event<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> Choose one of the following effects; Acid, [Binding](/docs/glossary/binding), [Compulsion](/docs/glossary/compulsion), [Curse](/docs/glossary/curse), Fire, Ice, Lightning, Break/[Destroy](/docs/glossary/destroy), or Stone. Once per [Reset](/docs/glossary/reset) the player may choose to activate this defence negating the effect. The call for this ability is "[Ward](/docs/combat/defences#ward)".<br/>

This Ritual can be cast on a target more than once
