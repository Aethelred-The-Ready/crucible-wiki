# Archery
<b>Requirements:</b> None<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> The Character gains the ability to wield Bows and Crossbows in combat. The Character also has the ability to use these Weapons to block Weapon Attacks. In order to fire a shot from their (Cross)Bow the Character must remove an arrow, represented by an Archery Packet, from their Quiver. Then touch the Packet to the hand holding the (Cross)Bow and then role-play the drawing of the arrow back before throwing the Packet. For information on the requirements for the [Physical Representations](/docs/equipment) for Bows and Quivers, see Chapter 5: Equipment. Unlike most packet attacks, Archery Packets can be blocked by Weapons and [Shield](/docs/glossary/shield)s. For more information on attacking with Archery Packets see Chapter 6: Combat.<br/>
