# Weapon Prowess
<b>Requirements:</b> See Text<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is bought, the Character may grant themselves the effects of a [Vorpal](/docs/glossary/vorpal) 5. This allows them to increase the Weapon Damage call of their next five(5) landed hits by five(5). The call for this ability is "[Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) Vorpal 5"

A Character may have a number of Weapon Prowess that does not exceed five(5) plus one(1) for each Weapon Training they possess.<br/>
