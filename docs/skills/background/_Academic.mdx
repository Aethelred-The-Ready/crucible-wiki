The Academic is a Character who has devoted a 
large portion of their time to learning a variety of 
subjects gaining access to borderline, and 
occasionally legitimately, supernatural abilities due to 
their studies. Each time this Skill is bought, the 
Character may purchase a single Sub-Skill for which 
they meet all prerequisites. These Sub-Skills may be 
purchased in any order provided the Character has 
any requisite Sub-Skills. 

	Additionally, the Character gains the following 
abilities; as they gain Ranks in this Skill. The 
Character automatically gains these abilities for free 
as soon as they have the prerequisite Ranks in the 
appropriate Skill.

### Researcher [Academic]
**Prerequisites:** One Rank in Academic\
**Benefit:** Whenever the Character is in a library 
setting related to any of the Character's Academic 
Sub-Skills they may inform the encounter's Marshal 
of this fact. The Marshal may, at their discretion, 
provide the Character with additional information 
related to the subject matter. 

	The encounter's Marshal determines how, if any, 
knowledge is distributed but the most common 
methods are to inform all those with an applicable 
Sub-Skill at once with all information, or to distribute a
single piece of information to each applicable 
Character.

### Studious Focus [Academic]
**Prerequisites:** Five Ranks in Academic\
**Benefit:** Once per Day, the character may grant 
themselves the effects of a "Physical Compulsion 
Ward".

### Eclectic Knowledge [Academic]
**Prerequisites:** Ten Ranks in Academic\
**Benefit:** Once per Day, the Character may perform 
the Identify Invocation. The Character must follow all 
rules for the casting of the Invocation. This Skill does 
not grant the Character the ability to perform any 
other Invocation, nor does it count towards the 
number of Invocations the Character may cast each 
Day.

## Academic Sub-Skill Descriptions

### Archaeology [Academic]
**Prerequisites:** None\
**Benefit:** The Character may also use the Diagnose 
Skill on Constructs. Additionally, they may, once per 
Day,  deliver by packet, the effects of a "Magic Break 
&lt;Item&gt;". Finally, the Character may use all 
applications of the Physician Skill on Constructs.

### Archaeology, Advanced [Academic]
**Prerequisites:** Archaeology\
**Benefit:** Once per Day, the Character may deliver a 
packet attack causing a "Physical Vanquish 
Construct" effect. Additionally, a Character with the 
Skill Smithing may use the Field Mend ability to 
restore five(5) Hit Points to the target Construct.

### Astrology [Academic]
**Prerequisites:** None\
**Benefit:** The Character gains knowledge of the stars 
and their movements allowing them a number of 
benefits. Firstly, the Character may act as a 
Secondary Caster for any Ritual Magic effect cast 
under the night sky. They may not be the Primary 
Caster unless they have Ritual Magic Skill. If the 
Character has the Ritual Magic Skill they count as an 
additional Caster when performing any Ritual Magic 
effect cast under the night sky. Finally, the Character 
may also discern more accurate information when 
using the Prophecy Ritual Magic effect.

### Astrology, Advanced [Academic]
**Prerequisites:** Astrology\
**Benefit:** Once per Day, the Character may perform a 
special divination ceremony taking at least five(5) 
minutes. Once they have completed this ceremony 
they may grant themselves the effects of a "Magic 
Mirage 5".

### Botany [Academic]

### Botany, Advanced [Academic]
