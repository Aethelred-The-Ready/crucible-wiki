# Frighten
<b>School:</b> [Affliction](../affliction_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 5<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 10 Seconds<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This [Compulsion](/docs/glossary/compulsion) Spell causes the target to immediately flee the Caster's presence attempting to break line of sight with them. They cannot use any Skills while under this effect. This Spell lasts ten(10) seconds.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Compel you to be Frightened."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Lesser](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#lesser) [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Fear](/docs/glossary/compulsion#fear)"<br/>
