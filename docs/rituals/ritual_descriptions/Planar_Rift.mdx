# Planar Rift
<b>Cost:</b> P4 C2 E2 S2 + P1 per additional target<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Magic<br/>
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](/docs/magic/conjuration_list), [Protection](/docs/magic/protection_list), [Transmutation](/docs/magic/transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Target:</b> The Caster and One additional target per additional power Component expended.<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> See Text<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual causes the targets to be transported to the target Extra-Plane for up to one(1) hour per [Caster](/docs/rituals/ritual_glossary#Caster). The exact duration is chosen at the time of Casting the Ritual. Additionally, all targets gain the effects of Planar Attunement to the target plane. The maximum number of targets of this Ritual is equal to twice the number of Casters participating in the Ritual's casting.

 Casting this Ritual on a target under its effects causes the Ritual to immediately end returning the target to its home plane.<br/>
