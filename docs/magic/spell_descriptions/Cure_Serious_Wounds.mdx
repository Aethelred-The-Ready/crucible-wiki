# Cure Serious Wounds
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 4<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell causes twenty(20) points of [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing) to the target. Any healing above the Character's maximum is lost.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dawn to Cure Serious Wounds."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "20 [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing)"<br/>
