# Mirror Image
<b>School:</b> [Transmutation](../transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 2<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Day<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the Caster a Dodge against the next effect, delivered by Weapon or Packet, by which it is struck. Unlike the Dodge Skill, the Caster does not choose when this ability activates, it is automatically triggered as soon as the Caster is struck by an applicable effect.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the power of a Mirror Image."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Mirage](/docs/glossary/mirage) 1"<br/>
