# Coagulant
<b>School:</b> [Alchemy](../alchemy_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 1<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self and Touch<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Sustained<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Alchemy grants the Caster the ability to deliver a "[Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) [Stabilise](/docs/glossary/stabilise)" by Touch at will. The Caster may continue delivering this effect until they die or cast another Alchemy. Each individual casting of the Touch effect is subject to Disruption. Stabilise instantly puts a target which is Bleeding Out to zero(0) Hit Points.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) [Stabilise](/docs/glossary/stabilise)" … "Poison Stabilise"<br/>
