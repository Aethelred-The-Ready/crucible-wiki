# Innate Spellcasting, Greater
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 6 [Spell Slot](/docs/new_skills/magic/Spell_Slot.mdx), [Innate Spellcasting](/docs/new_skills/magic/Innate_Spellcasting.mdx)<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> This Skill grants the Character the ability to deliver all 4 th , 5 th , and 6 th level Spells, the Character casts from memory, without their Incantation. Additionally, they may cast these Spells while unable to speak, such as while under the effects of a Silence, or without a free hand.<br/>
