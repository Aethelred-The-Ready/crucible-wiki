# Displacement
<b>Type:</b> Magic<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Charged<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This [Invocation](/docs/invocations) grants the Caster three(3) Charges. The Caster may expend one(1) Charge to negate a single effect delivered by Weapon or Packet. The Call for this ability is "[Dodge](/docs/glossary/defences#dodge)".<br/>
