# Veil of the Hunter
<b>Cost:</b> Varies<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Magic<br/>
<b>School:</b> Any<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Any number of Spirits<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Event<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> Once cast, all targets gain the benefits of the Hunter's Veil specified by the scroll. Eg:     Spider Hunter's Veil: Targets gain: Escape from [Binding](/docs/glossary/binding) on 3-Count. Reduce damage from [Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) by half. Poison Resistance

 Vampire Hunter's Veil: Targets gain: [Immunity](/docs/combat/defences#immune) to Vampire Charms, Vampirism and Create Undead.<br/>

This Ritual can be cast on a target more than once
