# Undead Fortitude
<b>School:</b> [Affliction](../affliction_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 1<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Indefinite<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the target five(5) [Fortification Points](/docs/glossary/fortify). This Spell requires the target to openly wear one or more humanoid bones as part of their costuming.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the power of Undead Fortitude."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Fortify](/docs/glossary/fortify) 5"<br/>
