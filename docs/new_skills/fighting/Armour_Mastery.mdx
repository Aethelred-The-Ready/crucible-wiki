# Armour Mastery
<b>Requirements:</b> None<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> The Character's familiarity with [Physical Armour](/docs/equipment/armour) grants them additional protection when wearing it. For every time this Skill is bought, the number of points of Physical Armour the Character gains from wearing Physical Armour is increased by five(5). The maximum bonus from this Skill is equal to the Armour Points of the [Physical Representation](/docs/equipment) of the suit of Physical Armour the Character is wearing, or their maximum Wear Total, whichever is lower. For example, a Character wearing a Physical Representation worth fifteen(15) points is able to benefit from no more than fifteen(15) additional Armour Points from this Skill. Even if the armour under the [Enhance](/docs/glossary/enhance) Armour effect.<br/>
