# Conjure Weapon
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 3<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Day<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the Caster a weapon formed from magical energy. This weapon is mundane in nature but functions only for the Caster. As such, it must be red or marked by a red band on the handle. This Spell does not grant the Caster Proficiency in the chosen weapon. The Caster may put the weapon away and retrieve it at any time so long as they have at least one free hand.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Conjure a Weapon."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Conjure](/docs/glossary/conjure) Weapon"<br/>
