# Polycast
<b>Type:</b> Magic<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Charged (1)<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Invocation grants the Caster one(1) Charge. The Caster may expend one(1) Charge when casting a Spell, with a range of "Packet", to throw any number of packets, all at once. Any creature struck by one or more packets takes the effects of the Spell. Alternatively they may expend one(1) Charge, when casting a Spell with a range of "[Contingency](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#touch)" or greater, to be able to deliver an additional copy of Spell, by Touch, on any number of targets until one of the following occurs; the Spell is Disrupted, the Caster goes ten(10) seconds without affecting a new target, or the Caster uses any other Skill. No target may benefit from the effects of a single Spell cast using this ability more than once.<br/>
