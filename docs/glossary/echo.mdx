---
title: "Echo <Delivery Verbal>"
---

This effect allows the Character to deliver a copy of the last applicable Technique they cast. This ability must be activiated within ten(10) seconds of the initial Technique. The copy includes all the same options, including any and all Mutations or Metamagics, or any other abilities used on the first Technique.

The call for this ability is "Echo: &lt;Delivery Verbal of copied Technique&gt;"
