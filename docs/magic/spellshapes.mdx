# Spellshapes

Spellshapes are a category of Magic Skills that allow a character to modify the casting of a spell in some way

### [Channel Spell](/docs/magic-skills/channel-spell)
Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is
bought, the Character may deliver a single Spell as
an &lt;Effect&gt;-Strike using a Weapon, instead of a
Packet, with which they are proficient. The Delivery
Type used to deliver the effect is determined by the
Delivery Type of the Spell. For example, if the Spell
allows the Character to deliver a Magic Sleep effect,
the call for this ability is “Magic-Strike Sleep”. If the
effect causes “10 Elemental Fire” damage, the
Channelled effect is “10 Elemental-Strike Fire”. The
Character does not need a free hand to cast the Spell
if they are wielding a Weapon with which they are
Proficient. 

[Click here for more information on Channeling Spell](./spellcasting.mdx#channelling-a-spell)

### [Contingent Spell](/docs/magic-skills/contingent-spell)
Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is
bought, the Character may cast a Spell and have it
triggered upon one of the following conditions; Dead,
Dying, Berserked, Binding, Compelled, Cursed,
Enthraled, Imprisoned, Poisoned, Sleeping, or
Unconscious, chosen at the time of casting. If the
Character dies before this effect is used it is lost. The
call to first activate this ability is “I Invoke a &lt;Delivery
Verbal> Spell, Contingent upon my &lt;Trigger
State>.”A Character may have only one Contingency
effect, from this Skill, at a time. The call when this
ability is triggered is “Contingency: &lt;Delivery
Verbal>”.
### [Echo Spell](/docs/magic-skills/echo-spell)
Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is
bought, the Character may deliver a copy of the last
Spell they cast. The Spell must be delivered from
memory to use this Skill. This ability must be
activated within ten(10) seconds of the initial Spell.
The copy is delivered without Incantation and
includes all the same options, including any and all
Spellshapes and other abilities used on the first Spell.
The call for this ability is “Echo: &lt;Delivery Verbal of
copied Spell>”

### [Empower Spell](/docs/magic-skills/empower-spell)
Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is
bought, the Character may double the damage or
healing of a single Spell with a Duration of Instant. 

### [Piercing Spell](/docs/magic-skills/piercing-spell)
Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is
bought, the Character may deliver a single Spell, with
a Range of Packet, as Piercing. The call for this
ability is “&lt;Incantation> &lt;#> Piercing &lt;Delivery Type>
&lt;Effect> &lt;Option>”. For example, a Sleep Spell
would use the Delivery Verbal “Piercing Magic Sleep”
while an Elemental Bolt would use the Delivery
Verbal “10 Piercing Elemental &lt;Type>”.

### [Quicken Spell](/docs/magic-skills/quicken-spell)
Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is
bought, the Character may deliver a single Spell
without its Incantation. Additionally, they may cast this
Spell while unable to speak, such as while under the
effects of a Silence, or without a free hand.
