# Magic Shield, Greater
<b>School:</b> [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Indefinite<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the target a [Shield](/docs/glossary/shield) against the next two(2) effects delivered as [Magic-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) by which they are struck.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the protection of a Greater Magic Shield."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) Greater Magic [Shield](/docs/glossary/shield)"<br/>
