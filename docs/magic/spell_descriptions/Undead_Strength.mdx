# Undead Strength
<b>School:</b> [Affliction](../affliction_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 2<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Touch<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Day<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell grants the target one(1) point of [Strength](/docs/glossary/strength) increasing their Weapon Damage by one(1). This effect does not stack with Strength from any other source. This Spell requires the target to openly wear one or more humanoid bones as part of their costuming.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the power of Undead Strength."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Imbue Skill](/docs/glossary/imbue-skill): [Strength](/docs/glossary/strength) 1"<br/>
