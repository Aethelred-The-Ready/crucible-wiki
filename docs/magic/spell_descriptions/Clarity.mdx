# Clarity
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell instantly grants the target the benefits of having Meditated. This applies only to expended Skills which are recovered by Meditating.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dawn to grant you Clarity."<br/>
