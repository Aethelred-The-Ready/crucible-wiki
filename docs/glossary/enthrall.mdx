# Enthral
This powerful effect places the target under
the complete control of the caster. They will follow all
orders, even self destructive ones. This effect is not
removed by the Character’s [Death](./states-of-living#death). Only a [Cleanse](./cleanse)
effect or the Character’s Resurrection can remove it.\
Once a Character has been affected by this effect
they are unable to move, take any action, or even
speak, until one of the following occurs:
- five(5)
minutes go by without receiving an order from the
source of the Enthral effect
- they are given an order

Once they have been given an order they are
unable to use any effect on themselves which would
remove the Enthral effect such as casting Cleanse on
themselves unless ordered to do so.

Please note
that this spell does not force a player to engage in
any activities they are not comfortable with
performing.
