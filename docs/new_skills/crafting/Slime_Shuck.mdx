# Slime Shuck
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 5 Alchemy Slot<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is bought, as long as the Character has at least one(1) Armour Point of [Natural Armour](/docs/equipment/non-physical-armour) available they may expend all remaining Natural Armour Points to grant themselves a "Poison [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel) Binding" effect. The call for this ability is "Absorb. Poison Dispel Binding".<br/>
