<b>School:</b> [Transmutation](../transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 3<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>

<b>Incantation:</b> "I alter this form to Refit Physical Armour."<br/>
