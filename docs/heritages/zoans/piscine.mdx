# Piscine

The Piscine possess varied but obvious aquatic animal traits. Some appear fish-like while others have an appearance that more closely resembles mammals or reptiles. Supposedly there are even cephalopod Piscine but these are extremely rare.\
When they venture out to become adventurers they often join up with other groups. They work tirelessly and efficiently doing their best to help those around them. Despite often being mistaken as helpless, the Piscine boast a number of extremely capable warriors and powerful mages.

### Costuming Requirements

Aquatic animal traits.

### Costuming Suggestions

Being aquatic, the Piscine have a habit of dressing for the water, even when on dry land. Inexplicably, many forego this clothing during the rain or when actually on the water.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Keep Swimming:
The Character has [Sleep](../../glossary/compulsion#sleep) [Resistance](../../glossary/resistance). This ability reduces the duration of all Sleep effects, other than those delivered as [Arcane(-Strike)](../../glossary/delivery-types#arcane) or [Piercing](../../glossary/delivery-types#piercing) to ten(10) seconds. Additionally, while under the Sleep effect, this creature, though still unaware of its surroundings, may continue to move at a normal walking pace.\
Additionally, when the Character becomes [Unconscious](../../glossary/states-of-living#unconscious), but not [Dead](../../glossary/states-of-living#dead), as a result of Hit Point Damage, they may continue to move at a normal walking rate for ten(10) seconds before having to fall to the ground, as soon as it is safe to do so. The Character cannot use Skills, or items, while in this state.
