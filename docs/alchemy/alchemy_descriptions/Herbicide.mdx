# Herbicide
<b>School:</b> [Alchemy](../alchemy_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Alchemy instantly Vanquishes the target Plant Type Creature. A Vanquished creature instantly begins Bleeding Out. Some powerful Plant creatures may instead take damage from this Alchemy.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) [Vanquish](/docs/glossary/vanquish) Plant"<br/>
