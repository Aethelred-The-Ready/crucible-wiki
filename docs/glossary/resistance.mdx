# &lt;Delivery Type/Effect> Resistance

This ability causes the Character to take reduced effect from
effects to which it is applicable. All effects to which it
applies have their durations reduced to ten(10)
seconds as though they were delivered as [Lesser](./delivery-types#lesser).\
Additionally, any damage to which it applies is
reduced by half, rounded down.

Effects which are delivered as [Piercing](./delivery-types#piercing) or [Arcane](./delivery-types#arcane) bypass this ability.

For example,
a creature with Magic Resistance reduces the
duration of all effects delivered as Magic(-Strike) is
reduced as is all damage which is delivered as
Magic(-Strike). A creature with Curse Resistance
reduces the duration of all Curse effects to ten(10)
seconds. A creature with Fire Resistance reduces the
damage from all Fire damage by half. Perfect
Resistance indicates a creature has Resistance to all
effects. The call for this ability is “Reduced”
