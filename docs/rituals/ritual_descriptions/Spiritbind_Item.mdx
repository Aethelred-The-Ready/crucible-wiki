# Spiritbind Item
<b>Cost:</b> P3 C2 E1 S1<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Magic<br/>
<b>School:</b> [Transmutation](/docs/magic/transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Target:</b> Item and Spirit<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Event<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual binds the target item to the target creature. The item cannot be used or even carried by any other creature. All Spiritbound items must be marked with red either with a 1cm thick red band on the top or bottom of the grip, or be at least seventy- five(75)% red. Spiritbound items can be Conjured to the Character's possession any time the Player has access to a valid [Physical Representation](/docs/equipment). A hold may not be called for the Player to retrieve the item. A Spiritbound item cannot be stolen, moved or used by any Character except the one to whom it is bound though it is acceptable for a Player to return the item directly to its owner. A Spirit may have multiple Items Spiritbound to them, but an Item cannot be Spiritbound to multiple Spirits.<br/>
