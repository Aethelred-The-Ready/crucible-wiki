# Vorpal Finisher
<b>School:</b> [Slayer](../slayer_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 8<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Charged<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Artistry grants the target the ability to cause five(5) additional points of damage with their next five(5) weapon attacks. The target can continue calling this bonus damage until they successfully land a valid strike on their target. Once all of these Charges are consumed, the Caster may deliver a [Physical-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) Causing twenty-five(25) points of damage of the Weapon Type of the weapon used to deliver them.

 If this Artistry benefits from the Assassin Tradition, Echo, or similar effects, it applies only to the final Physical-Strike.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Vorpal](/docs/glossary/vorpal) 5" and "25 [Physical-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) &lt;Weapon Type&gt;"<br/>
