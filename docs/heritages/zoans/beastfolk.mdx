# Beastfolk

The Beastfolk are Zoan who appear to be part mammal. They often display the behaviours of the animal to which they resemble, but this is not always the case. Some even deliberately avoid these behaviours, viewing them as vulgar.\
Beastfolk adventurers often operate as part of a group where they are able to share information and resources, increasing their chances of success in whatever situation in which they find themselves.

### Costuming Requirements

Mammalian animal traits.

### Costuming Suggestions

Beastfolk tend to favour practical clothing foregoing fashion for the sake of efficiency. This also means they have a tendency to only replace clothing when it has been completely destroyed.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Predator: 

The Character gains the ability to [Conjure](/docs/glossary/conjure) a single [Claw](/docs/glossary/claw), up to a [Long Sword](/docs/equipment/weapons) in length, and gains Proficiency in Claws. Additionally, the Character may grant themselves a “[Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types) [Ambush](/docs/glossary/ambush) 1” effect. This effect can be recovered by [Meditating](/docs/glossary/meditate).\
If the Character has the [Two Weapon Fighting Skill](/docs/skills/fighting/two_weapon_fighting), they may Conjure up to two(2) Claws at once in order to wield one(1) in each hand.\
If the Character is under an effect which prevents the use of Skills, such as [Drain](/docs/glossary/curse#drain), the Character is unable to attack or defend with these Claws.
