# Living Spell
<b>Type:</b> Magic<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Reset<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Invocation grants the Caster three(3) Charges. The Caster may expend one(1) Charge when casting a Spell to Quicken the Spell, as per the Skill Quicken Skill. Alternatively, they may expend three(3) Charges in order to cast a Spell when they are in a state where they would be unable to Cast Spells such as under the effects of a [Confine](/docs/glossary/binding#confine) or [Drain](/docs/glossary/curse#drain) effect. This does not allow the Character to cast spells while [Dead](/docs/combat/states-of-living) or Unconcious.<br/>
