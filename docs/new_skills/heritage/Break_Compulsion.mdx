# Break Compulsion
<b>Requirements:</b> None<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> A Character with this Skill may spend sixty(60) seconds, role playing with another creature. This can either be in quiet concentration or by speaking with the target. At the end of the minute, the Character states "[Break](/docs/glossary/break) [Compulsion](/docs/glossary/compulsion). [Arcane](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#arcane) [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel) Compulsion.". This ability is usable at will.

Alternatively, the Character may expend this Skill, becoming unable to use its benefits until Reset, to instantly negate all [Compulsions](/docs/glossary/compulsion) on any target, including themselves, by [Contingency](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#touch). When used in this manner the call is "Arcane Dispel Compulsion". When used in this way the effect may be delivered as Touch or by Packet. The Skill cannot be recovered by the [Inspiration](/docs/glossary/inspiration) effect.<br/>
