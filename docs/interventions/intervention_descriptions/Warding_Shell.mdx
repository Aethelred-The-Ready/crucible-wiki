# Warding Shell
<b>Type:</b> Crafting, Magical<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> When performed as a Magical Intervention this Intervention can only be performed by a Character able to cast Spells from the [Protection School of Magic](/docs/magic/spellcasting#school).

 This Intervention creates a magical defensive barrier preventing anyone from entering, but not leaving, the area and rendering all fully within its bounds [Immune](/docs/glossary/defences#immune) to all effects originating from outside of its boundary. In order to use this effect the Character must be inside of a valid Circle. The Caster may "Recognize" an individual into the Circle. This effect continues until the Character activating this Intervention leaves the Circle, or enters a state where they are unable to use Skills such as [Unconscious](/docs/glossary/states-of-living) or [Dead](/docs/glossary/states-of-living). This effect does not end if the Caster is under the effects of [Stasis](/docs/glossary/stasis), though it does prevent the Caster from recognizing anyone in.

 Alternatively, the Character may instead grant themselves five(5) charges of this ability. The Character may then expend one(1) Charge in order to negate any effect, other than those delivered as [Arcane](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#arcane), by which they are struck as per the [Ward](/docs/glossary/Ward) ability. The Character may, at will, Meditate to regain one(1) Charge of this ability.

 The effects of this Intervention last until the end of Combat.<br/>
