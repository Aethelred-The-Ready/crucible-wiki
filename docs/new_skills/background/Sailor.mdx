# Sailor
<b>Requirements:</b> None<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> The Character gains immunity to many environmental effects while fighting on, or beneath the sea. The specific effects are up to the Marshal who must be informed of this Skill at the start of the Module. Additionally, once per Reset the Character may, when drinking any beverage, grant themselves either of the following effects; 20 [Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing), or Poison [Remove](/docs/glossary/remove) &lt;Delivery Type&gt; of any of the following Delivery Types; Elemental, Magic, Physical, or Poison. Once per Reset the Character may Rest to recover the use of this ability.<br/>
