# Gauntlet Forms
<b>School:</b> [Duelist](/docs/martial_arts/martial_arts#school), [Guardian](/docs/martial_arts/martial_arts#school)<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> Upon casting this Kata the Caster designates one arm as their off-hand arm. So long as the Character is not wielding a weapon with their off-hand arm they are [Immune](/docs/glossary/defences#immune) to Weapon Damage which strikes the chosen arm, except for Weapon Type Massive. This effect is suppressed if the Character wields any two-handed weapon, a second weapon or a shield. It is also suppressed if the Character holds any in-game item in that arm. Finally, the off-hand does not count as a free hand for Spell casting. The Character does not need to wear a Gauntlet on their off-hand but must wear a red bracelet around the wrist.<br/>
