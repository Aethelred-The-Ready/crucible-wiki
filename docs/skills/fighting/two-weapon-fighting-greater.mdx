# Two Weapon Fighting, Greater
import Skill from '../skill.tsx'

<b>Requirements:</b> [Two Weapon Fighting](../../two-weapon-fighting)\
The Character's damage with their off-hand
Weapon is increased to an amount equal to the
Character's main hand Weapon Training minus
two(2). While this means the minimum damage
bonus is zero(0), the Character may still cause the
Base Damage of the Weapon. A Character with this
Skill would need to have at least three(3) Ranks in
Weapon Training before they will receive any bonus
damage to their off-hand Weapon.
