# Verdant

The Verdant are a diverse Heritage of humanoid plants, and organisms similar to plants. Though these groups often have distinct names for themselves they share the same general characteristics. Verdant are extremely durable and resistant to most harmful effects making them outstanding protectors of both nature and the civilised world. They are also quite ponderous thinking carefully about what they'll say before they say it. This can sometimes lead to others believing they are being ignored when the verdant is simply being cautious about what they'll say.\
Verdant adventure to see new places. There may be people there but it's really the terrain, flora and features of the natural world that they want to experience. When they do form a bond with others, it's extremely strong and long lasting. It's not unusual for a Verdant to maintain friendship with a family through several generations.

### Costuming Requirements

Leaves or flowers in hair. Overt plant or fungal traits.

### Costuming Suggestions

Verdant dress like most people decorate their gardens. They have bright colours, wooden armour pieces and a habit of looking like a scarecrow which has been reclaimed by nature.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Verdant Recovery: 
Once per [Reset](../../glossary/reset), while standing on natural terrain, the Character may deliver, by Touch to themselves, a [Physical](../../glossary/delivery-types/#physical) [Rejuvenate](../../glossary/rejuvenate) or Physical [Regenerate](../../glossary/regenerate). If this effect is interrupted the Character may [Meditate](../../glossary/meditate) to recover the use. This Ability cannot be used if the Character is standing on artificial terrain such as indoors, on a road, or on masoned stone.\
The Character may [Rest](../../glossary/rest) in order to regain the use of this ability.
