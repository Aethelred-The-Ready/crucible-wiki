# Wild Elf

Wild Elves can be found in forests, deserts and pretty much anywhere else where they can be away from civilization. This is not due to them being somehow less “civilized” than others, they have simply chosen to live alongside nature.\
As a result, they tend to be very minimal when it comes to their possessions, able to live without the comforts most people consider essential. They have a strong respect for nature and the rules which govern it

### Costuming Requirements

Like all other Elves, the Wild Elves have pointed ears.\
They decorate themselves with furs and facial markings of one or more colours. These markings may be of any colour though green, brown and red are most common. Regardless of the form the markings take, no single colour may cover more than 25% of the face and the total makeup can cover no more than 50% of the face. This is to avoid appearing as another Heritage.

### Costuming Suggestions

Wild Elves tend to dress in clothing meant to camouflage themselves with their surroundings. Some also wear furs.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Forest's Mark:
The Wild Elf may Invoke this ability on a single Character designating it the target for this ability.\
Once their Mark has been chosen, the Character increases all Weapon Damage against their target by two(2). This ability lasts until the end of combat.\
Alternatively, the Character may expend this effect early by delivering a single Alchemy, Martial Art, or Spell from their memory as Piercing.\
Once per [Reset](../../glossary/reset), the Character may [Rest](../../glossary/rest) to regain the use of this ability.\
The call for this ability is “I invoke Forest's Mark against you.”
