# Defensive Forms
<b>School:</b> [Guardian](/docs/martial_arts/martial_arts#school)<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> For every Rank the character has in the Skill Martial Kata, they may designate a one(1) Character as a target of this Kata. Every target, other than the Caster, increases their maximum Hit Points by five(5). This effect does not stack with any other effect which increases Hit Points.<br/>
