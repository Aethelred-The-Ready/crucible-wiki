# Quicken Spell
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 3 [Spell Slot](/docs/new_skills/magic/Spell_Slot.mdx)<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> Once per Reset, for every time this Skill is bought, the Character may deliver a single Spell without its Incantation. Additionally, they may cast this Spell while unable to speak, such as while under the effects of a Silence, or without a free hand.<br/>
