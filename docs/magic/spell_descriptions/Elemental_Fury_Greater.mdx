# Elemental Fury, Greater
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 6<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Sustained<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> As part of casting this Spell the Caster chooses one of the following types; Fire, Ice, Lightning, or Stone. The Caster may deliver a packet attack causing two(2) [Elemental](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental) damage of the chosen type at will. This Spell ends if the Caster Dies or casts any other Spell. The individual packet attacks are subject to Disruption.

 This Spell cannot benefit from the effects of any [Spellshape](/docs/magic/spellshapes) or ability which modifies the casting of a single Spell.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Conjure the Greater Fury of the Elements."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) Greater [Elemental](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#elemental) Fury" and "2 Elemental &lt;Type&gt;"<br/>
