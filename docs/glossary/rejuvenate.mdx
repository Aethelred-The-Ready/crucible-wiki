# Rejuvenate

This effect removes, after a sixty(60)-
count, all harmful effects that would be removed by
the target’s [Death](/docs/glossary/death). It is up to the Character’s player to
determine which effects are harmful. Rejuvenate is,
usually, interrupted by the Character taking damage
though some creatures have the ability to continue
Rejuvenating even if they are under attack or even
Dead. A small number of creatures have the ability to
Rejuvenate in less than a sixty(60)-count but these
are very rare.
