# Oil of Escaping
<b>School:</b> [Alchemy](../alchemy_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 5<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This effect removes all [Binding](/docs/glossary/binding) effects from the target. Additionally, this effect frees the target from any form of mundane confinement including, but not limited to; rope, manacles and stocks.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel) [Binding](/docs/glossary/binding)"<br/>
