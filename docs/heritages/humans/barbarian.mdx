# Barbarian

These humans live in the wild regions of the world in small nomadic societies. They waste little and are careful in their hunting and practices making sure that their tribe is fed, safe and healthy.\
Many Barbarians associated themselves with a particular totem. A totem is an abstraction of natural forces which they believe are embodied by a magical animal. It is possible for two different tribes to both have the same ideals and come to the conclusion that a different animal represents those ideas.\
Barbarians are able to call upon the power of their totem to grant themselves strength of body and mind, often gaining visions or insight into their problems.\
Unfortunately this was initially mistaken as superstition as it was a form of magic that was not known to those who observed it leading to the group being branded as Barbarians.

### Costuming Requirements

All Barbarians wear fur, this can be belts, cloaks, mantles, or any other fur addition to their costume.

### Costuming Suggestions

Barbarians tend to include simple iron jewellery or wooden trinkets depicting important ancestral, historical or mythical figures. It's also not unusual for them to wear makeshift bandoleers of useful items.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Rage:

Once per [Reset](../../glossary/reset), a Barbarian may enter into a Rage granting themselves the following effects; Physical [Vorpal](../../glossary/vorpal) 5 and [Immunity](../../glossary/immunity) to [Berserk](../../glossary/berserk) and [Fear](../../glossary/compulsion#fear) effects so long as they have at least one(1) Charge of the Vorpal effect granted by this ability. Once per Reset, the Character may spend five(5) minutes [Resting](../../glossary/rest) in order to recharge this ability allowing them to use, at most, two(2) times per Reset.
