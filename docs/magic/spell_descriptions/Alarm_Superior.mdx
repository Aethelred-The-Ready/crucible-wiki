# Alarm, Superior
<b>School:</b> [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Room<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Day<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell causes the target Room to become magically protected. Any creature within the room at the time of casting gains an investiture into the [Alarm](/docs/glossary/alarm). Any creature, who is not invested, that attempts to enter this room must knock loudly for ten(10) seconds upon entering. A room which is the target of this Spell must have a large "A" emblazoned on the back of the Room's Marshal Notes. Additionally, the intruding creature is affected by a single harmful Spell from the Caster's memory which is cast as part of casting this Spell and below 7th Level. The chosen spell is delivered as [Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) against the intruder. This effect cannot be [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel)led.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the protection of a Superior Alarm."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) Superior [Alarm](/docs/glossary/alarm) &lt;Spell Name&gt;"<br/>
