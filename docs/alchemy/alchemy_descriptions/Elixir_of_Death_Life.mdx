# Elixir of Death/Life
<b>School:</b> [Alchemy](../alchemy_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 9<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> As part of casting this Spell the Caster chooses one of the following options; [Death](/docs/glossary/death) or [Life](/docs/glossary/life). This Alchemy allows the Caster to deliver the chosen effect as a packet attack.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Poison](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#poison) [Death](/docs/glossary/death)" or "Poison [Life](/docs/glossary/life)"<br/>
