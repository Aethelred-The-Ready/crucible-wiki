# Martial Tradition
import Skill from '../skill.tsx'

<b>Requirements:</b>; Any Weapon Proficiency\
The Character is able to learn, and gain the effects of, a single Martial Tradition.

<Skill name="Martial Tradition" />

Martial Traditions represent the manner in which a Character learned, or practices hurting other Characters. Effects which renew or recharge Skills, such as Inspiration, may never be used to increase the number of times a Character can benefit from the effects of their Tradition.\
The effects of Martial Traditions apply only to Martial Arts the Character delivers from memory.

### Assassin
Requirements: Level 1 Martial Arts Slot\
Benefit: In order to use this benefit of this ability the
Character must be behind their target and able to see
both of their target’s shoulder blades. The Character
may double the damage of any single Martial Art
which is delivered as Physical-Strike. This ability is
usable a total of three(3) times, but the Character
must Meditate in between each use. The Character
may, once per Reset, spend a [Rest](../../glossary/rest)to recover all
uses of this ability for a maximum of six(6) uses per
day.

### Berserker
Requirements: Level 1 Martial Arts Slot\
Benefit: Once per Reset, the Character may [Rest](../../glossary/rest) in
order to recover three(3) expended Martial Art Slots
of any level(s) they can cast. Additionally, once per
Reset, the Character may grant themselves the effect
of a “Physical Clarity” granting them the benefits of
Meditating instantly.

### Combat Archer
Requirements: Level 1 Martial Arts Slot\
Benefit: Whenever the Character delivers a Martial
Art as Physical-Strike using an Archery packet they
may, if the initial packet fails to strike any target, or
the attack is negated by the defensive ability Dodge,
deliver a copy of the effect, including any Skills which
modify the attack, within ten(10) seconds without
using another Martial Arts Slot. If this second packet
fails to hit any target it may be recovered by
Meditation. If the second packet attack does strike a
valid target the Martial Arts Slot may not be recovered
by Meditation. The call for this ability is “Echo
&lt;Delivery Verbal&gt;”.

### Focused Warrior
Requirements: Level 1 Martial Arts Slot\
Benefit: The Character chooses a single weapon
type; Archery, Axe, Mace, Sword, or Thrown. The
Character loses the ability to use Martial Arts while
not wielding their chosen weapon. The Character
gains one(1) additional Martial Art Slot of each level
they can cast. These additional Martial Arts Slots do
not count towards any Requirements for Skills.

### Prepared Warrior
Requirements: Level 1 Martial Arts Slot\
Benefit: After each Reset, the Character may choose
one(1) Martial Art of each level, for which they have
Martial Art Slots, adding them to a list of specially
prepared Arts. The Character may cast each Art on
their list of prepared Arts once without expending a
Martial Art Slot.

### Readied Warrior
Requirements: Level 1 Martial Arts Slot\
Benefit: Prior to casting any Martial Art the Character
may spend ten(10)-seconds, during which time they
must plant their feet and role-play “readying” their
weapon. Once this count has been finished their next
Martial Arts used, before moving, is delivered as
Piercing.

### Tireless Warrior
Requirements: Level 1 Martial Arts Slot\
Benefit: Once per Reset, the Character may [Rest](../../glossary/rest) in
order to recover a single expended Martial Arts Slot
for each level they can cast.

### Twin Weapons
Requirements: Level 1 Martial Arts Slot\
Benefit: Whenever the Character uses a Martial Art
which is delivered by weapon they may deliver a copy
of the Martial Art within ten(10) seconds of the first.
This second Martial Art must be delivered using a
different weapon than the first. This ability is usable a
total of three(3) times, but the Character must
Meditate in between each use. The Character may,
once per Reset, spend a [Rest](../../glossary/rest) to recover all uses of
this ability. The call for this ability is “Echo &lt;Delivery
Verbal&gt;”.

### Unfaltering Champion
Requirements: Level 1 Martial Arts Slot\
Benefit: Whenever the Character performs a Martial
Art they may grant themselves a number of
Fortification Points equal to the level of the expended
Martial Art. This effect must be delivered within
ten(10) seconds of the Martial Art being cast. Unlike
the Magic Tradition Living Magic, multiple Martial Arts
cast in sequence do not increase the number of
Fortification Points granted by this ability, only the
most recent Martial Art performed matters. The call
for this ability is “Physical Fortify &lt;X&gt;”.
If the Character has the Skill Bodyguard, they may
expend a use the Parry Skill to negate a single effect,
to which the Skill Parry is applicable, which strikes a
target within the Character's weapon reach.
Additionally, they may, once per Reset, [Rest](../../glossary/rest) in order
to regain the use of Parry granted by the Bodyguard
Skill for use on their charge
