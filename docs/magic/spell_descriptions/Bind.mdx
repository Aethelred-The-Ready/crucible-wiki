# Bind
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 4<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 5 Minutes<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell forces the target's arms to their sides and keeps them there throughout its duration. The creature cannot use any Skill which requires the use of their arms such as using weapons or casting Spells. This Spell is a [Binding](/docs/glossary/binding) effect and removed by [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel) Binding.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Conjure a Force to Bind you."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Bind](/docs/glossary/binding#bind)"<br/>
