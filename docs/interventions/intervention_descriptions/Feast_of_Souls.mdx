# Feast of Souls 
<b>Type:</b> Crafting, Magical<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> When performed as a Magical Intervention this Intervention can only be performed by a Character able to cast Spells from the [Affliction School of Magic](/docs/magic/spellcasting#school). The Character delivers, by voice, fifty(50) [Arcane](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#arcane) [Body](/docs/glossary/body) damage, to all enemies on the field. Additionally, the Character delivers to all allies, fifty(50) Arcane [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing). The call for this ability is "[Sound of Voice](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#sound-of-voice): All Enemies, 50 Arcane Body! Sound of Voice: All Adventurers, 50 Arcane Healing!"<br/>
