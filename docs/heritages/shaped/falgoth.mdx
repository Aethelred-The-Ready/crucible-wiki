# Falgoth

The Falgoth are highly advanced slimes believed to have been meant to act as a servant class by the Shapers. Whatever their intentions, the nature of the Falgoth are intelligent and capable, being able to learn complex tasks in little time. Where they lack is in innovations and philosophy. Falgoth imitate but seldom create.\
As adventurers, most Falgoth are out to collect new skills for their pools, literal lakes of elder Falgoth who have aggregated into a vast living library of found knowledge.

### Costuming Requirements

Shiny skin with blue tint

### Costuming Suggestions

Falgoth tend to avoid restrictive clothing preferring loose fitting, or minimal obstructions to their movements.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Malleable:
The Character has [Binding](../../glossary/binding) [Resistance](../../glossary/resistance) reducing the duration of all Binding effects to ten(10) seconds. Effects delivered as [Piercing](../../glossary/delivery-types#piercing) or [Arcane](../../glossary/delivery-types#arcane) bypass this ability.
