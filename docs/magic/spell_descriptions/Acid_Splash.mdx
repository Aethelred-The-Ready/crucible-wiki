# Acid Splash
<b>School:</b> [Conjuration](../conjuration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 2<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell allows the Caster to deliver a packet attack causing ten(10) points of [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) Acid damage.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Conjure an Acid Splash."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "10 [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) Acid"<br/>
