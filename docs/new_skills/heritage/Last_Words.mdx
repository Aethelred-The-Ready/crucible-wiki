# Last Words
<b>Requirements:</b> None<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> Whenever the Character enters their [Death](/docs/glossary/death) Count, they may say up to ten(10) words at a normal speaking volume. If the Character has Rank 4 of the Seer Skill, they may use this ability while Manifested. If the Character has Rank 10 of the Seer Skill, they may once per Reset, touch a target and deliver a "[Lesser](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#lesser) Arcane Imbue Skill: Last Words" allowing the touched target to use this Skill for ten(10) seconds.<br/>
