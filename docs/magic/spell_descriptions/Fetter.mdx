# Fetter
<b>School:</b> [Affliction](../affliction_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 3<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> 5 Minutes<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This [Curse](/docs/glossary/curse) Spell prevents the target from running or jumping. The target must have at least one foot on the ground at all times and cannot move faster than a normal walking pace.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I Curse you to be Fettered."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Fetter](/docs/glossary/curse#fetter)"<br/>
