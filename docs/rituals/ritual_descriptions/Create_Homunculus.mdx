# Create Homunculus
<b>Cost:</b> Varies<br/>
<b>Type:</b> Alchemy<br/>
<b>School:</b> None<br/>
<b>Target:</b> None<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Event<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Ritual creates a creature of the type specified by the [Diagnose](/docs/skills/crafting-skills/scroll) to perform one(1) or more services for the Primary Caster. The power and number of services performed by the creature is determined by the number of Casters participating in the casting. For every three(3) Casters participating, rounded down, the power or number of services may be increased by one(1) to a maximum of five(5) in either category. For example six Casters could increase the power to two(2) and the number of services to two(2). Alternatively they could increase the power to three(3) and have only the one(1) service provided by the base effect. A Service is a single finite task. The maximum time a Service can take is one(1) hour. Examples of valid Services are; "Follow and protect me on this adventure.", "Go to the town of Exampleton and tell me how many soldiers are there.", "Aid me with the casting of this Ritual.". Examples of invalid Services are; "Guard this area forever.", "Stay in this Circle and help us cast all our Rituals.", "Explore all the camps of the foreign military and bring me a list of all commanders.".<br/>
