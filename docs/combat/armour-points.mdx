# Armour Points

Armour points represent a layer of protection
around a Character’s body. Most damage is
subtracted from Armour Points before it causes any
reduction in [Hit Points](/docs/combat/hit-points). Armour which has been
targeted by a Break &lt;Armour&gt;, Breach &lt;Type&gt;
Armour or Destroy effect is instantly reduced to
zero(0) Armour Points. Once armour is reduced to
zero(0) Armour Points any remaining damage is
subtracted from the Character’s Hit Points.

Each Player Character is created with the ability to
benefit from a maximum of twenty(20) Armour Points.
This is the Character’s Wear Total. Any armour in
excess of this value is ignored. A Character may
increase their Wear Total by purchasing the Skill
Wear Extra Armour.

If a Character has more than one type of armour
their maximum Wear Total applies to the combined
value of all armour the character has. The Character
chooses the order in which their armour is damaged
by attacks.

### Refitting Armour \{#refit}
A Character may recover all lost Armour Points by
performing a special sixty(60) second Count. The
Role-playing requirements for this Count are
described in the individual Armour Types described
below. A Character can perform the Counts to Refit
multiple Armour Types as part of the same count. The
Character may also perform this Counted Action
while walking and speaking but cannot do so if they
are struck by any effect or use any Skill.

### Arcane Armour
This Armour is composed of magical energy which
surrounds and protects the wearer. If the Character is
struck by a Dispel Magic effect, any remaining
Armour Points from Arcane Armour the character has
are lost and the Character is reduced to zero(0)
Arcane Armour points until they Refit. Arcane Armour
can be Refit by the Character spending a sixty(60)-
second count concentrating, by the Refit Arcane
Armour effect, or the Refit All Armour effect.

### Avoidance Armour
This Armour is a Character’s ability to roll with
attacks and otherwise avoid injury. If the Character is
struck by any effect which immobilises them such as;
Confine, Paralysis, or Web, any remaining Armour
Points from Avoidance Armour the character has are
lost and the Character is reduced to zero(0)
Avoidance Armour points until they Refit. Avoidance
Armour can be Refit by the Character spending a
sixty(60) second count stretching, by the Refit
Avoidance Armour effect, or the Refit All Armour
effect.

### Natural Armour
This Armour is a Character’s natural protective
armour that is part of their body. Natural Armour can
be Refit by the Character spending a sixty(60)
second count role-playing the repair of the armour in
any way they see fit, for example applying oils and
lotions, moulting off old plates and so on. It can also
be Refit by the Refit Natural Armour effect, or the
Refit All Armour effect. Natural Armour cannot be
Broken or Destroyed.

### Physical Armour
This armour is any which is manufactured and
created by the Smithing Skill. It is the most common
form of armour being made of toughened hide or
metal. It comes in a variety of forms and grants
Armour Points based on its material and the parts of
the body it covers. In order for armour to be
considered covering an area it must cover a total of
75% of the total area. The Armour Points granted by
coverage of each area can be found in the table
below. As an item, Physical Armour is a valid target
for the Break and Destroy effects. Both of these
effects instantly reduce the number of Armour Points
to zero(0) Armour Points. Physical Armour which is
Indestructible is not reduced to zero(0) Armour
Points. Physical Armour is Refit by spending a
sixty(60)-second count role-playing adjusting straps,
plates and armour sections in order to ensure proper
coverage. It can also be Refit by the Refit Physical
Armour effect, or the Refit All Armour effect.

### [Fortification Points](/docs/glossary/fortify)
Fortification Points are a form of temporary
protection which extends over a Character’s body
protecting both Armour and Hit Points from damage.
Whenever a Character is affected by an effect which
causes damage the damage is first subtracted from
any Fortification Points the Character may have. Any
damage which exceeds any Fortification Points the
Character may have causes damage to Armour
Points, or Hit Points, as appropriate.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, no abilities
which grant Fortification Points stack, instead any
Fortify effect simply overlaps with any current
Fortification Points the Character possesses. For
example, if a Character has three(3) Fortification
Points and if affected by a “Magic Fortify 5” effect the
Character will have five(5) Fortification Points. If a
Character has ten(10) Fortification Points and if
affected by a “Magic Fortify 5” effect the Character
still has ten(10) Fortification Points.
