# Berserk

This effect forces the Character into an
insane killing frenzy forcing them to attack everyone
around them to the best of their Player's ability using the
most effective means at their disposal.

<b>FAQ</b>\
Berserk is not a Compulsion, Curse, or Curse, and must be removed by either [Delivery Type](./delivery-types) or an effect taht removes all unwanted effects like [Cleanse](./cleanse)
