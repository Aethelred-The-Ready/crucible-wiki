# Standard Calls

These are the common responses to an effect, and what they indicate

### Got It
This can be represented by any acknowledgement of the hit. It indicates that the hit has been noticed and its full effect has been delivered

### Got It - Enhanced Effect
This indicates that the creature has taken some additional effect from the effect delivered

### Shield
The effect is negated, and has been shielded.

### Dodge
The effect is negated, and the target dodged it.

### Evasion
The effect is negated, and the target evaded it.

### Ward
The effect is negated, and has been warded.

Abilities that are Warded may be [Meditated](/docs/glossary/meditate) back

### Resist
The effect is negated, and has been 

Abilities that are Resisted may be [Meditated](/docs/glossary/meditate) back

### Reduced
The target is [Resistant](/docs/combat/defences#resistance) to this kind of effect, either through delivery type, the nature of the effect (binding, compulsion, curse), or the specific effect. They will take a reduced effect, typically turning 5 minute durations to 10 seconds, or halving the damage delivered.

### Reflect
The target takes no effect, and causes it to rebound at the attacker. An effect reflected twice is fully negated

### No Effect
The target is Immune to this kind of effect, either through delivery type, the nature of the effect (binding, compulsion, curse), or the specific effect

Abilities that the target is Immune to may be [Meditated](/docs/glossary/meditate) back
