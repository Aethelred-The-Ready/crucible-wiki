# Sword Mastery
<b>Requirements:</b> None<br/>
<b>Benefit:</b> The Character gains the ability to wield all Swords in combat. When wielding a Two-Handed Weapon, the Character may block attacks while holding the Weapon with one hand but cannot attack or use the Weapon to deliver any Skill until they have both hands on the Two-Handed Weapon. At the time this Skill is bought, the Character may "sell back" any Skills which are included in its benefit. For information of the requirements for the [Physical Representations](/docs/equipment) for this, and other Weapons, see Chapter 5: Equipment.<br/>
