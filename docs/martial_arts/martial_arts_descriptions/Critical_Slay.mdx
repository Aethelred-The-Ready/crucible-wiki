# Critical Slay
<b>School:</b> [Slayer](../slayer_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 9<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Strike<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Artistry allows the Caster to deliver a Weapon Attack causing fifty(50) points of damage appropriate to the Weapon Type of their weapon.<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "50 [Physical-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) &lt;Weapon Type&gt;"<br/>
