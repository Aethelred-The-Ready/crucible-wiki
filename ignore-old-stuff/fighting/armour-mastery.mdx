# Armour Mastery
import Skill from '../skill.tsx'

<b>Requirements:</b> None\
The Character's familiarity with [Physical Armour](../../glossary/armour#physical) grants them additional protection when wearing it. For every time this Skill is bought, the number of points of Physical Armour the Character gains from wearing [Physical Armour](../../equipment/armour) is increased by five(5). The maximum bonus from this Skill is equal to the [Armour Points](../../equipment/armour) of the Physical Representation of the suit of Physical Armour the Character is wearing, or their maximum [Wear Total](../../glossary/wear-total), whichever is lower.\
For example, a Character wearing a Physical Representation worth fifteen(15) points is able to benefit from no more than fifteen(15) additional Armour Points from this Skill. Even if the armour under the Enhance Armour effect.
