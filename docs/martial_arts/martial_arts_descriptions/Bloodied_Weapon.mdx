# Bloodied Weapon
<b>School:</b> [Slayer](../slayer_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 2<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Strike or Self<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> As an additional cost to cast this Artistry, the Caster must expend two(2) Hit Points. The Caster may either deliver a Weapon Strike causing five(5) points of [Piercing](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#piercing) [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Body](/docs/glossary/body) or Piercing Physical [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing).<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "5 [Piercing](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#piercing) [Physical](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical)-Strike [Body](/docs/glossary/body)" or "5 Piercing [Physical-Strike](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#physical) [Healing](/docs/glossary/healing)"<br/>
