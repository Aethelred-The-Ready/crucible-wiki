# Troll

Trolls are large, durable, and nearly unkillable. They excel and enjoy combat to the extent that many others see them brutes. This is not true however as many trolls have been shown to be capable of crafting,stealth and even the occasional mage has been seen.\
Trolls tend to live in the moment, often doing things solely for the sake of entertainment. Boredom to a troll is intolerable and many have started a fight just because they haven't done anything in a while.

### Costuming Requirements

Green to yellow-greenskin.\
Long nose or short horns.

### Costuming Suggestions

Trolls don't really have much of a fashion sense. As a result they often grab whatever is closest or most easily obtainable making them look even more chaotically coutured than even Sprytlings.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Heal Good: 
Whenever a Troll is affected by [Healing](../../glossary/healing) which would put them above their maximum Hit Point total, they gain a number of [Fortification Points](../../fortification) equal to the amount of over healing. The maximum number of Fortification Points the Character can gain from this ability is ten(10).
