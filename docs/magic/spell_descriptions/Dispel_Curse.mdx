# Dispel Curse
<b>School:</b> [Restoration](../restoration_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 8<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Instant<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell instantly removes all [Curse](/docs/glossary/curse) effects from the target. The following effects are [Curses](/docs/glossary/curse); [Cripple](/docs/glossary/curse#cripple), [Drain](/docs/glossary/curse#drain), [Fetter](/docs/glossary/curse#fetter), [Fumble](/docs/glossary/curse#fumble), [Frailty](/docs/glossary/curse#frailty), [Paralysis](/docs/glossary/curse#paralysis), [Weakness](/docs/glossary/curse#weakness).<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dusk to Dispel Curse."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Dispel](/docs/glossary/dispel) [Curse](/docs/glossary/curse)"<br/>
