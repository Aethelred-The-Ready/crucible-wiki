# Reset

At 06:00 AM and 06:00 PM all Skills are Reset. All Characters
regain the use of all Skills. Effects which state that
they end at Reset immediately end. All Effects which
have a duration of Charged, Concentration or
Sustained immediately end. Characters who are on
an Adventure during Reset are not affected until the
Adventure ends unless told otherwise by the Marshal. The
time between Resets is referred to as a “Reset
Period”.

Abilities which Reset at the end of the Day or are
usable once per Day do not Reset at 06:00 AM.
