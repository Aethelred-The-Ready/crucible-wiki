# Secutor

The Secutor are humanoids with traits not only limited to insects, but arachnids, crustaceans and just about anything found in nature with an exoskeleton. They are incredibly socially cooperative with little in terms of private property and privacy. As a result they all work together doing what their best at to make
things better for everyone.\
Secutor adventurers are often in support roles ranging from healers to front line guardians keeping not only themselves, but those around them alive. There do exist the occasional independent sort but these are relatively rare.

### Costuming Requirements

Arthropod traits.

### Costuming Suggestions

Secutor almost all wear armour. This armour tends to be plate and modelled after the exoskeleton of the invertebrate they resemble.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Living Armour:
Any [Physical Armour](../../glossary/armour#physical) worn by the Character also counts as [Natural Armour](../../glossary/armour#natural). This prevents their armour from being Broken or Destroyed and, finally, allows the portion of their Natural Armour which comes from their worn Physical Armour to be Refit by spells and effects which target either Physical or Natural Armour
