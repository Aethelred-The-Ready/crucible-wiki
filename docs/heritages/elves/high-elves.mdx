# High Elf

According to them, they are the most ancient and greatest heritage to live in this world. While it is true that they have the greatest cities and benefit from a history which has seen the rise and fall of civilizations and even predates the arrival of many of the other peoples there is some debate as to whether or not their claims are completely true. High Elves tend to be haughty and aloof, often self superior and overconfident in their abilities. Though their culture has earned this position it is not unusual for them to group the achievements of their people with their own, leading many to finding them entitled and insufferable

### Costuming Requirements

Pointed ears.\
At least three pieces of conspicuous metallic jewellery, usually gold or silver, in the form of earrings, tiaras, rings, bracelets, necklaces and similar accessories.

### Costuming Suggestions

High Elves are typically well groomed and dress formally.

### Heritage Abilities

#### Invested Weapon:
A High Elf may during any [Rest](../../glossary/rest), attune to a single Weapon in their possession.\
The Weapon acts as though it had a [Magic Aura Ritual effect](../../rituals/magic-aura). The [Invested](../../glossary/invested) Weapon allows the Character to deliver a [Killing Blow](../../glossary/killing-blow) that counts as Magic. The Weapon has this ability only when wielded by the Character who Invested it. Though this ability is usable at will, the Character can have no more than one Invested Weapon at a time.
