# Enhance Armour
<b>School:</b> [Protection](../protection_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 3<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Touch<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Day<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> This Spell increases the Armour Point total of the target suit of [Physical Armour](/docs/equipment/armour) by five(5) for its duration. This effect does not stack with any other effect which Enhances Physical Armour.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I grant you the protection of Enhanced Armour."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Enhance Physical Armour](/docs/glossary/enhance) 5"<br/>
