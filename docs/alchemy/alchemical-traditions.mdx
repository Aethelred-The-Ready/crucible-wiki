# Alchemical Traditions
Magical Traditions represent the manner in which a
Character learned, or practises magic. Effects which
renew or recharge Skills, such as Inspiration, may
never be used to increase the number of times a
Character can benefit from the effects of their
Tradition.

The effects of Magical Traditions apply only to
Spells the Character casts from memory.
### Bardic Magic [Tradition, Magical]
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 1 Spell Slot

<b>Benefit:</b> The Character, the Bard, may create a
specially prepared Stage which allows for the casting
of Spells on multiple targets. In order to use this
ability the Character must first prepare a Stage. A
valid Stage is either an enclosed Room, a structure
with a roof or a valid Circle representation.

 The process to prepare the Stage requires the
Character to perform a sixty(60)-second, or longer,
count where they role-play; setting up props, testing
the acoustic or otherwise preparing a stage for a
performance. Once the Stage has been prepared it
remains prepared until the Character leaves its
boundaries. Multiple Characters can share the same
Stage, but each must prepare it for their own use as
above.

 Whenever the Character casts a Spell while within
the boundaries of a Stage they prepared they may
perform a sixty(60)-count performance in the form of
music, oration, or other form of public performance.
Ideally this performance should be relevant to the
Spell being cast, this is not necessary. At the end of
this performance the Bard may cast a single Spell,
with a Range of Packet or Touch, and deliver it as
Touch against any number of targets who are within
the boundary of the Stage. No target may benefit
from a single Spell more than once. Once the Bard
goes ten(10)-seconds without affecting a new target,
the Spell ends.
### Bound Caster [Tradition, Magical]
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 1 Spell Slot

<b>Benefit:</b> The Character is unable to throw packets to
deliver Spells and is restricted to delivering all Spells
as Touch. This restriction applies to Spells the
Character casts from Scrolls, Wands, and Runes.
The Character can throw Spells Imbued into Ritual
Items normally. Spells which grant the Character the
ability to deliver a packet attack, such as Elemental
Fury and Force Storm, cannot be cast by the
Character. Though this ability does not prevent the
target from casting other harmful spells, the fact that
they must be delivered as Touch means that they are
essentially useless in attacking. Any time the
Character delivers a Spell by Touch they may cast a
second copy of the Spell, which must be delivered
within ten(10) seconds of the first. The call for this
effect is “Echo &lt;Delivery Verbal&gt;”. Unlike the Echo
Spellshape Skill, the additional casting granted by
this ability does not include any Spellshapes which
were applied to the original Spell.
### Dawn Magic [Tradition, Magical]
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 1 Spell Slot

<b>Benefit:</b> Once per Reset, the Character may Rest in
order to recover a single expended Spell Slot for
each level they can cast.
### Dusk Magic [Tradition, Magical]
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 1 Spell Slot

<b>Benefit:</b> Once per Reset, the Character may Rest in
order to recover three(3) expended Spell Slots of any
level(s) they can cast. Additionally, once per Reset,
the Character may grant themselves the effect of a
“Magic Clarity” granting them the benefits of
Meditating instantly.
### Incanter's Repertoire [Tradition, Magical]
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 1 Spell Slot

<b>Benefit:</b> A number of times per Reset, equal to the
level of the highest level Spell Slot the Character can
cast, the Character may Rest in order to regain the
expended Spell Slot for any Spell they cast from
Memory and delivered with the Incantation. For
example, a Character with a 4th Level Spell Slot can
use this ability four(4) times per Reset while a
Character with 9th Level Spell Slots can use it nine(9)
times per Reset.

 Additionally, once per Rest, any Spell the
Character has cast using its full Incantation, which
could be regained by Meditation, may be recovered
by the Character on a ten(10) second count. This
ability may be recharged by Resting.
### Living Magic [Tradition, Magical]
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 1 Spell Slot

<b>Benefit:</b> Whenever the Character casts a Spell, from
memory, they may also deliver, by Touch, an amount
of Magic Healing equal to the level of the Spell cast.
This healing must be used within ten(10) seconds of
casting the Spell.

 Alternatively, multiple Spells can be cast in
sequence. So long as no more than ten(10) seconds
go by between castings the total levels cast can be
combined into a single Magic Healing effect. For
example a Character could cast a number of
protectives on their target whose combined levels
equal seventeen(17), they could then deliver “17
Magic Healing” by Touch to any target within ten(10)
seconds of the last Spell being cast.

 Spells which grant the Character the ability to cast
multiple effects similar to spells, such as Healing
Touch, only trigger this ability upon the expenditure of
the originating Spell Slot.
### Metaphysical Magic [Tradition, Magical]
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 1 Spell Slot

<b>Benefit:</b> The Character may spend a sixty(60)-
second count to attune to an element. Upon doing so,
the Character may choose one of the following
elemental damage types; Chaos, Destruction,
Dream, Essence, Fire, Ice, Light, Lightning, Order,
Reason, Shadow, or Stone. Whenever the Character
casts a Spell which gives them the option to choose
from the following types; Fire, Ice, Lightning, or
Stone, they may instead choose the elemental
Damage Type to which they have attuned. The
Character can attune to a new element but must Rest
before changing elements. Spells which list only a
single damage type, such as Fireball, cannot be used
in conjunction with this ability.
### Prepared Magic [Tradition, Magical]
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 1 Spell Slot

<b>Benefit:</b> After each Reset, the Character may choose
one(1) Spell of each level, for which they have Spell
Slots, adding them to a list of specially prepared
Spells. The Character must have access to the
School of Magic of any Spells which they prepare.
The Character may cast each Spell on their list of
prepared Spells once without expending a Spell Slot.
### Sorcery [Tradition, Magical]
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 1 Spell Slot

<b>Benefit:</b> When casting a Spell from memory the
Character can choose to expend a Spell Slot which is
one(1) or more levels higher than that of the Spell
they wish to cast. Casting a Spell in this way
consumes only the higher level Spell Slot.

 If the Character has this Tradition and casts the
Cantrip Spell Conversion, they instead regain the
ability to cast one(1) of their expended Cantrips as
though they had Rested, in place of this Cantrip's
normal effects.
### Specialised Magic [Tradition, Magical]
<b>Requirements:</b> Level 1 Spell Slot

<b>Benefit:</b> The Character chooses a single School of
Magic. The Character gain one additional Spell Slot
of each level they can cast. These additional Spell
Slots must be used to cast Spells from the chosen
School of Magic. These additional Spell Slots do not
count towards the requirements for any Skill.
