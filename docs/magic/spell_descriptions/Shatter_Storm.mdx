# Shatter Storm
<b>School:</b> [Transmutation](../transmutation_list)<br/>
<b>Level:</b> 7<br/>
<b>Range:</b> Self and Packet<br/>
<b>Duration:</b> Concentration<br/>
<b>Effect:</b> As part of casting this Spell the Caster must plant both feet and choose a single item. This Spell allows the Caster to deliver, by packet, a "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Break](/docs/glossary/break) &lt;Item&gt;" effect at will. The Caster may continue to throw packets for this effect until they go ten(10) seconds without delivering this effect, move from their planted location, or use any Skill. A Broken item cannot be used until it is [Repair](/docs/glossary/repair)ed. Certain creatures, such as Constructs, may be damaged by this Spell. If the target has multiple items in their possession which match the description of the item designated by the Caster it is the target who chooses which of their items are affected. The target cannot choose an item which is [Indestructible](/docs/glossary/indestructible) unless it is the only item they have which matches that description.<br/>
<b>Incantation:</b> "I call upon the Dusk to create a Shatter Storm."<br/>
<b>[Delivery Verbal](/docs/glossary/delivery-verbal):</b> "[Magic](/docs/glossary/delivery-types#magic) [Break](/docs/glossary/break) &lt;Item&gt; … Magic Break &lt;Item&gt;"<br/>
